## Investigation brief

- **Thesis under test.** A necessary simple ground can explain contingent things without making
  them necessary. Downstream, this is the main cost of the necessary being route.
- **Strongest case for it.** Necessity of the bearer is not necessity of what it does; an effect
  can be contingent while what the bearer is does not vary.
- **Strongest rivals.** The collapse objection; necessitarian emanation; a brute contingent stop.
- **Reused.** #462 `OutputProfile`, `StableActOutput`, `SameActEverywhere`; #750 `ReasonClosed` and
  rivals; #742 `Marks`, `NetSelects`, `acting`; #755 `Arena`; `IsImmutable`; the IX.6 reading.
- **Decisive fork.** Which member goes, and what explanation is left once it has gone.

## Findings

1. **The core needs less than the objection supposes.** Act necessity, strict efficacy and a
   contingent effect are inconsistent in modal K, with no frame condition and no premise about the
   bearer. A deletion model exists per member.
2. **The core needs more than the objection states.** Both box claims must be read of one token:
   `twoTokens` has one necessary token, another efficacious token and a contingent effect. A
   description denoting something wherever an effect exists does not give a rigid token.
3. **Bearer to act is a condition.** The bearer's necessity gives act necessity only with
   `BeingCarriesAct`. The identity of act and being would give that condition; that simplicity
   gives the identity is not derived here.
4. **What the route itself exposes.** The IX.6 reading and `IsImmutable`, with `ModalCapacity`,
   exclude any respect true at an alternative and false here. A unique actual decree treated as a
   respect then collapses the effect. The fork is: some world without the effect has no decree, or
   `ModalCapacity` fails. Since `oneRespect` has no decree as a respect, there are three exits.
5. **Explanatory cost: the gap moves and does not close.** Every reason-closed founded account of a
   contingent fact has a reason that does not strictly suffice, is not contrast-relevant, and has a
   noncontingent reason for a contingent member.

| Branch | Model | Keeps | Gives up |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nonnecessitating necessary act | `ground`, `inefficaciousAct` | contingency; foundation | strict sufficiency and contrast at the effect |
| Optional act or extrinsic effect | `relocated`, `optionalAct` | strict sufficiency and contrast at the effect; foundation | the same one step back, at the act |
| Brute act | `brute` | contingency; foundation | a reason for the terminus |
| Regress | `chain` | a reason for each member | foundation |
| Necessitarian | `necessitarian`, `necessitarianAct` | every reason necessitates | all contingency here |

Selection does not close the gap: #742's `acting` has marks of choice with a contingent target on
two necessary reasons and no net selection either way, and a one-member base has no such marks. An
enabling condition does not close it: a true condition is itself contingent, and the effect itself
qualifies, so a condition as such explains nothing.

So a closed founded account of contingent creation must deny that each reason necessitates and
that reasons exclude every rival. That price is a standard and not a contradiction: nothing here
shows that the standard is owed. The same price falls on every rival that keeps a
contingent fact, a founded account and closure: the result does not single out classical theism.

## Semantic grounding

**`Necessitates`, `StrictEfficacy`.** Meaning: wherever the first holds, so does the second.
Example: being a triangle and having three sides. Counterexample: a good reason to act and the act.
Distinct from admission as a reason, causation and production. Reflexive and transitive at a
fixed world; not asymmetric, not well-founded. Abstraction: none claimed from experience; it
is a modal test. Rival: a nonmodal account of sufficiency; **OPEN**.

**`ModalCapacity`.** Meaning: what a thing is at an alternative it can be here. Example: wood that
could have had a Hermes cut from it. Counterexample candidate: a merely extrinsic alternative, such
as a statue that could have been admired, with no power to be so. Distinct from the IX.6 `CanBe`,
which it bridges. No transitivity or well-foundedness is claimed. Abstraction from cases of intrinsic
change to divine alternatives is **OPEN** and is what the extrinsic reply denies. Rival: the respects
in question are mere Cambridge properties.

**Decree as respect.** Meaning: a way of being in act, one per world. Examples or counterexamples
in the divine case are unavailable; the ordinary analogue is a person's decision. Distinct from
the effect and from the extrinsic relation of creating. Structural properties are the listed
exclusivity and supply clauses, which are deletable. Generalization from persons is **OPEN**.

The node is OPEN and has no registered concluding argument in the inspected
dossier. Its refutations of necessitarian and collapse rivals assume it; they

Four kinds of evidence must remain distinct. A fixture validates logical
compatibility of its stated relations. Conceiving a description may support
initial intelligibility but does not establish real compatibility of its causal
and essential conditions. Epistemic uncertainty permits our not knowing which
modal account is true; it does not give a world where the actual individual

inference. A changing state or an effect-token replacing the substantial
individual would also change the witness. No new countermodel is needed.

On identical actual-source assumptions, necessitarian and contingent-production
proposals can each explain the supplied actual causal facts; those facts alone
do not say whether their production must recur in all alternatives. The retained
necessitarian proposal supplies a mechanism for recurrence: effects follow from
the source's nature with necessary complete conditions. If that account really
warrants same-act performance and strict efficacy for x, #555 excludes x's
contingency. The result is an exact conditional conflict, not an independently
established conflict with reality in this application. Necessitarianism owes
its cross-world sufficiency/identity claims; it does not earn them from an
unchanging equation or merely denying our knowledge of alternatives.

The contingent-production proposal preserves the actual source and production
while allowing nonperformance of the selected effect-directed act, or a
nonnecessitating relation to the effect. Its positive rationale distinguishes
what the source is from whether/how it produces this other; a stable bearer
need not have a fixed complete output. The existing optional-act/extrinsic-
effect and nonnecessitating branches show the conditional logical forms of
this reply. They do not establish actual free agency or real alternative
creation. If the proposal also demands a founded reason-closed explanation,
it incurs the existing nonnecessitating/contrast-standard cost at some link.
That is an explanatory commitment requiring assessment, not a contradiction or
an objection solely because unusual. The necessitarian proposal avoids that
particular nonnecessitating link by denying contingency; it then conflicts with
a warranted contingent effect if one is supplied. Neither proposal is defeated
merely by the other's preferred explanatory standard.

source. A natural source can propose necessary nature with nonnecessitating

**Scope of the conclusion.** The mirror case presses (i) necessitarian accounts,
personal and impersonal alike (full collapse by clauses 1–4; concrete
necessitarianism only together with a chiral individual, clause 8), and (ii) any account that claims full
contrastive explanation from the intrinsic profile of an invariant source. It
does not favour personal over impersonal accounts: the non-necessitarian rows
pay identical prices, and the necessitarian rows pay identical prices.

**What the mirror case adds beyond the general tie case.**

- It closes the qualitative tie-break escape, for rules invariant under the
  laws' symmetry. In a value tie between qualitatively different arrangements a
  necessitarian can appeal to a non-value qualitative rule (a physical
  tie-break, least action, an end-realization ordering). CPT twins agree on
  every feature invariant under the laws' symmetry, so by clause 5 no such rule
  selects between them. P-twins do not: the weak interaction distinguishes them.
- It motivates parity in purity for lane P's mechanism without any value tie, if
  purity is mirror-invariant: wherever the world has a mirror twin, a real
  selection token then has an equally pure alternative.
- It is weaker than the tie case in one respect: relationalism about
  orientation (option 2) dissolves it, while it does not dissolve a tie between
  qualitatively different arrangements.
- Against non-necessitarian accounts it adds nothing beyond the tie case.

### C. Counter-intuitiveness as evidence

- **The intuitions.** (a) A globally mirrored world seems as possible as this
  one. For the plain reflection P this intuition meets physics: the actual laws
  are not reflection-invariant, so the P-twin is nomically impossible and, on
  dispositional essentialism, excluded by creaturely natures. The intuition
  survives for the CPT-twin (a right hand of antimatter, history reversed),
  which is less immediately imaginable. (b) A rule that fixes global handedness
  seems arbitrary.
- **The kind of warrant.** Modal intuition, prima facie. It is the same kind of
  warrant the corpus already accepts as a support option for particular
  contingency judgments (above, "Contingency warrant"). It is defeasible.
- **The necessitarian's strongest reply.** Identity of indiscernibles with
  relationalism about orientation (Leibniz's translation argument `7dd2af35`,
  navigation for reflection): the "two" worlds are two descriptions of one, so
  (a) confuses distinct representations with distinct possibilities. A second
  reply: modal intuitions about global symmetries are unreliable, because the
  imagined difference makes no difference to anything within the world.
  Kant's incongruent counterparts (navigation only) point the other way for a
  single hand; this note does not adjudicate them.
- **Recorded judgment.** The intuition supports `HandednessReversedAt` only
  against a non-relationalist background, and preservation only for the CPT
  reading. Under essence grounding necessity, accepting preservation *is*
  accepting the mirror world's possibility (clause 9), so the argument adds the
  symmetry consideration to a direct modal intuition; it does not replace one.
  The necessitarian's reply costs relationalism about global orientation (σ w =
  w for this σ, clause 4); clause 1 adds no further cost to a necessitarian who
  already holds essence-grounded possibility. Prima facie evidence of low weight
  against necessitarianism that also holds essence-grounded possibility; neutral
  between personal and impersonal non-necessitarian accounts. The earlier
  attempt `05c7e8d0` rejected "PSR alone → relationalism"; nothing here uses the
  PSR alone.
