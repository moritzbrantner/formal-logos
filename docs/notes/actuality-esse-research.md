## 1. Question and result

Does a genuinely actual perfection of a bearer entail that the same bearer has being in esse? Which weaker commitments actually suffice for the existing God-existence and attribute producers?

Select this because the previous route analysis identified it as indispensable to all 25 tested modal-free root derivations, and its meaning directly constrains both causal perfection and the proposed esse-only goodness route. It is distinct from causal superiority, universal causal transfer, intrinsic possession, composition, and the value bridge investigated in #1917. No new attribution to a source or formal status is asserted.

The provisional judgment favors direct existential import where `InAct x r` is genuine real exemplification by the same bearer `x` at the selected world. Its warrant is the bearer’s role in the actualization. Applicability to every input of the downstream `InAct` dictionaries remains open.

Do not solve this by declaring `InAct x esse` definitionally equal to `∃ r, InAct x r`. That would encode the contested direction. Do not identify every actual or necessary mathematical truth with an actual causal bearer of the perfection. Retain the original premise and its direct-assumption option.

## 3. Source and rival reassessment

### Aquinas

ST I q.4 a.1 ad 3 answers the objection that esse seems imperfect and receptive of all modification. The reply makes esse the act by which other determinations, including form, are actual. Its final sentence draws a distinction between the received formal principle, as in the esse of a human or a horse, and the bearer that exists. The corpus's consequence about actuality and esse is already read here (`6311aef8`) and in q.3 a.4 co. (`5fd6b154`). The selected-world adapter in §4 is an analyst proposal. [Public Dominican translation, reply 3](https://www.newadvent.org/summa/1004.htm) · [Q.4 claim](../../corpus/claims/aquinas-st-i-q4-a1-ad3-actuality-as-being.json) · [Q.3 claim](../../corpus/claims/aquinas-st-i-q3-a4-esse-actuality-of-every-form.json)

### Meinong, §3–4

In *Über Gegenstandstheorie* (1904), §3 p. 8 presents *Sosein* as what characterizes the object itself, including the golden mountain and the impossible round square. Meinong affirms the independence thesis. He separately qualifies the warrant for a posteriori judgments about *Sosein*, which depends on knowledge of being. In §4 pp. 11–13, he distinguishes *Existenz* from *Bestand* and rejects an unrestricted inference from the subsistence of an objective to the being of its object. An example is the objective of an object's nonbeing. In his account the object's own *Sosein* is available to inquiry independently of an existential judgment. [German transcription from the 1913 collection](https://ozekik.github.io/meinong/1904/)

Two mappings are open. The rival needs a bridge from objective characterization (`Sosein x r`) to the real same-bearer `InAct x r` consumed by the causal proofs. The formal rival requires a mapping from the relevant denial of being, given the *Existenz*/*Bestand* distinction, to `¬ InAct x esse`. A mapping to `¬ E(x)` supplies that denial given `ActOfEsseSufficesForExistenceAt` (`546975ef`) on the same frame. The public text was consulted during independent review. ## 4. Positive metaphysical warrant and exact calibration

An imagined golden mountain, an actually golden object and an existing drawing of a golden mountain are not the same bearer of the same property. The drawing can really have pigment; its subject can be characterized as gold without an actual mountain bearing gold. The difference between a realized property and a describable nature must remain even on a generous ontology of intentional objects.

The reason is **subject-involving actualization**: where the actuality is intrinsically the actuality of this bearer, not of an image or of a proposition about it, the bearer is part of what is actualized. A nonexistent object cannot be that actual bearer unless one rejects precisely this subject-involving reading of intrinsic realization. A process or trope ontology may deny a separate enduring substance; in that case the real event or trope can be the relevant bearer, and the conclusion concerns it rather than an inferred substance. This does not prove hylomorphism, the PSR, a sustaining cause, or modal necessity. For a realist about abstract objects, the same question is whether their determinations are actual in the intended sense; if so the application must count their appropriate being, or supply an explicit restriction. Do not exclude abstracta by stipulation in a generic carrier.

Reuse the open analyst bridge `ConceptualExistenceBoundary.ActualityExists` (`0884890e`) with `actual x := ∃ r, InAct x r` and `Exists x := m.ExistsAt w x`. Unpacking the existential gives the proposed calibration `∀ x r, InAct x r → m.ExistsAt w x`. Its positive warrant applies where the supplied relation records genuine real exemplification by that same individual at `w`. How abstracta fit the selected existence predicate and whether every virtual or power reading meets this calibration remain open. The intended power profile can supply existence via `IntendedProfile.powerExists` once an explicit premise takes the relevant `InAct x r` to that profile's `hasCausalPower w x` on the same carrier and frame. ## 9. Does a particular actuality share in its bearer's received being?

**Provisional answer.** Yes for an actuality whose realization consists at least partly in this very subject actually being or doing something, in the following limited sense. If the subject owes its present existence to another, the occurrence draws on that received actuality insofar as it is this subject's occurrence. The subject can still account for what it does by its own power, and another agent can account for why it does it now. Calling the occurrence a recipient of a further, separately bestowed act of existence requires another argument. So does attributing productive causation of this exercise to the giver. The subject-involving reading has the strongest warrant in ordinary intrinsic qualities and exercises. Universal application to every attribution in the grades frame remains open.

### What varies, and what receives

Fix an actually existing subject, one occasion and one respect. Asking both whether the subject exists and whether it is exercising a power on this occasion permits a real difference in the answers. A violin string can exist under tension and be silent, then vibrate while remaining the same string. Its tension is real before it vibrates. Its particular vibration is real while it occurs. Damping the vibration can change what drives the soundboard while leaving the string in place. These distinctions give us reason to recognize a real determination beyond the bare fact that the string exists. The contrast does not count the determination as another substance, establish a metaphysical cause of the string, or prove that every feature is detachable. An immanent operation has the analogous contrast between being able to attend and actually attending, even when no outward effect is produced.

| Candidate | What makes this an actuality of the bearer? | Limit of the inference |
| --- | --- | --- |
| An intrinsic quality, such as the string's tension | Its current physical condition enters what it can presently do. Merely specifying a possible tension leaves that condition unsettled. | The current quality may persist without the action that first produced it. Its existential dependence concerns the present subject. |
| The operation or exercise, such as this vibration | The string itself undergoes the motion. A recording or another string vibrating at the same pitch can reproduce the sound without preserving this occurrence. | The subject's existence and capacity can persist through silence. They do not suffice for this exercise or select its frequency. |
| A real relation, such as two strings being at the same pitch on this occasion | The pitch determinations of both relata and their equality suffice on the founded reading. Removing either instance destroys that very comparison. | An alteration of the other string can establish equality without a new intrinsic quality in the first. An independently productive influence from the first string's giver on the second does not follow. An irreducible tie would require an account of what realizes that tie. |
| A merely extrinsic denomination, such as being thought about | The underlying thought is an operation of its thinker. A description alone need not express an intrinsic operation in its object. | First locate the real episode and its participants. Predicating something of a subject licenses no new received item in that subject. |

The adopted esse criterion applies to an individual existing in re. In `7067553d`, from `InAct x r` one obtains `InAct x esse`, for the **same x**. In `791fa216` one obtains `ModeOfBeing x r`. Neither step changes the subject to an episode, identifies its giver, or settles what makes a mode obtain. A mode can be represented by an indexed predication throughout. Alternatively, if a particular quality is independently identified as a real individual, the adopted criterion can apply to that quality under its own interpretation of existence. This still supplies no efficient-cause edge. The corpus's subject-focal reading supplies a populated one-world interpretation with an existing nonsubstantial dependent in a distinct substantial subject. It separates inherence from productive causation. The q.75 distinction also separates non-inherence from completeness in a species. Thus existential derivation can pertain to a way a thing is, while substantial reception pertains to the existing subject itself. The relevant commonality is derivation in actuality. The recipients and orders of explanation differ. ### A local principle and two tests of its warrant

**Bearer contribution.** Count the actual subject among the constitutive contributors to *this* realization wherever what realizes it includes the subject itself in operation or determination. Count its actually sustained existence as a mediated existential contribution to that extent. This requires neither a separate accident-substance nor the claim that every cause of a condition causes everything that requires it. Its weakest content is the inclusion of the bearer in the actual realization. To rule out *every* independent realization, one additionally needs an identity claim across alternatives: no adequate realization of this very particular can bypass the actual subject. To establish that one giver is indispensable *through loss of the bearer's existence*, one further needs its supply of the bearer to have no replacement in those alternatives. A replaceable giver of esse could still be indispensable in another role, for example as the only supplier of the selector.

**Substitution test.** Hold the realized pattern, its type, and its effects fixed while changing the performer. A second string can supply a vibration with the same pitch, amplitude and impact on the soundboard. It cannot thereby make the first string vibrate. Specifying the pattern plus the first string's concurrent existence still leaves open which string is moving. What answers that question is actual motion *of that string*. Substitution identifies whose vibration is at issue. For an explanatory reason at the level of the instrument, keep bow and pitch instruction fixed and vary the tension or damping of this very string: its response and the transmission of its motion to the soundboard change. A mere identifying label does no such work. This favors including the string in its actual physical condition among the contributors at this level. It does not show the bare existence of the string produces the motion, or give the macroscopic subject priority over its moving material. Compare an interlock required for the instrument to be played. It can be replaced by a different interlock while the same string continues its motion. Its being indispensable under a particular arrangement does not give it the string's constitutive role. This discriminates two putative necessary conditions without first assuming universal transfer. It supports dependence for the bearer-involving particular. It leaves open whether the underlying motion is more fundamental than the enduring string.

**Two explanatory contrasts.** Suppose a giver sustains the string equally at rest and in motion. Holding its existential contribution fixed while varying the bow's contact discriminates silence from vibration. Holding the pattern and bowing instructions fixed while removing the actual string, with no substitute for its role in *this* exercise, leaves a specification and no performance by that string. A secondary cause's success on the first contrast gives no reason to delete the subject on the second. Conversely, success on the second does not show the giver applies the bow or productively contributes to the exercise as an exercise. The positive concurrence proposal concerns the actuality of the secondary efficacy itself: the agent properly acts, and its acting, as well as its power to act, is supplied in the execution. Its added warrant would be evidence that the giver contributes to the execution in that capacity. Reclassifying a conserved power as an applied power assumes that point. A cause of past origination alone does not meet the premise about present reception. There is a cost to **complete** independence when the rival retains the bearer-constituted reading. ## 10. Can a producer withhold being from the very actuality it produces?

**Judgment.** Let **P** mean that some agent genuinely produces a particular new actuality while giving no being to that very effect. Three readings have different verdicts. **P-onset** denies the producer even a mediated contribution to that particular's actual coming-to-be. I favor **¬P-onset** on the ordinary productive reading. **P-direct** denies that the producer is a direct source of the generated individual's being as such. I favor **P-direct** for substantial generation. In accidental change the subject acquires a real determination; requiring a second subsistent recipient for the accident's own esse misidentifies the terminus. **P-no-mediated-esse** says a producer makes no contribution, even instrumentally, to the particular's esse as such at onset, in a case where that predicate has an eligible subject. The present evidence leaves that claim open. Showing that the generator neither creates nor continuously sustains the effect does not establish this last exclusion.

### Fix the effect before asking who gives it being

Consider one agent, an acted-on subject, and the actual completion of one operation. “New” excludes uncovering an already actual item and assigning a fresh name. It need not mean new underlying matter. Assess the agent through its exercised power, patient, and actual interaction. Having a standing power or furnishing a condition is insufficient. The question is whether the agent which does this productive work could leave *all the work of making the particular effect actual* elsewhere. Keep three contrasts apart: this effect coming to be versus remaining unrealized; this determination versus a different determination of a transaction that occurs either way; this whole actual transaction versus no existential basis for it. The last contrast concerns origin and sustenance at a different level even when all three questions refer to one effect at the same instant.

| Ontology of the effect | What begins, and what does the producer give? | What remains to be explained? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A numerically distinct dependent individual | In substantial generation, the materials can preexist and the offspring can be genuinely new. The generative process accounts for there being this organized living individual at its onset. If an accident is instead individuated as a particular dependent entity, the same argument concerns the onset of that particular, provided the action actually originates it. | Distinctness does not imply subsistence. Giving a real individual its beginning need not supply the act by which it exists throughout, or explain the being of the producer, patient and process. Identifying an originating edge with a strict cause of esse at onset already needs a bridge. |
| A real determination without separate subsistence | What comes to obtain is that this subject is now thus. Producing an indentation makes the wax actually indented here. The wax's existing beforehand does not already make this true. Its deformation under load supplies the realization; a distinct entity called “the indentation” is dispensable. | Asking who gives a second individual its substantial esse misidentifies the effect. Why the wax exists, why it deformed now, and why the impression subsequently holds are intelligibly different questions about this same case. |

The warrant for P-direct in the first row concerns the generated individual itself. On Aquinas's univocal account the parent makes the received form actual in this matter. At the first instant at which the offspring exists, it is already the offspring's own constituting form and matter by which it is this living individual. The parent explains their achieved union by its generative activity. The positive basis on the offspring's side explains why that union need not be an ongoing act of the parent. This accounts for the difference between the two roles without postponing it until the parent is gone. Whether the very action by which the parent achieves the union is also an instrument of the first giver of the offspring's esse is a further question about *that same action*. Later persistence cannot decide it. In the second row the proper direct-esse question can still be asked of the wax; asking it of a separately subsisting indentation changes the ontology.

Neither row infers metaphysical status from admission of an event index. The adopted esse criterion and `7067553d` give existential import to the actual bearer on their selected readings; `791fa216` classifies the actual respect as a mode. Section 9's `ca58266a` concerns loss of a derivation when the bearer's esse supply is cut. `9e3109ea` and `9be98ad0` give sufficient immediate and mediated contributor conditions. None says who **exercised** a power to produce this effect. Its enabler control keeps the secondary producer dependent on the universal source. It supplies no case of production wholly independent of that source.

### What makes the effect actual?

Take a press with a patterned die, a drive and a wax blank. The pattern exists before the stroke. As the drive loads the die, the relief transmits pressure at these points on this yielding wax. The yielding is the production of the impression in this subject. With the die held clear, the same design and an operating drive can leave the blank untouched. A feature of the die can reproduce its local detail or defect in successive impressions. After unloading, the impression can persist by the condition acquired by the wax. These comparisons identify the relevant work more closely than asking merely whether disabling the whole press prevents success. A guard interlock can also pass that last test. The contact geometry explains how the work is delivered to the very portions of the patient which acquire the profile. This is an illustration of positive causal warrant, not an experimental refutation of a metaphysics.

**The strongest division-of-labour objection** grants that the agent determines which effect there will be, while another source supplies all the actuality. This has two substantially different implementations. A controller can choose a die before the stroke and be disconnected while the stored setting, drive and die execute it. This is a positive explanation of how selection can be real, causal and over before the effect occurs. The controller need not sustain any force during the stroke. Its executed setting can still contribute historically to which *actual impression* results. Fix the effect criterion. If the effect is the pressing episode identified by this stroke on this blank, a switch from A to B can leave the episode fixed on that criterion; the controller chooses its lettering and need not account for the stroke occurring at all. If the effect is the actual realization of A in this wax, the retained A-setting enters the executed route by which that determination obtains. This contrasts two explananda within the actual episode without assuming that every difference in lettering makes a numerically different event across possibilities. Crediting the controller with producing the whole impression depends on which effect is at issue and whether mediated selection counts as its production. The drive and loaded die are a harder case for the objection. Their productive operation changes this wax; describing it as determination of an abstract pattern leaves the actual deformation out. Conversely, a source that supplies the energy of the drive need not itself select the lettering. None of these roles alone explains why there is an actual world or even an existing press.

A metaphysical version gives the first cause the whole effect insofar as it exists and gives the secondary agent the determination of that **same** effect. It has a coherent explanatory proposal in the following limited sense: it specifies a source of actuality and an operating cause of the effect's determinate realization, and explains how both enter one production. It can deny the secondary agent a second existential donation. If its shaping acts on an actual patient and makes this determination obtain, it already grants the secondary contribution affirmed by ¬P on the onset reading. A stronger occasionalist can instead explain the outcome by one efficacious agent responding systematically to the setting and contact. This accounts for particular actuality, and it can preserve the ordinary observed correlations. Its price here is precise: if the secondary power effects nothing in the patient even through its exercise, the attribution of genuine secondary production must be withdrawn or given a regularity-based interpretation. Ordinary manipulation by itself does not settle that dispute. The explanatory advantage of secondary efficacy is that the same independently characterizable contact which transmits the load accounts for the local profile. A rival making the entire efficacy reside elsewhere owes an account of this systematic fit; its denial of intrinsic secondary efficacy is no counterexample *granting* intrinsic secondary production.

This leaves a defensible primitive stopping point. An actual exercise of a power in a suitably disposed subject can explain a new realization. The independent marks here are transmission, localization, failure of a specified exercise with design and available power retained, and subsequent retention without the stroke. They positively distinguish an operative contribution from a catalogue of prior capacities. They do not make efficient causation reducible to a counterfactual, require that the producer be sufficient alone, or imply a further event that actualizes the actualization. The stronger existential question cannot be won simply by repeating “but is the exercise actual?” Conversely, merely naming an independent existential donor without saying how the actual power and patient yield the particular effect leaves the production question unanswered. No additional brute actuality follows from dividing these explanatory tasks. A power account may take exercise as primitive; a primary-cause account may take the first agent's efficacy as primitive. Either must still explain which positive outcome its proposed basis bears on.

Fix the fire, the water and this heating of the water. Grant that the fire genuinely heats and that the same giver sustains both bearers in existence. Two questions remain. There is also a philosophical account on which the giver acts *with* the fire on the heating itself. This third account matters to the God-existence argument because its transfer premise requires causal responsibility for an actual respect. It specifies no mechanism of application. ### The argument from being as proper effect

Aquinas gives a reason why application might extend beyond cases involving tools. Natural agents produce particular perfections, and all production reaches something actual. In an ordered work, the achievement common to the subordinate operations can be the proper effect of the principal agent. *SCG* III.66.3–7 argues from this order, the priority of being among effects, and God's being by essence. III.67.1–4 extends the argument to substantial and accidental being, distinguishes giving a power from applying it, and invokes an unmoved first mover for the latter. III.70.5–7 explains how the same effect can come wholly from each agent once the lower power derives its efficacy for **this effect** from the higher. The commonality of being, ordered agency and the identity of the first source do different work in this reasoning. [*SCG* III.66–70, Latin witnesses](../../corpus/passages/aquinas-scg-iii66-70-lat.json)

*De potentia* q.3 a.7, body and replies 1, 6–7, presses the rival directly. Sharpness can remain in an idle axe. The actual efficacy of the art in the cut has a different mode of being. Aquinas grants a natural thing its own abiding power, and argues that possessing the very power of the first cause to give being as its own would make it a universal principle of being. He describes what God brings about in it for actual operation as an *intentio* with incomplete being. This is a claim about the formal object and derivation of a power. Inferring a universal power from the mere fact that a fire effects one real heating would miss that argument. [*De potentia* q.3 a.7, especially reply 7](../../corpus/passages/aquinas-de-potentia-q3a5-lat.json)

There is positive warrant here. An agent can exercise genuinely its own power and owe the efficacy of that very exercise to a higher agent. The engaged press in the earlier investigation shows how one source explains the stroke *through* the ram, while the tool accounts for a determinate profile. The ordered-work argument also explains why locating a complete local process in the patient leaves room for a higher cause. Its contested step is identifying the source of all bearers' existence as principal in **this order of production**. A commander's victory exemplifies this structure because the command actually organizes the participating units. A fire's power has restricted scope, and its effect is real. Both the conservational and immediate-concurrence accounts grant those facts. Molina can grant that the heating itself proceeds from both agents. Application adds a direction of dependence. The fire's efficacy as agent in this heating derives from the giver putting that efficacy to work. The conservationist and Molina give different reasons for declining that further claim. The earlier arguments for receipt of a bearer's esse identify a real dependence. Receipt alone does not supply this further ordering. ### Can effect-side concurrence do the explanatory work?

Molina takes a natural power, such as heat acting on a suitably placed patient, to differ from an axe that needs someone to move it. He locates God's immediate contribution with the secondary cause in the operation they produce and in its terminus. He allows a conciliatory reading of Thomas. The two accounts compared here are distinguished by their commitments about application. They need not exhaust the readings of either author. [*Concordia* II, disp. 26, §§2–5](../../corpus/passages/db477c06-7c60-4a05-be0e-6953fdfbe588.json)

Molina explains the particular through a numerically single action, its specification by the creature, and an eternal divine determination to assist under the conditions of exercise. Each contributes to the whole effect as a partial cause. His regress challenges an added created power which would itself need activation. A defender of simultaneous application can deny that each exercise requires a fresh activating power with an exercise of its own. That reply still owes an explanation of the direction of dependence in the one act. Conversely, a standing willingness to cooperate explains the giver's commitment. To obtain efficient credit in this case, that commitment must issue in an actual contribution. Molina expressly affirms that it does. [Disp. 26, §§9, 11–16](https://freddoso.com/translat/molina26.htm)

The word *exercise* can obscure the disagreement. If it designates the one action proceeding from the fire and received in the water, Molina positively attributes that action to God. If it designates God's putting the fire itself to work as the subject of a further causal dependence, Molina disputes the need for that ordering. Describing the patient-side heating as also the fire's action establishes no productive influence on the fire *as agent*. Equally, the absence of evidence for such an influence gives no reason to deny cooperation in the action. These different termini matter when interpreting the press comparison below.

**A test at the first instant.** Molina argues from the immediate conservation of the very effect after its secondary producer stops to the need for the same influence in its first production (§§7–8). Consider a seal whose impression survives the pressing. If the giver efficiently sustains *this impression itself* throughout, including its first instant, its action reaches the mark on the patient while the die still works. The die can explain the mark's shape. The giver need not also direct the die to explain the mark's real obtaining. This earns local credit on the reading of cause as effective responsibility for the actual determination. If all that is established is later preservation, a handover at release remains intelligible. For example, the force making a seam and the clamp subsequently holding that seam closed can have different sources. And if all that is conserved at onset is the wax, its presence under both a blank and an engraved die leaves the giver's role in the actual impression open. The premise with leverage is continuous efficient dependence of the **same effect in the relevant respect**, including onset. It is stronger than either premise about the bearer or about later persistence. Aquinas's account of conservation as continuation of the giving of existence supports continuity on an independently identified subject of such giving. It does not by itself identify each mark as such a subject. [ST I q.104 a.1, body and reply 4](../../corpus/passages/aquinas-summa-theologiae-q104-en.json)

**A test of causal direction.** Hold an incoming beam, mirror and target fixed. An actuator holding a tilting mirror against a load during reflection keeps the illuminated spot at a particular location. One can trace the exerted force to the maintained angle and the angle to the outgoing ray. Changing the maintained angle moves the spot. Now let a latch bear the load after the actuator sets the same angle. The same optical explanation still applies, but the latch now does the holding. The actuator retains historical credit for the setting. These cases illustrate why retaining precisely the feature by which an effect is determined can give a maintainer productive credit in that respect. Supplying the bare existence of the mirror under every angle leaves open who holds the angle. Even the active controller's contribution to the spot's location does not show it supplies the light or initiates reflection. The test is about the role of a source in a specified respect. An inseparable existential cause need not be physically switchable. In its case the counterpart to the force path would be an independently warranted account of how the giver's influence enters the exercise or reaches the effect itself.

**When two descriptions pick out one efficient terminus.** Pluck one string and identify the resulting oscillation by following that string's motion from onset. Causing this oscillation to begin gives the plucker credit for the string's starting to vibrate. Motion of this string constitutes the entire change picked out by the second description. There is no further transition needed to put the string into motion once precisely that oscillation is underway. Producing the string on which the oscillation takes place also gives rise to an entity essential to this particular's history, but an idle string shows what further transition that explanation leaves outstanding. The distinction is between producing the complete change under two descriptions and producing one of its conditions. It does not depend on whether ordinary language has a noun for the change.

This yields a limited positive argument for a **distinct dependent particular**. Suppose the subject and individuation of e are settled independently, e's beginning consists entirely in x's acquiring this very r, and the named giver efficiently originates e itself. A de re attribution of effective responsibility for that acquisition then has a singular causal terminus already in hand. Demanding that the giver also pick the pitch, or first act on the plucker, adds a further condition to that attribution. The strongest objection is respect sensitivity. Producing one oscillation can deserve credit for the fact of vibration without explaining the precise pitch or its being consonant with a different string. Co-reference to an event does not transfer causal credit under all these descriptions. The argument reaches the respect for which the identified production is already the complete change. Under a reading that demands contrastive responsibility for the pitch, one needs the further contribution of what sets the pitch. For a **nonsubsistent determination**, the same reasoning starts with independently identified efficient influence on the subject's change. Universal coverage of existing individuals cannot furnish that starting point merely by adding an index e. ### Provisional judgment and graph consequences

I favor productive credit to the existential giver **where its own action can be identified in the realization of this determination**, including credit through a secondary or immediately with it. This is weaker than requiring that the giver direct each secondary to act. Immediate concurrence is the strongest standing objection to that requirement. It explains how both agents contribute to the same particular while allowing the creature to determine the action's kind. The ordinary cases distinguish the roles and support this limited criterion for attribution. They give no basis to prefer a universal theory of application over immediate concurrence. The conservationist poses the stronger challenge to **either** form of universal concurrence. An intrinsically active, sustained power engaging its patient supplies a positive explanation of why a heating occurs. Accepting such operation as primitive commits the rival to real local efficacy. Nothing in this comparison shows it must also posit a brute second event that makes the operation actual. Its weakest point is the scope of divine conservation at onset. ## 12. What the conservationist grants about this effect

**Judgment.** For an ordinary effect of an exercised power, I favor mediated causal responsibility over the claim that the giver of that very operative power makes no productive contribution to the effect. Durand himself grants the mediated attribution. His conservationism therefore supplies no counterexample to an instance of `b26c10a0` merely by denying immediate divine concurrence. A stronger rival remains available. It grants that the giver sustains the bearer while locating the productive feature, its history and its exercise elsewhere. Whether this rival works depends on what the giver actually supplies. The word *conservation* alone settles neither the positive nor the negative attribution. ### Durand on one action and two causes

Durand asks whether God reaches the very form produced by the creature. Creating the pre-existing matter answers a different question (§6). A concept common to being and living supplies no second immediate agent over and above the agent producing the living thing (§10). A natural power adequate to its kind can elicit its act given the conservation of its nature and power (§11). In §12 he explicitly credits the proximate cause immediately and the remote universal cause mediately with the **same numerical action**, because the latter gives and preserves the former's power. His two ship-haulers allow joint immediate action where each contributes only part of what is needed. His restriction concerns two distinct powers each claimed to be the complete immediate principle of the one action. The position thus grants substantially more than dependence of the matter or a divine permission to act. This is redaction C, II d.1 q.5. The parallel in redaction A has different question numbering. [Pinned Lyon 1563 witness, §§6, 10–12, 17](../../corpus/passages/39ad946a-1814-4dd7-82ec-9c06ab19fcfb.json) · [Venice parallel, §12](https://durandus.uni-koeln.de/chunks/II_1_5_12)

Two questions consequently require different verdicts. **Can a secondary agent be the complete proximate producer?** Durand offers an affirmative account. **Does this exclude effective responsibility of the giver of the power by which it produces?** On his own account the answer is no. Aquinas's account of the efficacy of a higher power in a lower one and Molina's account of immediate partial co-production challenge different parts of the first answer. Durand's argument against two complete immediate producers is insufficient against Molina, who distinguishes producing the whole effect from supplying the whole causal contribution. Conversely, a reason for the giver to receive mediated credit cannot by itself decide that debate over immediacy. Freddoso explicitly defines a merely remote cause as an *active cause* and frames the disagreement about production in terms of immediate and per se causation. His account of immediate divine conservation also extends to constituents and accidents, a substantive scope premise beyond preserving bearers. [Freddoso, §§2–4](https://freddoso.com/papers/conserv.htm) · [Molina on one operation](../../corpus/passages/db477c06-7c60-4a05-be0e-6953fdfbe588.json)

### Does a complete local explanation exclude the giver?

Consider a spring driving an escapement. Its tension, the teeth in engagement and the release explain this advance of the wheel. Suppose one infers that because the wheel's response is fully settled once those local facts are fixed, whoever supplied the tension made no productive contribution. Apply that test first to an identifiable winding operation. Leave the wheel and its release alone. Wind the spring farther within its working range and more work becomes available. Disconnect the winder and the spring can retain the work already put into it. In the actual run, follow that stored work through the engaged teeth to the wheel. Now demand that one vary the winding **while holding fixed the very charge it produced**. An independent replacement charge can accomplish this. The resulting invariance shows that the outcome is insensitive to which source provides the same charge under replacement. It would erase an acknowledged case of mediated production if used to deny the actual winder any credit for the wheel's advance.

This defeats the proposed inference on behalf of the stronger rival. Fixing a sufficient intermediate state can screen off a cause precisely because its influence reached the effect through that state. It gives no general reason to delete upstream causes. Equally, if the winder supplied a disconnected reserve while another spring drove the wheel, the same amount of work being available in the apparatus gives the winder no such role. What discriminates the two explanations is the provenance of the working input, including storage, routing and engagement. The actual starter can deserve credit for the moment of release, and the spring's maker for its elastic response. Credit for advancing the wheel does not require that every contributor settle the timing. An earlier winding can earn historical credit after the winder has ceased to act. Identifying a **present** existential giver as responsible requires connecting its present contribution or its own prior gift to the operative power, with the two temporal attributions kept distinct.

This argument has a limit that matters for natural powers. A finite charge is spent in doing work. A capacity such as elasticity can remain while many charges come and go. Showing that the source supplies the material of an elastic body, while its temper and shape are acquired from another process, can leave that source's respect-specific role unsettled. Changing the temper while preserving the material can alter the impulse produced on release. That supplies a positive explanation of which feature does the work. It cannot identify which source, if any, continuously bestows the efficacy of that feature. Conversely, durable powers can have mediated effects without consuming a portion of themselves. For example, changing the tooth spacing of a retained escapement changes the step it actually transmits. The cutter that gave these very teeth their spacing can acquire historical credit for the step size even though no cutter operates during release. That argument requires continuity of the operative profile into this use. It would not extend to a cutter whose teeth had been replaced before engagement.

### Is the first instant an exception?

Suárez's challenge has force against a conservationist who accepts immediate conservation of the very effect after production. If this very heat intrinsically requires a present divine cause in virtue of its participated being, heat already has that character when the fire produces it. The simple fact that the heat is now older supplies no change in what it is. Suárez himself considers the reply that God might take over immediately only when the fire ceases. He also presses the parallel question about the created action itself. The proposed handover is his dialectical reconstruction. [Suárez, *Disputationes metaphysicae* XXII.1.7–9, p. 3](../../corpus/passages/dde24765-6aeb-457d-a4c7-aa9f44fea06d.json)

There are two ways to test the asymmetry. First, keep the explanation of the later need fixed. If the reason given is an intrinsic feature of the effect sufficient to require influence from **this same giver in this same mode** whether or not the secondary producer is still acting, and the effect already has that feature at onset, the onset exception contradicts the reason given. If the actual reason is loss of an alternative producer, a change in the causal surroundings can matter with no intrinsic change in the effect. A clamp taking over the load of a press is a case in which such a handover can be identified. This vindicates the possibility of a change in the source of support. It gives no positive reason for a special divine takeover whenever any effect is just past its beginning. A participant's need for *some* cause and its need for immediate influence from *this* cause also differ. The distinction survives even if its need for some cause is intrinsic.

Second, compare production and preservation where a secondary cause continues to operate. A lamp can light a surface and continue illuminating it. Keeping the illumination going still calls for a locally active source. The mere sufficiency of a secondary explanation at onset therefore cannot be the reason to deny immediate divine causation there if one grants it during equally well-explained persistence. The strongest conservationist answer keeps one policy throughout: the local source acts in both phases, and the giver's gift and conservation of that source's operative power earn it mediated responsibility for the resulting determination. Durand (§17) can grant that the agents in his example depend immediately on God for their being. The mediation in his conclusion concerns their action. This answer gives up any premise that every dependent determination *as such* needs immediate divine efficient action once it has lasted. It retains present existential dependence of the working substances throughout. The evidence here favors that uniform treatment over introducing a change of divine causal mode solely because an effect has begun. It leaves immediate concurrence open against the uniform mediated account. [Earlier first-instant test](#can-effect-side-concurrence-do-the-explanatory-work) · [Freddoso's symmetry argument and its premise about conservation, §4 and notes 25–26](https://freddoso.com/papers/conserv.htm)

The hard case is heat remaining after the fire has gone. Invoking a continuously burning lamp cannot explain that case. On the determination reading, consider a heated, insulated body. Heating changes the state of its material. Once heating stops, that acquired state supplies a basis for subsequent thermal behavior, and changing the insulation changes its rate of loss. The body need not keep performing the original action of heating itself in order to be hot for a time. This supplies a positive account of retention while granting that the bearer and its actual material powers are continually sustained. It relies on persistence of the acquired state under the relevant dynamics. Insulation alone would leave a cold body cold. On the distinct-entity reading, if a particular quality has a standing need for a present efficient cause of its *own* esse, appeal to insulation or absence of a destroyer does not identify the cause that meets that need. Here Suárez has the stronger argument against combining immediate conservation on essential grounds with an exception at onset. A rival can consistently take a continued state of the subject as basic only if that is indeed its ontology or if it explains how it grounds the quality. There is a further limit to the conservationist reply. When a secondary generates a new substance, the effect itself is now a bearer. A rule that restricts immediate conservation to substances still raises Suárez’s first-instant question for that very individual. The insulation example explains a retained determination in an already existing subject. It gives no account of why a newly generated substance would first need its giver only after it begins. This is the additional difficulty Freddoso raises in note 26.

### When an upstream contribution can be established independently

Take a machine with one unit of usable input already in storage and one more arriving from a named supplier. The machine spends one unit on work and finishes with one. Both histories fit: the new unit did the work, or the new unit stayed in storage. Now suppose it spends both units, with no other inlet. Every accounting that respects these quantities assigns the supplier a contribution to the work. If the machine powered two separate one-unit operations, either could still have used the stored unit. A measurement isolating the selected operation, or evidence about the routing, distinguishes those cases. This bears on what was consumed in the actual run even when a backup *could* have done the work. A certificate of actual contribution and a claim of indispensability under replacement answer different questions.

More generally, let W be the work expressed in units of a conserved input, P the opening stock plus receipts from other inlets, and A the named receipts in the window. In a balanced account the possible named contributions range from max(0, W − P) to min(W, A), inclusive. The lower bound follows because the pool can provide at most P. To see sharpness, take any integer n between the bounds. Assign n of the named units and W − n of the pooled units to work. Both classes have enough units, and the units left over add up to measured closing stock by balance. Thus an independently measured deficit in the other available supply warrants credit for some input from this source. An earlier gift from the same supplier may already be included in opening stock. The bounds concern new receipts. [General result](../../corpus/results/18548e6e-ac9e-4fad-87cd-64feef80384a.json) · [Two-output comparison](../../corpus/results/7331bf4a-2791-418c-b4b0-ed17250cdd2e.json)

In Lean the units are nonnegative integers freely routable between work and closing stock. Under that model every integer in the interval is realized. Restricted plumbing, losses, arrival order or a different patient boundary can change the applicable model. An exercised contributor step in this reading means that a positive amount of the identified input was used for the work. In a real case, that interpretation needs an identified productive use, as distinct from coincident waste. The event indices themselves supply no giver of esse. The result is affirmative evidence that mediated contribution can sometimes be identified through an independently testable feature. It supplies no conservation law for esse and no general premise that exercising a natural power depletes it.

**How far does the argument extend beyond consumable inputs?** Suppose an agent is given the very nature in virtue of which it has a basic power. Merely observing that the agent could not exist without that power leaves open the direction of explanation. The stronger case is where giving this organized nature itself realizes the capacity: assembling a balance mechanism with its pivot and arms in working order makes it able to compare loads. Identifying the principle in operation connects the assembly to a later comparison through that capacity. A person who supplies previously manufactured parts can also earn credit, but the assembly has its own explanatory work to do. This provides a reason to include basic powers when the named source is known to give the concrete nature **with its operative organization**. The argument loses that purchase when the only identified gift is a bearer's existence, or when the feature doing the work arises through a later reorganization that has yet to be traced to the source. This distinction leaves open mediated credit to the same giver through the reorganizing agents. Establishing it requires tracing their powers in turn. Simply repeating the claim at each new link gives no reason that the chain reaches this giver in the requisite respect.

## 13. Who causes an acquired quality or a relation to obtain?

A worker lengthens one rod until it matches a second. It is now true of the untouched rod that it is equal to the first. Does the cause of that equality have to act on the untouched rod? And if one giver sustains **both** rods, does that make the giver responsible for their equality?

**Provisional answer.** The worker can make the equality obtain by acting on one rod. The other rod's existing length helps constitute the match. Knowing who sustains both rods in existence leaves open who gives them these lengths or brings them into agreement. For an acquired quality the corresponding question is which exercise produced this determination and what preserved its contribution through later changes. In both cases one effective contribution by the named giver can earn mediated credit, even if someone else selects the outcome. Calling a relation true, a subject dependent, or a causal history finite does not identify that contribution. [Previous question on the giver of an operative power](#12-what-the-conservationist-grants-about-this-effect)

### An acquired quality can have more than one productive route

Press a letter into wax, smooth it out, and impress the same letter again. Consider two histories of the second die. In one, it was made from a copy of the first impression before the wax was smoothed. In the other, it was made independently from an alphabet master. The bearer and the two letters can look exactly alike in both histories. Yet if the original impression had contained a distinctive error, the copying process would have conveyed it to the second die, while the independent process would still have used the master's pattern. The stamp, copy and surviving template give a reason to credit the first impression's maker for the renewed *lettering* in the former history. The first maker's contribution to the earlier lettering gives no such reason in the latter. Erasure at the patient alone settles neither case. A difference elsewhere in the productive history matters to who produced the acquired quality here.

A patient can also contribute actively to what it acquires. Consider a spring-loaded mechanism with two possible seats. A selector opens the way to one seat. The spring then pushes the movable part home. There are two relevant tests. With the route selected, unload the spring and support the part short of the seat. It can be correctly aligned yet unseated. With the same spring loaded in a second run, let an independent actuator carry the part home while a catch takes the spring's force off the part. In the first operating arrangement the spring supplies the final movement. In the second it can be present, loaded and released without supplying that movement. Engagement under load, the changed position, and the path of the force distinguish the roles. This is a comparison of mechanisms, not a report of an experiment. It supports an inference about the spring's giver **if** that giver supplied the operative capacity or input that was transmitted in this seating. Simply being capable of receiving the setting is a different role. The selector's independent choice of seat is consistent with both explanations. [Existing test of productive credit and mere enablement](load-bearing-terms.md)

These cases answer a quantifier question hidden in §12. Universal transfer asks for credit to the same giver at **each respect**. It does not require that giver to have selected each outcome, supplied all its immediate inputs, or excluded other agents. For this route, one surviving contribution to an actual realization of each eligible bearer’s respect would suffice. In the second stamping the failure of the copying route says nothing about possible credit through the power that drove the new die. Conversely, an effect may depend on many actually existing participants without its proposed giver's action being traced through any of them in the relevant respect. Their existential dependence is shared ground between the accounts.

### A relation can be real at an unchanged subject

Aristotle observes that changing one correlate can make a relational predication true or false of the other while the latter remains unchanged. Aquinas's commentary takes up the harder case in which the relation is real at both ends. Each person's height already grounds equality with anyone of that height. When another person attains my height, my existing quantity now grounds equality with **this person**. The particular equality did not already obtain while the heights were unequal. The newly specified term requires no new height or internal operation in me. A new color, by contrast, requires an alteration of the colored subject on the account Aquinas is discussing. [*Physics* V.2, 225b12–15](../../corpus/claims/aristotle-physics-225b-relative-motion.json) · [*In Physicorum* V, lect. 3, nn. 7–8](../../corpus/passages/6cad8064-7503-48a2-b29a-c5f06c4c0a76.json)

The column at an animal's right is a useful control. Aquinas locates the real basis of right and left in the animal's orientation. A change there can make a description of the column true. It supplies no newly acquired accident of orientation in the column. In the equality example the unchanged person's own quantity grounds a real relation. **“Nothing changed inside x” cannot decide which case we have.** The test is what in each relatum grounds the relation under the selected account. ST I q.13 a.7 also distinguishes the two sides of the creature–Creator relation. Using that theological example to identify a still unnamed universal giver would assume an identification the GOD_4 route is meant to earn. The corpus's earlier `RelationalReceipt` tests concern an origin relation to the principle of being. The two ends of that relation do not interchange as the heights in an equality do. [ST I q.13 a.7, body and replies 4–5](../../corpus/passages/aquinas-summa-theologiae-q13-a7.json) · [Existing receipt interpretation](../../TruthCore/Causality/RelationalReceipt.lean)

**Why credit the worker at the unchanged rod?** A gauge set from that rod can guide the extension of the other. The stopping operation brings their measured difference to zero. Keep both rods, the gauge and the starting lengths fixed but stop the extension short. The rods still exist and remain unequal. The worker's action reaches the feature that completes the match. Each direction of predication has its own subject. Even on an ontology that distinguishes the two subject-side relations, the same matched quantities explain both; equality of explanation does not make them one accident. Reversing “the fixed rod equals the extended one” to “the extended rod equals the fixed one” preserves this reason. A rival can consistently demand a separate cause for each newly produced intrinsic accident. In this example, stopping short prevents the match in either direction, and on the founded reading the fixed rod’s existing quantity already supplies its side of the comparison. That explains why the worker can cause the new match with a single extension. This warrants mediated responsibility for the **obtaining of the relational fact**, if that is the reading of causing x in this respect. A narrower claim about producing an intrinsic alteration in x has a different terminus. A realist who posits a further irreducible relational tie owes an account of what fixes it with the quantities, comparison standard and numerical pair held fixed. A theory on which the tie necessarily follows the complete foundation need not postulate a second independent act to fasten it. Constitution of the match by its foundations and efficient production of a foundation still do different explanatory work.

The distinction also bears on stable matches. Two pieces can agree because both were cut to the same working gauge. Citing the machining and gauge explains why the pair matches even if replacing that common gauge with a slightly larger common gauge would leave an equality. Conversely, equal additions to the ends of an unequal pair leave the same difference. A source could genuinely alter both lengths yet contribute nothing to *closing their gap* by those additions. The first example asks who produced the matching lengths. The second asks what changed the relation from false to true. Dependence on a comparison under one specified variation is evidence about that contrast. Its absence cannot establish the absence of every productive contribution.

Not all relational foundations are endpoint qualities. Mount the same unaltered pieces in contact, then mount them with a gap. Their internal lengths and shapes can be held fixed, but their new arrangement affects whether a compressive load can pass directly from one to the other. That is an independent reason to include placement in the foundation of contact. Giving each piece its intrinsic dimensions can leave open who brought about this conjunction. Conversely, a source whose influence actually reaches the seating operation might earn credit even if another maker fashioned the pieces. A relation such as parenthood requires a further historical fact about generation. There is no single two-length test for all real relations.

### The universal giver and a complete causal history

The affirmative case begins with **concrete productive natures**. Suppose the giver gives a nature precisely with its active organizing power, that power is exercised in acquiring a skill, and the resulting capacity is actually used on a later occasion. The acquired capacity can carry an influence that began with the native one. A complete account of practice would identify what the learner did, what changed, and how that change guided the later act. The same learner could remain in existence without doing this practice. But a teacher choosing the exercise does not annul the learner's active contribution to acquiring the skill. The giver need not choose the exercise to act mediately through the learner's effective power. The argument has purchase because the operative feature and its transmission are identifiable apart from attributing the final act to the giver. A finite succession of actual transmissions can extend the same explanation to much later products.

The conservationist rival can grant all of that where the premises hold. Its stronger reply is that later organization arises from interactions of sustained subjects and that keeping the subjects in being need not itself produce each operative input. The copied and independently recut dies explain how histories diverge inside a population of existing bearers. Neither case requires another *existentially independent* source. The rival gains no argument for **zero** mediated credit merely by accounting for local transmission. It must explain why every proposed effective route from the named giver to this particular misses. The affirmative account likewise gains no universal victory from observing that every producer in the history exists. It must show that at least one actual productive route to each eligible respect reaches this giver. Tracing one downstream exercise after another can display that condition where origins are independently known. In a circle of mutually refreshed copies, tracing internal copying links never settles who first supplied the pattern. A history with no first member would need another basis for attribution, such as an independently identified present operative contribution. The claim concerns a source that enters the particular productive route under examination. It entails no temporal beginning of the world.

There is a stronger affirmative suggestion than reliance on the bare word “all.” A universal giver gives the very concrete agents that work, and a real agent always exists in some determinate state. Why should that gift stop short of their operative states? Compare two questions about the second stamp. Who supplied *the die that in fact bears this lettering*? Who supplied it *insofar as it bears this lettering*? One maker may have supplied a finished engraved die. Another may have supplied an otherwise identical blank that a different engraver later cut. The definite description can pick out one existing die in each case, though it explains the lettering only in the first history by itself. For the existential giver the blank, engraver, and completed die all fall under its scope. There is therefore a further available route through the engraver that the ordinary blank maker need not have. If giving the engraver its concrete nature includes giving the discriminating or moving power actually transmitted in the cut, that route can earn mediated credit for this engraving. The special extension of the universal giver's scope makes this a serious positive argument. It does not establish the required respect-specific attribution by substitution of names. Giving a concrete nature with its characteristic powers is the strongest direct premise here. To credit the giver with a particular later determination through those powers, we also need their history of use.

### Two formal tests

**Following the productive history.** Fix one named source, a family of events for which a history is being claimed, and a natural-number stage for each event. At every covered event require either an actual contributor step from an event of that source, or one such step from a covered event of lower stage. Induction then constructs a nonempty productive path from that source to **the very covered event**. A starting event merely bearing the source's name does not suffice. Stages order the chosen antecedents. Other inputs can come from outside the family, and the stages need not be clock times. An earlier available power that never enters the productive group fails the step requirement. A loop without entry fails the strict descent condition. The apparatus formalizes why the same source can retain credit through a change of bearer or a new acquisition if something productive is transmitted at each link. The interpretation of the chosen groups is open. [History vocabulary and exact interfaces](../../TruthCore/Causality/AcquiredHistoryTransfer.lean)

The transfer proof takes this ancestry together with two further premises. For every eligible source and every distinct recipient at each non-esse respect demanded by the original target, the history family must contain an occurring event at that **recipient and respect**. At esse the target already supplies the edge. Finally, a productive path interpreted as credit in the exercise frame must warrant effective causation in the original frame. With these inputs Lean derives the existing universal claim verbatim. At each eligible bearer and respect the target is a bearer-level causal attribution. The history premise only needs one corresponding occurring realization. If the same bearer bears that respect in many episodes, a path to one of them does not establish source credit for every token or for a different date. A claim about a specified token must preserve its witness, or refine the respect or frame. The coverage premise contains no conclusion about productive paths. The interpretation premise contains no assumption that a universal giver exists or that it has a path to any event. If one builds the history family by looking up exactly the final causal attributions one hopes to prove, these distinctions add no independent warrant. Actual records of copying, replacement and engagement give other ways to warrant a *particular* history. A standing real relation may be inadequately represented by an event of its bearer. A proof built on that representation needs an account of why a change of the other relatum realizes this relational respect at the named bearer. Assigning a tag is no such account. This route establishes a conditional support option alongside the previous total-cause derivation. In the earlier route, a source of esse for all the other bearers must qualify as a total cause of each effect, total causation must extend to the modes, and each actual respect must be such a mode. The history route need not settle total causation. It asks for one operative line reaching each eligible bearer in its actual respect. Its own difficulty is explaining why every eligible respect is reached, particularly one that is standing rather than newly acquired. The two accounts overlap in their assessment of the giver’s productive reach; their separate proofs are not two independent demonstrations that its reach is universal. The three open inputs can hold together in the existing application profile of the press. There the giver drives the stroke and a separate indicator, and the stroke makes an imprint on the patient. The chosen cover consists of those three outcomes for that giver. The imprint has a higher stage than the stroke. The giver gives the distinct patient esse, and the patient actually has the work respect, so this checks a non-esse instance. In this profile `CausesIn` already means productive path credit. **Varying the two foundations.** A separate integer model studies the test “left reading + offset < right reading.” Fix the readings with the named source's two assigned increments omitted and all other contributions retained. Restoring its increments turns on exactly those offsets from the residual right-minus-left gap, inclusive, to the actual gap, exclusive. If the gap shrinks, the reversed interval describes the offsets turned off. Every signed offset is insensitive to the contribution exactly when the two increments are equal. The zero-offset case is an ordinary strict comparison. It is distinct from equality itself and from Aquinas's ontological judgment about equal heights. In two control histories the fixed rod stays at 2 units while the other goes from 1 to 3. Both “the fixed rod is shorter than the extending rod” and “the extending rod is longer than the fixed rod” turn true. In one profile the named universal giver drives the extension, in the other a distinct producer does. The giver's esse edges remain present in both profiles. Omitting its contribution to the extension in the second leaves the other producer's action in place, so the comparison still holds there. This residual need not be the earlier observed state. The exact transfer formula holds in the first profile and fails on a comparative instance in the second. For these two comparative respects the model measures effective difference under that particular omission. It does not trace the other producer's power to the universal source or exclude redundant contributors. The four-role assignment therefore neither establishes nor refutes universal causal transfer in reality. The history proof and these control profiles use different interpretations. **Assessment.** The absence of an intrinsic alteration at the named subject is a poor objection to causal responsibility for a real founded comparison. The other end can supply the decisive change. For acquired qualities, the preservation of a real contribution through practice, copying or active response is a better test than persistence of the bearer or sameness of the resulting quality. I favor these two forms of local attribution. For universal transfer this route needs at least one qualifying realization for each eligible bearer at each actual non-esse respect, with a contribution from that very existential source. Whether the histories cover that range, and whether the attribution applies to real relational respects, remains open. The same wax could receive its lettering twice. A path to the first impression warrants no inference by itself about who made the second. A case in which the alleged contribution is screened off by actual replacement, while preserving an independent route to the same observed outcome, would defeat that *specific attribution*. A reason why at least one such history for each eligible bearer and respect includes a source-supplied input would strengthen the universal case. Neither revision follows merely from adding actualities to the formal domain.
