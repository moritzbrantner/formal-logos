## Positive package and the explanatory fork

Layer 1: posit substantive necessary ethical principles, actual nonmoral
features and their applicability conditions. Necessity concerns the conditional
or principle, not the actual existence of a suffering episode or child.
Mind independence does not mean absence of relations to contingent creatures.

Layer 2: distinguish two candidate interfaces. An instantiation account says
that an actual case satisfies the antecedent of a true normative principle,
so its derivative moral status follows. A robust-making account additionally
asserts metaphysical production/dependence of that status on those features.
Neither entailment nor covariance defines that extra relation. If it is causal,
the cause is the natural feature and the moral property may be an effect with
no further causal powers; there is no contradiction from causal impotence.
No temporal process, energy transfer, asymmetry or well-foundedness is inferred
merely from the word making. These remain OPEN interpretation/property burdens.

Layer 3: substantive norms plus accurate antecedent facts conditionally entail
the token moral status. Under an instantiation account this is a candidate
explanation, not yet an answer to the explanatory objection. Whether it has
the required direction and relevance, rather than merely restating a true
conditional, remains **OPEN**. Defending the norm's content or explaining why
it is fundamental is a further question; separating these questions does not
establish that the token explanation succeeds. The strongest rival reply takes
necessary ethical truths as legitimate explanatory termini and contingent
creatures as their instances, with no random matching event requiring an
arranger. A comparative argument must justify rejecting that stopping point
and apply the same standard to divine goodness, wisdom and legitimate authority.

## Two discriminating cases

Pain/cruelty: fix a sentient patient, an agent deliberately causing intense
unwanted suffering solely for enjoyment, feasible refraining, and absence of
consent, therapeutic benefit or emergency rescue. A candidate basic norm says
that such suffering counts against causing it independently of endorsement.
The actual features plus that substantive norm supply a pro-tanto reason;
a decisive prohibition additionally needs the relevant priority/defeater rule.
Pain's badness for the patient alone does not quantify over agents' duties.
Pleasure, anesthesia and justified treatment variations test applicability,
not a change from necessary norms to contingent norms. This is conditional
token entailment and a candidate explanation under the
instantiation account; explanatory direction and relevance remain OPEN. It
does not derive the norm from pain's purely descriptive character.

Parental duty: fix equal moral worth of two children, equal immediate need,
one parent's established care responsibility for one child, ability to help,
and no replacement carer. Need supplies a welfare consideration; a separate
responsibility norm selects who owes care. Biology alone must not silently
supply that responsibility: adoption and abandonment are discriminating cases.
A necessary relational norm can apply to contingent adopted relationships
without treating duties as optional. A parent who lacks ability exposes a
scope/assistance question rather than proving the child has no moral worth.
When both children need rescue and only one can be saved, a pro-tanto special
duty does not yet fix decisive priority over greater urgency or survival benefit.
Standing to demand and all-things-considered priority remain separate questions
for #1508; no numeric or total-order decision procedure is assumed here.

These examples favor testing a structured natural base with patient, agent,
relationship, alternatives and defeaters. A single Boolean base loses exactly
the relations at issue. A second arbitrary mapping over that base would only
repeat the existing control, so no duplicate Lean library was introduced.

## Symmetric replacement comparison and proposed assessment

The robust realist can take impartial concern plus special responsibility as
basic, applying without a person's endorsement. That is a positive replacement
with specific content, not a rulebook whose authority depends on consent.
Its open burden is warrant for those principles and whether primitive making
adds explanation beyond true normative instantiation. This is not a known
inconsistency or a demonstrated explanatory failure.

The #1460 theistic proposal could connect creaturely vulnerability, goods,
intelligible ends and moral order through one wise source. For these cases it
still needs sentience-to-due-good, due-good-to-agent-reason, responsibility and
priority bridges, plus wise intentional authorship and rightful jurisdiction.
Bare creation, actuality goodness, or a common source supplies none by itself.

natural bases. They also need the independently defended impartial or normative
criterion and a responsibility account. Their lack of universal instrumental
incentive for the indifferent agent is not absence of moral obligation. None
of these three packages wins merely by having fewer named primitives.

Proposed overall provisional assessment: the positive necessary-norm package
supplies conditional token entailment from substantive norms and accurate

## Responsibility/urgency test: complete revised draft for challenge

The earlier proposed test assumed an “adoptive responsible carer.” This test
instead stipulates descriptive facts, without building moral responsibility
into a person's title. These are analyst cases, not reports about actual people.

Mira and Noor are sentient children of equal moral worth. Beno is Mira's
biological parent, but has never provided care or given assurances; nothing
is stipulated about how Mira came to depend on others or Beno's past ability
to intervene. Ada is unrelated biologically to either child, has voluntarily
undertaken Mira's daily care, and has repeatedly assured Mira and the other
adults that she will collect and feed her. Mira has consequently come to rely
on Ada; adults who otherwise would collect her have made other arrangements.
Sol has undertaken no such care and made no assurances to either child.
In three separate rescue trials Beno, Ada or Sol is the sole adult present.
Each can rescue either child at equal personal cost, with equal success, but
cannot rescue both; no other help can arrive in time. In trial E the children's
preventable harms are equal. In trial U only Noor's threatened harm increases
substantially; ability, success, relationships, prior assurances and Mira's
threatened harm stay fixed. These stipulations describe available alternatives;
they do not supply permission, obligation, comparative desert or standing.

Compare three explicit candidate packages, not exhaustive or historically
attributed theories:

- Robust normative realism R adds a substantive principle: voluntarily inducing
  a vulnerable person's reliance through care and assurances gives the agent
  a pro-tanto duty to secure that care or an adequate substitute. Impartial
  concern supplies reasons to aid both children. Applying the responsibility
  principle assigns Ada a special duty to Mira. Its antecedent is absent for
  Beno and Sol; this is no derivation of their freedom from *other* duties.
  A biological duty for Beno requires an additional principle. Necessity of R's
  principle would make its applicability conditional, not the relationships
  necessary. Fundamental normative truth is R's positive stopping point; warrant
  and explanatory direction remain open.
- A naturalistic interests package N identifies reliance, interrupted care and
  avoidable vulnerability as harms to objective interests. This identifies
  features relevant to Ada and Mira, but does not itself yield Ada's obligation.
  Add explicitly a normative principle that agents ought to protect interests
  they voluntarily lead vulnerable people to entrust to them. That principle
  yields Ada's pro-tanto duty; Beno's biology alone again supplies no derivation
  under this selected rule. The attempted reduction from a description of
  reliance to an obligation fails at the missing normative inference. This is
  a recorded gap of this reconstruction, not a refutation of all naturalism.
- A theistic package T adds that legitimate divine direction requires agents
  to protect vulnerable people whose reliance they voluntarily induce, and that
  this direction binds them. Those are substantive premises in addition to a
  wise creator. They yield Ada's pro-tanto duty; creation alone did not entail
  their content or jurisdiction. A rule selecting biological parents still
  needs separate defense. This tests the proposed bridge rather than claiming
  that theism cannot furnish one.

A prima facie rationale for the selected responsibility principle is that
voluntarily eliciting reliance changes the patient's practical alternatives:
Mira and the other adults arranged care around Ada's assurances. Abandoning
that arrangement exposes Mira to harm Ada could avert by fulfilling it or
securing a substitute. This gives the principle content beyond mere kinship
and a reason to distinguish Ada from Sol. The normative step remains explicit:
that control over induced vulnerability warrants a special duty. It is a
candidate primitive supported by this rationale, not a demand for an endlessly
prior proof. No substantive objection to that bounded principle is established
here. Uninvited reliance is a proposed scope test, not adverse evidence; the
antecedent requires voluntary inducement. This rationale supports relevance
without settling how strong the duty is in a competing rescue.

Thus each expanded package derives the same *pro-tanto* Ada/Mira responsibility
conditionally on its stated normative premises. This agreement is not three
independent warrants: all packages share the contested undertaking-to-duty
content, presented in different explanatory settings. No theorem or general
non-entailment certificate is claimed. Beno's prior obligations are unresolved,
not erased by the fact that he has never undertaken care. In particular, a
causal-responsibility norm might apply to his role in creating dependency, but
the case does not stipulate that role merely from biological relatedness.

**OPEN care-to-rescue applicability.** Collecting and feeding do not by
themselves show that the threatened rescue harm concerns an interest entrusted
to Ada. The packages therefore yield the stated reliance duties without yet
yielding the same rescue reason. Hold reliance and urgency fixed and compare
harm to an entrusted interest with an unrelated emergency; establish each
package's scope before comparing rescue priority.

The following urgency comparison is conditional on an additional shared
applicability assumption: the rescue protects an interest within the undertaken
care under each package. That assumption is not derived here.

Now vary urgency. R, N and T as specified contain no conflict-resolution rule.
In E, Ada's special duty and the equal impartial reasons do not alone entail a
decisive choice; a rule giving the special duty tie-breaking force is additional.
For Beno and Sol even that selected tie-breaker has no demonstrated antecedent.
In U, assigning Ada a special duty still does not decide whether to save Mira
or Noor. This is the precise failure of the attempted duty-to-priority inference:
pro-tanto content does not state its weight against the increased harm. Two
conditional completions expose what a discriminating test would need:

1. A defeasible priority rule favors the entrusted child at equal harm, but
   requires rescuing the other child when the additional preventable harm is
   sufficiently grave. Under that rule Ada chooses Mira in E and Noor in U
   only if U satisfies the rule's independently specified threshold.
2. A protected special-duty rule gives Ada's entrusted patient priority even
   against that increase, within a specified domain of feasible rescue and
   absent an independently stated defeating condition. Under this rival Ada
   chooses Mira in E and in U within that domain.

Neither completion is derived or preferred here. No numeric threshold is
assumed, and “substantially greater” alone does not prove a defeater. Both retain
Noor's equal worth and a reason to rescue her; the second owes an account of
why the special duty withstands her greater harm. The first owes an account of
why and where reliance can be overridden. These are burdens, not contradictions.
Neither selection establishes standing to demand, blameworthiness or a total
priority order. Removing Ada's assurances while retaining habitual care is a
further discriminating case: the stated reliance principle might still apply
if the care itself voluntarily induces reliance; without that condition its
application remains open. A promise-only rule would require another antecedent.

**Draft judgment and strongest rival.** The comparison locates two shared gaps:
warrant for undertaking-to-duty content and a separately warranted conflict
rule. It does not show the packages equivalent in explanatory resources or
exclude a package-specific defense. The strongest standing robust-realist rival

could improve the comparison by independently deriving those contents from a
defended account of divine goodness and authority. Relabeling the desired rule
“wise” would not do so. The same prohibition on verdict-driven stipulation
applies to naturalistic “objective interests” and realist “basic norms.” No
winner or source attribution is certified.

## Does a child's actual nourishment identify its underived source? (#1812)

**Provisional judgment, 2026-11-24.** An observed
instance gives positive evidence of received material and of a determinate
operation in the patient. It also tells us *which* capacity an account of the
child's good must explain. The evidence selects a proximate material source.
An independently defended foundation for the child could select an underived
ancestor of that very child. These two selections answer different questions.
What would join them is evidence that the latter source gives or sustains the
organization by which this child makes use of the material.

### One patient, a measured course of feeds, a separate duty

Fix the infant in the first row of Table 2 of Davidsson et al. (2000). In
protocol A the authors estimated that 8.0% of the iron-58 dose in the formula
was incorporated into this child's circulating erythrocytes, using blood
isotope enrichment 14 days after feeding began. The course comprised six
feeds over about 24 hours. A nurse gave the first two to three bottles and
parents gave the rest at home. The estimate traces material from the labeled
course to this patient. It cannot apportion incorporation among bottles or
caregivers. The study did not measure
the value of that episode to the child, establish that the child was deficient,
or adjudicate anyone's care obligations. [Methods, pp. 848–849, and Table 2](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0007114500002464)

Fix the perfection under assessment as the child's exercise of the capacity
to incorporate dietary iron into red cells in the service of blood function.
The tracer identifies an exercise. The reason to count it as good *in this
respect* is its contribution to the child's own functioning, including
oxygen carriage by iron-containing hemoglobin. This gives a criterion
independent of which adult feeds the child. Larger tracer recovery need not
mean better functioning. The due-status judgment uses the scoped positive
direction of the form/end thesis: realization of a perfection appropriate to
the patient's form and end counts in its favor. The study supplies evidence
on realization. The evaluative direction remains a defensible premise.

### How far can the material be traced?

Iron mass admits additive accounting. Incorporation is something the child
*does with* an input. A feeder can repeatedly supply iron without losing
an equivalent quantity of their own capacity for incorporation. Repeated
feeding can support repeated operation of the same capacity. Neither point assigns
additive units of that capacity to the feeder–child edge. The percentage in
Table 2 measures the fate of a dose, not the sum of transfers across every
edge in an ancestry. On a short feeding ledger a bottle with an opening stock
can be a net exporter although its existence has an ordinary concrete ground.

This locates the failure at the application premise of the finite-flow result.
Even granting finite total throughput and a positive edge with no return path,
`returnOrNetExport` gives an upstream exporter in *that ledger*.
`actualRootOfLedger` additionally needs *every* actually grounded bearer to
cover exports by receipts there. Extending the interval to include filling the
bottle changes the boundary and who appears upstream. It supplies no reason
