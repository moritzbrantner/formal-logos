# Applying the demand for reasons to one's own stance (#756)

The objection: "If you reject the PSR, why demand a sufficient reason for it?" The
theorems are `stanceMap` and `bridgeLadder` in
`TruthCore.Results.Explanation.PSRSelfApplication.Solution`, over the vocabulary of
`TruthCore.Explanation.PSRSelfApplication`, which extends the #755 arena and the #397
norm schema. Both are zero-axiom. They establish conditional consequence and the
satisfiability of encoded bundles. They establish no PSR, exclude no brute foundation
unconditionally, and discharge no premise on a root route.

No source claim is added. Koons, Pruss, Della Rocca and their critics (#409) were not
read for this work, so nothing below is attributed to them.

## Conclusion

**The charge succeeds** against a stated rule in the same sense. A stance that says
nothing needs a reason cannot demand one. A stance that says every core commitment
needs a reason, and meets its demands, cannot accept a core commitment with no support.
Both are contradictions in stated commitments, and the second bundle's five members are
each deleted by a named model.

**The charge fails** when it moves from one sense of "reason" to the other. The named
`critic` at world `a` demands reasons for the claim that the PSR holds and for its own
core commitments, and meets them. Its root has epistemic support and no ontic reason.
Rejecting the universal PSR does not remove all reasons: the event there has a reason.
So bare `¬ PSR` plus a demand for evidence is consistent.

**What remains is a charge of asymmetry, and it is feature-relative.** A stance that
demands reasons for the PSR claim and not for its own root is selective relative to a
feature they share, and restricted relative to one they differ on. The same `selective`
stance has both standings, relative to `commitment` and `generalLaw`. No theorem here
says which feature is the right one. A feature test is also cheap: any status can be
made its own feature, so that agreeing on it forces agreeing in the status, and a boundary defined by having a reason holds in every arena and
excludes nothing. A serious boundary has to be stated independently of having a reason
and has to be able to fail, as the event-scoped boundary fails in `scene false`.

**The universal demand binds everyone.** No stance, PSR defender included, has at one
world every accepted claim backed by an accepted claim, well-founded support, and
something accepted. So anyone who accepts something and whose support is well-founded
has an accepted claim not backed by an accepted claim. A circle and an endless chain are
named models of the other exit, dropping well-foundedness. The critic can therefore
reply that a defender with well-founded support also has an unbacked claim. That is an
epistemic stopping point, and it is not an ontic one: in the `critic` model the event has
no support and has a reason, and the root has support and no reason.

**What would be needed to establish the PSR** is a premise that crosses from support
to explanation. A demand honored for the PSR claim is not enough: `defender` honors
it where the claim is false. The tested bridges:

| Bridge | Result | Cost or failure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `TrackingBridge`: supported truths have reasons | follows from the PSR; gives it under `SupportCoverage` | It is the PSR over the supported facts, so it cannot serve as an independent premise for that domain. |
| `EvidenceBridge`: truths used as evidence have reasons | follows from the PSR; forbids using a brute truth as evidence | Not implied by the demand (`premiseRoot`). Gives no PSR: `science` keeps a supported brute root that is never evidence. It constrains only facts used as evidence. |
| `GlobalDefeat`: an accessible brute truth defeats all support | with any support, no accessible brute truth; excludes a stance that accepts and demands a claim and meets its demands, when a brute truth is accessible | Valid, and undefended. The rival says defeat is local. Its core's three members are each deleted. At a self-accessible world, with any support, it cannot hold with the other members of the #755 collapse core. |

So, with a brute truth accessible, a stance that holds `GlobalDefeat`, accepts a claim it
demands and meets its demands is inconsistent, and under `EvidenceBridge` a brute truth
cannot be used as evidence. Neither bridge is established. The surviving
rebuttal is the restricted critic: defeat is local, the root is reached from its
effects and never used as evidence. That reply has a cost: under `EvidenceBridge` the
root cannot be evidence for anything, including a prediction. Whether prediction
can do without it is the separate #737 boundary, which is not imported here.

## The same test for a necessary ground

Necessary existence is not a free label. A necessary truth that necessitates what it
explains cannot explain a contingent truth, so the contingent act needs a
nonnecessitating reason, as in `ground true`. In `ground false` the contingent-fact PSR
holds and the necessary ground has no reason, so that model fails `FactPSR` as the
scene does. Each exempts something and owes an account of the line. `EvidenceBridge`
and `TrackingBridge` as stated bind contingent truths only, so they pass over a necessary
ground, which is exactly the exemption whose ground has to be defended. As #755 notes,
the `ground` signature has no predicate for personal agency, so nothing here separates
a necessary personal ground from a necessary impersonal one.

## The two relations

`Arena.explains w r f` (ontic) is `DEF-PSR-STRENGTH-REASON`. `Inquiry.supports w e p`
(epistemic) is `DEF-EPISTEMIC-SUPPORT`; `DISAMB-REASON-EPISTEMIC-ONTIC` keeps them apart.

- **Ordinary language.** "Why did it boil?" versus "why think it boiled?"
- **Examples and counterexamples.** Smoke supports "there is a fire" without being why
  there is one. A cause nobody has noticed is a reason why, and supports nothing for
  anyone. In `critic`, the event supports the root and the root is the event's reason.
- **Not the same as.** Each other, truth (`defender`), or modal sufficiency.
- **Structural properties.** None is assumed of `supports`. Well-foundedness is a named
  member of the regress core, deleted by a circle and by an endless chain.
- **Generalization.** No move from ordinary inquiry to a metaphysical law is made except
  through a bridge listed as a hypothesis.
- **Competing accounts.** A support structure with an unsupported base, a circle and an
  endless chain each have a model. The restricted critic and the global-defeat defender each
  keep a consistent position under this test. Probabilistic support has no row.

**Argument reconstructed, with its disputed assumptions retained.** In §1,
pp.65–67, she distinguishes general PSR, contingent-truth PSR, and PSR for
nonanalytic necessary truths. Her inductive argument starts with an allegedly
indispensable assumption of explanation in some justified theory choice and
extends it to a relevantly similar class. Footnote 8 distinguishes similarity
in grounds for having an explanation from already having the same explained
status. Treating similarity as identity of explanatory status would build in
the disputed extension.

In §2, pp.68–76, she contrasts rejection of an unexplained asymmetric fission
outcome with acceptance of an unexplained composition fact when alternative
answers to the Problem of the Many have unacceptable theoretical costs. This
is a proposed comparison of justified responses, not an undisputed metaphysical
counterexample. The dilemma depends on those responses and their relevant
similarity. She records objections based on other solutions to the puzzles,
transworld identity/counterpart assumptions, and differences in modal status
(pp.73–75). Her contingency arguments are not defended against every such
objection. These premises must remain open if formalized.

**Crucial scope control.** T2, the contingent composition fact, can be explained
by a composition rule plus actual circumstances. T3 is the residual selection
under the full relevant circumstances, arrangements, histories and other
nonhaecceitistic features (§2, pp.70–72, especially n.18). T3's necessity or
contingency is disputed. Replacing T3 with T2 would turn a proposed unexplained
rule/selection into an ordinary contingent explanandum and overstate the case
against contingent PSR. Failure of the inductive defense is explicitly
compatible with endorsing contingent PSR (p.68).

**Positive rival and its own debt.** In §3, pp.76–79, EP treats explanatory
power as a truth-tracking theoretical virtue: other things equal, greater
explanatory power favors a theory. Other costs can override it, analogously to
ontological parsimony. This explains how differing choices might be justified
without a universal ontic coverage principle. It is not an unconditional rule
that the most explanatory theory is true. Kleinschmidt acknowledges that the
justification of EP itself remains to be explained (p.78), notes induction
worries without resolving them, and expressly denies having shown that no PSR
is true or that existence has no explanation (pp.78–79). Thus neither EP's
truth-tracking nor an actual brute concrete foundation is established here.

survives her objection. To defend EP, the rival owes a truth-tracking account
and a principled account of overriding costs. Neither side may define its
preferred explanation standard into victory. Even successful fact-level
coverage would leave the concrete-support and actual-grounding foundation
obligations in the integration note open. No root premise is discharged, no

### Fix the target and distinguish the demands

1. E: Every actual contingent concrete individual has a genuine actual

   accepted facts are subject to the demand. `WhyHonorsNecessitating`

   It leaves the scope of `requiresWhy` open and does not require
   acceptance of the explaining fact or factivity of `explains`.

Classically, the negation of E is that some actual contingent concrete being
lacks an adequate actual existential reason, not that no being has one.
The negation of D is that some actual contingent concrete being lacks an
adequate determining reason; it can still have a nonnecessitating reason.
Neither negation asserts that the exception is a foundation. At the weaker
token level, the negation of 608a262d is the existence of an actual
contingent being with no reason token. For that same being `b36104ba` also
requires absence of a distinct esse-cause. Its quantifier ranges over beings,
so applying this rival to E or D also requires a concrete witness and an
interpretation connecting adequate reasons to represented tokens.
The local demand does not by itself settle a necessary modal law or a
possible exception in a nonactual world.

### The two jobs of an exemption

There are two questions a proponent of a restricted PSR must not conflate.
What makes an exemption from the rule reasonable? And what, if anything,
accounts for this exempt existent's actuality? A feature can do the first
job without doing the second. A necessary rule requiring some terminus
leaves its occupant open when different occupants can meet the rule.
Conversely, lack of an existential explanation does not prove the
rule distinguishing explananda was randomly selected.

**Role versus occupant test.** Suppose a candidate law of explanation says
that each received existence has a source and some basic bearer supplies
all actual received existence. Grant its warrant, not merely its
consistency. If n alone supplies that role in the actual history and
another alone supplies it in an accessible history satisfying the same
basis, that basis leaves open who supplies the role. The other history
can still contain n as a dependent. To defeat the proposed basis for
**n's existence**, exhibit an accessible history where the entire basis
holds and the same n is absent. Contingency supplies an absence witness;
it does not guarantee that the proposed basis holds there. Conversely,
if the basis actually obtains, explains n's existence, and excludes
every such absence while it obtains, it meets the determining test for
n. A contingent basis could do this for contingent n. The comparison
leaves the admissibility of the proposed histories to be defended.

| Proposed exemption | Positive warrant for its restriction | Remaining explanatory cost and test |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Existentially derivative versus underived | Receipt is a positive dependence relation: explaining the received act by its supplier fits the very explanandum. A thing lacking such dependence does not need that kind of supplier. | Defends a restriction of *giving*, not a general exemption from ontic reasons. Ask whether an intrinsic or noncausal reason could apply; if none, the contrast between actuality and absence is still left open. A criterion 'has no reason' merely relabels the exception. |
| Temporal initial state versus later event | Initial-value explanations legitimately stop a derivation at its input; independently warranted temporal firstness excludes an earlier event. | Why these boundary data or this bearer exists is not answered by temporal ordering. An initial condition may be a derivative existent or have a simultaneous source. The distinction is relevant to earlier-event explanation only. |
| Basic whole versus constituents | A constitutive account can make the whole primary and explain parts by the whole; compositional constraints can genuinely exclude arbitrary independent variations of constituents. | This requires actual whole-to-part dependence, not a label 'whole'. If the whole could be absent, its contingent existence is still the target. Member explanations do not automatically explain the whole, nor does the whole explain its own existence by having parts. |
| Necessary versus contingent | If a nature genuinely entails the existence of the very individual, the absence contrast is ruled out without a prior producer; see the intrinsic-reason investigation. | Necessary status alone is not a positive nature account. Applies symmetrically to a necessary physical base or a necessary personal source. Does not justify exempting a *contingent* foundation. |
| Independently identified fundamental kind or law | A substantive structural or essential constraint can exclude certain kinds from being derivative or from existing uncaused (e.g. a relational role that entails dependence). A theory may have explanatory success and constrained admissible histories even when its initial datum is brute. | The constraint must bear on the bearer's existence, not only its operation or our evidence. If it selects an actual contingent law rather than its rival, ask the same existential question about any contingent *bearer* of that law; broader factual coverage is an additional premise. A law whose admissible models include the foundation's absence is not a determining reason for that foundation merely by being true or useful. |
| Primitive restricted demand | Direct warrant can be legitimate: one may take dependence rather than contingency to trigger a requirement, and give reasons for believing that restriction without requiring further ontic explanation of every fact. This is consistent with the #756 critic. | Merely choosing the actual foundation as exempt supplies no discriminatory warrant. Primitive does not mean false, but the policy must disclose what its direct warrant is and what would count against it. A primitive rule can constrain other facts, but cannot thereby account for an independently contingent exemption or occupant. |

### Can the exemption remain local? The relocation and closure tests

The verified feature-relative parity result `6e7f5afd` and its symmetric
sibling `8fba6dd2` concern demands for some reason. For the proposed
determining version, suppose the stance accepts f and requires a
determining reason for g. If its demand at that **same quality** is
uniform in feature k, agreement of f and g on k transfers the demand
to f. By `WhyHonorsNecessitating`, meeting it at w gives a fact r with
`explains w r f` such that at every accessible world where r holds,
f holds. The actual nonexistence of any such r contradicts this bundle.
Factivity is a separate condition for r to be true at w, and E's
relevance and direction conditions still need warrant. A stance could
mistakenly believe no such r exists. The encoded `accepts` relation
supplies no logical closure or guarantee of truth.

Relocation test: take the warrant offered for demanding an explanation of
one candidate while exempting another. Preserve all the properties that
the warrant actually uses and move the unreasoned actuality to the other
candidate in the *comparison*, not necessarily in reality. If the warrant
then yields the same classification but the verdict is changed, the
offered warrant did not explain the verdict. This is a test of argumentative
adequacy, not a license for arbitrary metaphysical recombination. A
kind-specific relation genuinely bearing on dependence can block the
comparison. Bare designation as 'the actual foundation' cannot determine
its own actual existence. There is no requirement to prove the relevance
of a feature by endlessly earlier reasons: direct grasp of dependence,
observed causal patterns, constitutive relations and coherence can all
supply relevant warrant.

**A further contingent agent.** A policy requiring reasons only for
derivative beings can explain why a generated agent needs a source.
Taken alone it permits designating an additional agent as basic and
leaves open whether that agent can appear at a particular place and
time. A stronger rival gives a complete inventory of the initial
bearers and a rule by which every later bearer either persists or
arises from specified antecedents. Holding that inventory and rule
fixed excludes a later, newly appearing agent with no antecedent.
Recorded persistence and independently traceable production can
support such a rule for the kinds examined. Extending it to every
agent requires warrant that the inventory and production alternatives
cover every eligible bearer. Equations for the behaviour of the
inventoried beings alone leave an extra, unrepresented bearer untested.
An agent already in the initial inventory raises the separate question
why this inventory obtains. These tests apply equally to an additional
impersonal bearer. Agency by itself settles neither question.

The inference from **one** brute contingent foundation to the possibility
of arbitrary brute contingencies elsewhere is invalid without a further
modal extension principle. A restricted rival can posit a basic state,
a fixed lawful development and an invariant restriction that no derivative
existence is brute. In a mathematical description, start with a binary
initial value and a deterministic recurrence that copies that value at
each subsequent step. The two solutions differ at every step; there is
no solution in which a later value arbitrarily changes with the initial
value and recurrence fixed. The recurrence supplies a constraint on the
history but does not determine the initial bit. This is a control for the
*logical* claim that a primitive input entails unconstrained outputs, not
a model of actual existential giving or evidence that brute concrete
foundations are possible. On the rival's interpretation the same
restriction can be motivated by successful prediction and by a
dependence-only explanatory policy. Neither success nor consistency
explains why the actual initial input obtains. Permitting the one
exception forfeits contingency alone as a universally sufficient trigger
of the determining demand; requiring a reason elsewhere now needs a
different or additional ground.

**Self-application.** Ask whether the rule 'only
derivative existence requires an explanation' is itself (a) a belief
accepted with epistemic warrant, (b) an actually obtaining contingent
fact, and (c) an actual contingent concrete existent. These are
different types. A proponent can defend the belief directly while the
ontic existence principle does not even quantify over the belief's truth.
If the rule itself is contingently true, its own truth or obtaining
raises the analogous question *under the broader contingent-fact PSR*,
not automatically under E or D for concrete individuals. If a contingent
law-bearer is invoked as an existential reason for other bearers, that
bearer falls within E/D's scope if concrete and actual. If exempt, the
view has not contradicted itself merely by declining the broader rule;
it has located a further stopping point. If it invokes a second
contingent selector to answer which policy or foundation obtains, apply
the same test to that selector at its proper type. A necessary selector
might constrain the admitted options without selecting their actuality.
A contingent selector can determine the target while leaving its own
existence or obtaining open. The question recurs exactly where the scope
includes it, not by a universal metalinguistic regress.

For a proposed global constraint, ask for a restriction specifiable
before naming the disputed agent. Use the same inventory, generation
rules and scope to evaluate both a history the rival admits and the
proposed insertion. A violation locates the exclusion and gives
the restriction content beyond the designation
“brute.” The second test is whether the offered basis explains **why
the restriction governs these bearers at all**. In the existing
`LawActuality.omissions` control, each of two formal laws retains a
unique trace even in `emptyRealization`, where no state-bearer is
present. Solving the rule therefore supplies no instantiation premise
in that semantics. An actual constitutive structure whose nature
entails the restriction could supply one if its instantiation and
explanatory direction have independent warrant. Evidence from the
bearers' behaviour can support belief in that structure. It supplies a
determining explanation of its obtaining only if it also explains that
obtaining and excludes accessible alternatives where the evidence
holds and the structure fails to obtain. A primitive policy can
instead stop here if its scope allows, retaining its local exclusions
while leaving this further fact unexplained. The same test applies to a necessary
personal source or a physical nature invoked to select the actual
order. [Law actuality and its omission controls](law-actuality-743.md#omission-and-positive-controls)

### What a nonnecessitating explanation achieves

The #755 `ground true` control separates factive admitted explanation
from modal necessitation. In #942, `ProductiveAxis` tests #735 Supply
at target 2 in the `overlay` examples. `AgencyBoundary` uses a
different frame, `acting`, where the same structural choice marks are
compatible with either value of a supplied personal flag. These results
leave open whether productive supply and substantive agency describe
the same bearer and exercise.

Take a selection process with independently identified mechanism and
an actual exercise which produces one of two possible outputs. The
mechanism and its exercise can explain *how* this output was produced,
even if their specified prior conditions admit both outcomes. Distinguish
three questions: why there is a product at all, why this very product
exists, and why these conditions gave this product rather than the
other. A productive process may answer the first two by actual
production and individuation; a probability or motive may even favor
the actual outcome. It need not settle the third as a fully
determining contrast. Merely citing the actual output as the selector
fails to supply new explanatory content. Adding an actual act of
selection need not be circular: a free decision or a stochastic event
can be genuinely distinct from and explanatory of the product's
existence. But treating that act as determining the product relocates
the residual contrast to the act if its obtaining is contingent and
not itself determined. If its subject is a contingent concrete bearer,
the existential demand also applies to that subject. A contingent act
as such is a different explanandum unless broader fact/event coverage
is adopted. A causal explanation of the product is not automatically a
complete account of the act's obtaining.

This is positive warrant for E as a less demanding universal candidate:
we know explanatory mechanisms where producing the actual effect, not
deducing it from a complete earlier description, is the issue. It is
also a reason to preserve D as an explicit further commitment rather
than identify it with explanation. For D the decisive argument is
different: if an account claims to settle *why actuality rather than
absence*, and all its proposed determining conditions equally obtain
in an admitted absence alternative, the account is not determining.
It follows that such a claimed explanation is incomplete at that
standard. It does not follow that every actual existential contrast
has a determining answer. The additional universal demand is a
substantive metaphysical commitment, not the definition of an
explanation.

A full necessitating fact PSR plus the other members of the #755
collapse core is inconsistent with contingency. That result cannot
be applied to D alone: D quantifies over concrete existents, the core
needs the aggregate of contingent truths, distribution, factivity,
contingent irreflexivity and the specified coverage and necessitation.
A necessary reason cannot necessitate a contingent explanandum in the
same accessible scope. A determining basis for a contingent individual
can therefore include a contingent distinguishing condition; its
ultimate adequacy is a further question. Both theistic free creation
and impersonal indeterministic production must face the same
determination test. A nonnecessitating answer for a derivative
creature is not by itself an explanation of an underived n.

## Containment of evidence and adaptive calibration (2026-11-24)

This checkpoint integrates the boundary assessment above with #1904's
[continuation comment, especially sections 4–5](https://github.com/moritzbrantner/formal-logos/pull/1904#issuecomment-7113063138).
That comment already proposed future experimental indistinguishability and a
fallible-warrant comparison. The contribution here is a canonical Lean
formalization of its **deterministic subcase**, a relation-based sufficient
condition that permits different state representations, and an explicit
control showing how a history-sensitive operation defeats the construction.
The comment's stochastic-kernel argument and replacement-mixture calculations
are not certified by these theorems. No numerical probability is assigned to a
worldview, a past event or a corpus premise.

The graph destination remains local existential explanation
`608a262d-08fe-4dfd-9413-67e2ecf35b84`, assessed through the adequacy of rival
accounts of historical evidence. The same-individual necessary-concrete target
`b19ffd92-01bd-5c0b-9643-e7407137ea5e` and the existing B2 consumer remain

### What future experiments can and cannot repair

An experiment has a current state, a readout and a transition for each allowed
action. A policy selects its next action from its entire observation record;
it can therefore change its plan after each response. `StableMatching` says
that related states have equal readouts and remain related after every same
action. It is a condition on the complete selected experimental interface.
It is stronger than the claim that the two instruments currently display the
same number. Equality of the complete relevant state and the same readout and
transition functions is one way to meet it; two differently represented states
may also meet it through a relation.

`adaptiveAgreement` (result `83edc309-1c34-402d-9394-4d095218244a`) proves that
related states yield identical transcripts under every finite adaptive policy.
At each step the earlier observations agree, so the policy chooses the same
action; transition preservation gives related successor states and hence the
same next observation. This is a conditional induction over concrete operations.
It does not infer global historical identity, stochastic distribution equality,
or metaphysical possibility from a matching readout. Every quantified run is
finite; no completed infinite experiment or countable-recombination principle
is assumed.

`historyControls` (result `fe46786d-f478-4c90-a9c4-e9f31e00d6c9`) fixes two
Boolean coordinates. One is a stipulated truth marker for a past event; the
other is a mutable visible reading. The blind interface can repeatedly retain
or toggle the reading but cannot consult the historical marker. Opposite
markers with the same visible coordinate remain indistinguishable through
every adaptive run. The existing
`CorrectionCriterion.characterization` (`0590fe21-edb9-5915-9837-62560c8f40e2`)
therefore excludes an exact classifier for that marker from any such transcript.
The interface is not observationally inert: repeated toggles produce the
three-reading sequence false, true, false. The positive control supplies an
audit action that copies the historical marker into the reading. Both states
still have the same initial display, but one audit distinguishes them and
admits an exact classifier on the entire two-bit state space.

These are **structural surrogates**. The past is a truth marker, not a model of
the events, memory mechanisms, causal ancestry or numerical identities that
an actual historical interpretation requires. The absence of historical
coupling is part of the blind interface's definition, and the positive control
removes it. The theorem neither establishes that real history is inaccessible
nor supplies a physical past-reading instrument. It exposes a sufficient
operational premise for a claim that a proposed counterfeit survives *all*
future calibration. Defending this premise requires coverage of every relevant
intervention, including any real history-sensitive channel; the theorem does
not exclude other ways of establishing indistinguishability.

There is a useful matched-scope consequence. If an old and a proposed young
history satisfy this operational comparison, new experimental events may
really occur in both and validate the same local future predictions. Those
experiments nevertheless add no transcript distinction about the disputed old
event. Historical truth, current experimental performance and future stability
are separate targets. Failure to recover a particular old event does not
undermine logic or all present science. Conversely, stable future prediction
does not certify that the apparent earlier calibration actually happened.

### Three different kinds of containment

The following distinction sharpens the proposed dilemma without changing the
epistemic standard halfway through the comparison.

| Claim being defended | What must be supplied | What a primitive commitment can legitimately do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The unwanted history or intervention is metaphysically excluded | A justified restriction with the relevant global scope; the positive-control action tests a claimed experimental exclusion | A necessary constitutive constraint may terminate this question without an earlier cause. Its content and applicability still need warrant. |
| The unwanted history is admitted but does not defeat fallible confidence | The account's actual entitlement standard and the feature that limits the epistemic relevance of the rival | Prima facie memory entitlement, a warranted model class or a justified inductive policy may be basic. Their mere classification as primitive is no refutation. |
| The actual method is reliable | An actually truth-connected process, or other conditions the selected externalist account requires | An actually reliable process can confer warrant without an observer being able to rule out every duplicate from the inside. Its existence does not by itself explain the ultimate order. |

Metaphysical admission alone supplies no probability, no adverse likelihood
and no evidence that a particular method is unreliable. This limitation also
applies to the theist's comparison. For a rival that *already adopts* a
Bayesian standard, existing `ContrastiveWorldview.updateConstraints` says
that equal positive likelihoods preserve the prior. No equal prior is
required. For an account that represents calibration as the shared-plus-local
profile of `PrefaceConnectivity.crossCheckBoundary` (`4a85e1e3`), checks blind
to the shared condition cannot improve its support; even perfect local checks
leave that shared condition's probability unchanged. Neither representation
is imposed on ordinary inquiry. Their interpretations require evidence that
the alleged alternative actually matches the observations or that the actual
checks are blind to the alleged common failure. The matching theorem supplies
only the specified deterministic transcript equality.

The narrower self-application argument is consequently about a **particular
route to warrant**. If a worldview relies exclusively on historical evidence
that it concedes has no discriminatory force at its own required standard,
and offers no other entitlement for its historical conclusion, that route
cannot deliver the claimed warrant. This is not strict self-refutation: the
view might be true, possess another supporting route, adopt actual-process
reliabilism, or claim a weaker epistemic status. #1808 already treats an
inconsistent positive entitlement under an expressly strict norm; the present
comparison neither repeats that theorem nor silently demands that norm of a
fallibilist. Merely permitting a counterfeit or failing to refute one does
not satisfy the self-undermining antecedent.

### Where the restriction problem genuinely stops

A justification for excluding an added agent, a justification for believing
the exclusion, and an explanation of why the actual foundation exists are
different achievements. A restriction may succeed at the first two while
leaving the third open. This is explanatory incompleteness at the third
question, not proof that the first two achievements are illusory.

The strongest surviving rival to universal existential explanation in this
comparison is a **contingent primitive foundation with constitutive scope
restrictions and genuinely reliable secondary processes**. Its positive
content must say which kinds exist, which transitions their natures permit,
and why its claimed scope covers the putative interference. It may use direct,
empirical and cumulative warrant for those commitments. To exclude historical
counterfeit initialization, its constraints must concern admissible origins
or complete histories, not merely the subsequent operation of whatever
present state happens to exist. Alternatively it may admit that initialization
while defending fallible or externalist warrant without claiming empirical
discrimination of every admitted history.

This package is not established as the actual metaphysics, and the surrogate
does not certify all its commitments jointly. It survives the proposed
inference from a brute existence to universal arbitrariness because the
missing origin/recombination/epistemic bridges are substantive. Its residual
price is the expressly unexplained contingent actuality of the foundation and
whatever further obtaining it itself leaves primitive. A necessary restriction
is not automatically another unexplained *contingent* fact. The rule's own
truth enters the existential PSR only through an appropriate bearer or a
separately defended fact-level extension; the type distinction in the earlier
self-application test remains decisive.

The dilemma therefore has different defensible strengths. A restriction that
conflicts with a demand the rival applies uniformly to the same kind of fact
can yield internal inconsistency by the existing parity tests. A rule that
merely leaves actuality unexplained incurs a comparative explanatory cost
under an accepted demand for existential intelligibility. A view with no
defended restraint has not *established containment*. To infer that every
alternative is possible still requires positive admission or completeness
principles: absence of a known restriction is not unrestricted recombination.

### Symmetry, legitimate burdens and current assessment

An actual nonnecessitating productive explanation can explain a particular
effect even when it does not settle every contrast about the producer's
exercise. Both a genuine reason-guided free act and an impersonal stochastic
or dispositional process receive that credit at the same scope. A theist who
demands a determining explanation of every contingent fact must also apply
it to the creative exercise; necessity of the source does not settle that
question. An impersonal source's necessary nature can likewise supply real
constraints without establishing why one of its contingent effects obtains.
Metaphysical necessitarianism instead rejects the initial contingency at the
tested scope and must defend that denial; it is not a brute-exception model.

Classical theism gains a **substantive but conditional explanatory advantage**
over a wholly brute account of the same order if its source has an adequate
intrinsic existential explanation, actual reason-guided productive activity,
and appropriate truth-directed governance. These conditions supply specific
explanatory content. They cannot be replaced by the words necessary, personal
or good. Against a necessary impersonal source with warranted constraints and
reliable processes, the extra case concerns genuine rational authorship and
care for epistemic goods; no comparative defeat is delivered here. The
[practical authorship and epistemic-end tests](optimal-creation-choice.md#rational-authorship-versus-brute-selection-2026-11-23)
keep the corresponding interpretation and moral bridges open. Assertion-only
divine truthfulness is insufficient to exclude misleading nonasserted traces;
the scope of a no-systematic-frustration norm and any overriding reasons must
be independently assessed.

Legitimate burdens shift at three specific points. A rival facing a supported
same-feature parity argument must identify a relevant difference or reject
the demand, rather than merely designate its preferred exception. A rival
claiming universal empirical containment must answer an exhibited admissible
intervention that violates it, or reject the stated admissibility/coupling
premise with reasons. A view claiming historical warrant from a particular
channel must meet its own standard when a warranted failure mechanism targets
that channel. None of these shifts transfers the initial burden of proving
unrestricted recombination, imposes an infinite justification regress, or
establishes theism by default.

**Overall provisional assessment.** Retain the earlier moderate preference
for genuine adequate existential explanation E over an unexplained contingent
exception, and suspension about universal determining explanation D. The
new formal contribution sharpens the epistemic application boundary and
integrates previously unverified discussion; it supplies no new warrant for
the brute-to-counterfeit bridge. Restricted bruteness remains undefeated by
the explosion argument, while unrestricted historical replacement combined
with an incompatible claimed epistemic guarantee remains subject to the
existing conditional objections. These results do not reduce the value of
ordinary historical inquiry or establish a probability for its failure.

**Next discriminating work.** The universal adaptive question is answered
within the specified deterministic interface. The remaining application
questions include a positive account making the putative full replacement
history admissible with explicit identities, provenance and operational states
that preserve the commitments actually required by the selected interpretation.
A qualitative replacement may differ numerically and in historical origin;
it need not preserve the actual world's provenance unless that is a commitment
of the particular interpretation being tested. Other tests are a justified
history-sensitive constraint or intervention that breaks the supplied matching,
or a warrant account that survives without such discrimination. For theism, test
the no-systematic-frustration norm on the same evidence domain and source,
retaining the distinction between moral goodness and bare goodness of being.
For the restricted brute rival, defend the scope of its constitutive inventory

#### What the product bypass does and does not answer

The output bypass is a substantive limit on the original objection.
Every actual record supplying a still needs x, yet both have Supply,
because an independent actual source route first supplies x. The
two-sided obstruction fails at “every product supplier needs adoption.”
Consequently the inference from output-grounded adoption **alone** to
failed productive supply is invalid in this interpretation.

That does not establish the requested intentional explanation. A source
could actually produce x, after which an output-relative description of
the source is true. Such a description may be accurate without being the
practical specification under which the source acted. The productive
account can succeed while a proposed explanation by antecedent rational
guidance remains unsupported. Conversely, an exercise could be genuinely
guided by a specification even if the truth of the description “successful
producer of x” depends on x. The success-description and the operative
specification are different explanatory roles. The [independently
specified fulfillment test](optimal-creation-choice.md#12-the-practical-measure-test-a-stronger-candidate-than-output-matching)
therefore remains relevant after production is supplied.

The resulting test for the same n, a and x is more exact. Identify the
source's apprehended distributive specification independently of which
product happens to occur, and identify its actual, nondeviant role in
production. Then ask whether x is an indispensable input to **that role's
operation**, or merely to a true output-relative description. In the first
case, check every real product route for dependence on that role; the cut
applies only if both directions and the absence of independent exercise
are warranted. In the second case there is no cut merely from semantic
dependence, but the actual directive role still needs an account. No
invented choosing-to-choose event follows from either analysis.

The first-order existential scope remains fixed. In these controls n
and the recipient are beings; adoption is a role-event of n, not an
additional being. The existential-token PSR quantifies the former carrier.
A demand for a substantive explanation of the actual practical relation
therefore does not automatically add another existence-token obligation,
or require an earlier choosing event. Extending E to every actual
relation or exercise is an additional explanatory-scope commitment.

#### Direct exercise, explanatory reasons and the alleged regress

`exerciseBasis` keeps the same source, events and records and permits a
itself as independently supplied. The product then has Supply. This is a
precise direct-exercise option, not a proof that the option is adequate.
The existing constructor name `Supply.intrinsic` means independently
supplied contribution in this selected explanatory context. It does not
mean a new metaphysical intrinsic accident of n, and it does not infer
necessary occurrence from necessary existence.

A regress of decisions requires an additional thesis: every actual
exercise must be put into exercise by a further distinct decision or
productive event. Neither the joint-supply rules nor an ordinary rational
reason imposes that thesis. Reasons can specify why an agent does
something and the content under which it does it; their explanatory role
need not be another efficient production of the will. This is an analyst
option already distinguished in the [necessary-act source investigation](selected-effect-bridge-462.md),
not a new historical attribution. An actual direct exercise can be offered
as a primitive, with explicit conditions and defeaters, rather than being
defined as a further event caused by an earlier decision.

This permission is symmetric. If a directly exercised personal source is
allowed to stop the productive regress, a necessary impersonal producer
may offer its direct exercise on the same terms. Conversely, if explaining
the actual exercise requires more than the primitive's content and the
offered reasons, both accounts must supply that explanation. Requiring a
further determining event only from the impersonal rival would not be a
discriminator. Requiring one from both is a stronger explanatory standard
whose warrant remains a substantive question, not a theorem of PSR's
existence-token formulation.
