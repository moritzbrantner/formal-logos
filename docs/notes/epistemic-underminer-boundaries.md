The general retorsion distinction is already explicit in
`TruthCore.Alternatives.Retorsion.PresuppositionLink`. A performance can refute
the denial of its own sort only if the token belongs to that sort. For example,
uttering the denial of warranted belief does not supply a warranted belief.
Likewise the production of an inferential token does not certify the token's
reliability or the validity of its inference. A reflective concession can be
tested where the relevant status is actually claimed and warranted.

For the warrant and reliability bridges, a discriminating comparison is an
otherwise comparable competent count versus an attractive but uninformative
prompt, as in the [F6 investigation](epistemic-reasons-preconditions.md).
Sufficient evidence bearing on the answer supplies a candidate for warrant.
The constant-answer model explains why high observed accuracy alone leaves the
connection open. For demonstrative knowledge, a further test is to specify
what certifies an independently known premise or how a nonterminating chain
confers warrant, then check that account against the same dependency and
knowledge predicates. See the existing [justification introspection
investigation](carneades-epistemology-introspection.md) for the separate
question of knowing one's justification and [the Wittgenstein note](../wittgenstein.md)
for the hinge readings.

The narrow incompatibilities stand within their respective profiles. The
stronger claim that these profiles establish actual justification, global
method reliability, categorical standards, or a warrant-bearing hinge remains
open. The strongest rival depends on the scope. It is a reasonless truth for
case 9 without F6, thin pro tanto reasons without warranted beliefs for case
10, aim-relative merit for case 13, strong infinitism for case 15, and
suspension without active universal doubt for case 16.

## Rational basing: the particular transition must exercise the capacity

Research continuation, 2026-11-14, against main
`561ba7d5c774d15036128928b7293b0ab9ee137a`. This is an analyst argument and a
proposed application criterion, awaiting independent semantic challenge. It
introduces no corpus definition, registered theorem or source claim. The question
is what makes a particular belief formed **because of the content of its
premises**, rather than merely accompany relevant evidence. The scope is ordinary
reflective inference in an agent who can understand and reconsider a short
argument. A sufficient account for that scope need not classify every infant,
animal, perceptual belief or unconscious computation.

The two open targets remain
[`SomeEligibleReasonUptake`](../../corpus/statements/38eb1610-1b62-4fbe-9d7c-21dc2bf29fa7.json)
and
[`EligibleReasonUptakeWarrants`](../../corpus/statements/cf57c52c-c519-49c0-9536-d5fa8a9c9bac.json).
The existing
[`eligibleReasonUptakeYieldsWarrant`](../../corpus/results/1db47fd9-6ab1-4fa1-a9dd-0aa9457d8146.json)
consumes **both**. Its `Eligible` parameter bundles basing, adequacy and
undefeatedness. Specifying basing supplies neither the other conditions nor the
calibration to `Warrant`. Current `related`, `context` and dossiers show no
separate derivation of either input and no downstream root argument using this
result. This investigation develops its applicability and the proposed
#930–#943–#945 connection; it creates no new formal root edge.

### The positive accounts and the live disagreement

The following are consulted primary-source findings, with attribution to formal
corpus propositions deferred:

- **Primitive rational rule-following.** Boghossian, [*What is
  inference?*](https://paulboghossian.com/pb/files/WhatIsInference.pdf), pp.3–5,
  11–17, distinguishes inference from indirect causal chains and from direct,
  habitual association. His positive proposal treats accepted rules as guiding
  thought and ultimately allows rule-following to be primitive. Requiring every
  application to be another inference generates the regress. He explicitly
  acknowledges that a taking attitude still needs the right causal role
  (p.5 n.2). His conclusion about naturalism is a proposed challenge, not a
  demonstrated exclusion of every physical realization (pp.17–18).
- **Active treatment without an obligatory support-belief.** McHugh and Way,
  [*Against the Taking Condition*](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/phis.12074),
  §§2–3, 4.3–4.4 and 5, distinguish treating premises as supporting a conclusion
  from representing their support in an additional attitude. They retain the
  need to explain active inference and its normative commitments. Their regress
  objection targets requiring prior reasoning to activate each instance; it does
  not show that ordinary inferential agency is impossible. Their paper expressly
  leaves the full positive account for later work. Its objections therefore do
  not by themselves complete a causal theory of basing.
- **Exercise of cognitive capacities.** Turri, [*Believing For a
  Reason*](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10670-011-9271-5), abstract
  and publisher-displayed notes 2–4 and 18–22, connects his nondeviant causal
  proposal with manifestation. Note 21 gives a useful general distinction:
  exercise of a capacity somewhere in an outcome's ancestry need not make that
  outcome an exercise of the capacity. Notes 2–4 preserve complications about
  overdetermination and difference-making. Only the abstract and those notes
  were accessible here; the full paper was not read, so its complete analysis
  and replies are not attributed or assessed as established.
- **A positive functional successor.** The authors' [abstract of *What is
  Reasoning?*](https://eprints.soton.ac.uk/402250/) identifies reasoning as a
  functional kind whose aim of fitting attitudes fixes standards for doing it
  well. This supplies a relevant positive research lead. The full manuscript
  could not be obtained in this run; the construction below is our proposal,
  not a reconstruction claimed to reproduce their account.

These positions focus the disagreement. A rule may be a primitive rational
form of an act, or its application may be realized through a suitably organized
capacity. Both can require actual content-guided activity. An account that merely
renames a disposition "rational" explains no particular transition. Conversely,
requiring a separate inference to authorize every implementation step builds in
the regress before testing the capacity account.

### Proposed sufficient account for the selected scope

Let an agent consider premises Γ and conclude q by a particular procedure R.
Here Γ contains the **considerations used**, which may be misleading; calling
them premises does not already grant objective epistemic support. The proposed
basing account has four jointly required conditions:

1. **Content is available to this agent.** The agent accepts the relevant
   premises with their selected meanings and can distinguish those meanings
   from the inscription, sound or feeling that carries them. This is a modest
   local understanding condition, not mastery of every consequence. Content
   identity and truth conditions must be supplied independently of the
   correctness of this particular output. The [#943 semantics](intentionality-943.md)
   provides an existing interface; its actual application remains a burden.
2. **The transition has an intelligible procedure.** R uses the roles of the
   premises to address whether q: for example, it matches an accepted antecedent
   with the antecedent of an accepted conditional and carries that conditional's
   consequent forward. The person can exercise this procedure in suitable
   nearby cases and discriminate a relevant mismatch. Competence at executing
   a procedure is separate from whether the procedure is epistemically good.
   Someone can faithfully apply a bad inferential policy.
3. **This token transition exercises that procedure.** The actual production
   or maintenance of the belief includes the content-processing route just
   described. The relevant premises enter its selection and transition stages,
   and the stage's conclusion contributes to the resulting belief. Possessing
   the capacity, rehearsing its premises, or constructing a matching explanation
   after believing does not satisfy this condition. Neither does a causal
   chain that exits the reasoning process and supplies a new perceptual basis.
4. **The route belongs to the person's reflective activity.** The agent can
   attend to the argument, use its contents in related judgments, and reconsider
   the relevant contribution when a premise is withdrawn or a recognized
   objection undermines it. In the selected reflective case these are evidence
   of uptake and control, not a requirement to verbalize a theory of inference.
   Actual bypass, a closed review channel or overriding replacement prevents
   this sufficient test from licensing the attribution. No premise requires
   control over the complete past or
   authorship of the capacities used.

"Contributes" in condition 3 is a process claim, not unconditional dependence
of the final belief on one input. A belief can have two actually used sufficient
bases. Removing one may remove that contribution while the other sustains the
same conclusion. The criterion should therefore inspect the active route and
its relation to the belief, allowing simultaneous bases. It must distinguish
an active redundant contribution from a merely available backup that never
operates. This is a remaining causal interpretation task when applying the
criterion to real cognition, not something the word "contributes" proves.

The four conditions explain **basing on considerations**. For eligible epistemic
uptake add: those considerations actually favor q with sufficient strength;
the inference uses an adequate rule in its domain; and no applicable undefeated
undercutter or rebutter defeats the claimed warrant. An understood, deliberately
applied invalid rule can satisfy the basing conditions while failing adequacy.
Likewise, sound processing of fabricated testimony may fail the evidence
condition. These are determinate failure locations within the existing
eligibility bundle.

### Why this is a positive explanation rather than another label

The explanatory argument has three parts. First, the premises' semantic roles
specify what is processed: which assertion is the antecedent, which conditional
connects it to the proposed conclusion, and what would count as a mismatch.
Second, the actual transition uses those roles through an identifiable sequence
of operations. Third, the person's wider uptake explains why this sequence is
an exercise of their inferential capacity in the case under consideration.
Together these explain the particular move from these premises to this
conclusion. They predict different behavior and different active routes when
premise identity, acceptance or the available transition is changed.

These conditions can be investigated without first deciding that the conclusion
is true. For example, one can identify an antecedent-matching operation in a
reasoner who is applying the same procedure to a false premise. Independent
assessment of validity and premise warrant then asks whether that exercise is
epistemically adequate. This separation prevents success from defining the
capacity and prevents a correct answer from certifying its exercise.

For token mediation, distinguish two explicitly specified mechanisms. In the
first, the result of the premise-processing stage is routed into the conclusion.
In the second, that stage runs but a separate cue supplies the conclusion. The
cue may agree with the calculation on every observed trial. An intervention
on the calculated recommendation, with the cue and other inputs fixed, then
discriminates the routes inside this mechanism. The gate/override analysis in
`RevisionUptake` already gives that structural test. This supplies a test of
actual mediation, beyond merely checking whether the subject has a suitable
disposition. Identifying such independently intervenable stages in a real
person requires evidence; a retrospective explanation alone cannot identify
them. Later genuine reconsideration may also change a belief's sustaining basis,
so the initial route does not determine all subsequent basing forever.

For a simple modus ponens case, the reason for treating the transition as
adequate is also explicit: no valuation makes both p and p→q true while q is
false. That content relation supplies a correctness standard for the operation.
The transition's realization explains why the agent reaches q; the content
relation explains why that transition is truth-preserving. Neither explanation
alone supplies both. A capacity account proposes that one organized process
can satisfy both explanations, with the agent using the relevant contents.

The account does not require an additional premise "these premises support q"
at every application. Basic matching and transition operations execute without
another inferential endorsement of their execution. This gives a concrete
stopping point for the implementation regress. A reflective judgment about the
rule can still guide scrutiny of it, and an irreducible intellectual apprehension
remains a competing account of personal understanding. The proposed stopping
point is not a reduction of understanding or of normative force to machinery.

### A small construction that can fail

Use three distinct atomic contents, with all eight Boolean valuations. A
conditional token carries an ordered pair `(antecedent, consequent)`. An
accepted atomic token and an accepted conditional generate a recommendation
only if their antecedent identities match:

```python
def recommendation(atom, antecedent, consequent,
                   atom_accepted, conditional_accepted):
    if atom_accepted and conditional_accepted and atom == antecedent:
        return consequent
    return None
```

`None` means this route supplies no recommendation; it does not mean the
conclusion is false or ought to be rejected. The two acceptance flags are
attitudes in the proposed interpretation, not truth values of the accepted
contents. The finite interpretation supplies content and conditional truth
separately: the conditional is true exactly when its antecedent is false or
its consequent true. This is a selected material-conditional profile, not an
analysis of every ordinary use of "if". The algorithm's antecedent match and
consequent selection give
its operations determinate roles under that interpretation.

The finite diagnostic has 108 input cases (three choices for each content and
two values for each acceptance flag). Enumerating the eight valuations for
each case tests 864 input/valuation combinations: whenever it returns a
content and its accepted premises are true, that content is true. Enumerating
the six permutations of the atoms tests 648 input/renaming combinations:
renaming the inputs renames the recommendation, including preservation of
absence. These are finite executable checks, not a new Lean certificate or
proof of unbounded understanding. Replacing the selected consequent with a
fixed unrelated atom, or removing the antecedent comparison, yields specific
truth-preservation failures. With atoms numbered 0–2 and valuation
`(true, false, false)`, the first mutation sends accepted `0` and `0→0` to
false atom `2`; the second sends accepted `0` and `1→1` to false atom `1`.
The original function passes all 864 valuation and 648 renaming checks; both
mutation counterexamples were evaluated on 2026-11-14. The counts describe
this declared finite domain only.

To model uptake of this recommendation, reuse the existing
[`RevisionUptake.revise`](../../TruthCore/Modal/RevisionUptake.lean). For each q,
let `recommendation == q` be the proposed new value of a **contribution slot**;
feed it to `revise` with the old slot, gate and override. This slot tracks
whether this modeled route recommends q. It is not the agent's whole belief
or its warrant. The existing `screening` theorem already says that, with gate
open and no override, the new value is the recommendation independently of
the old acquisition. No duplicate screening theorem is needed. The
[`controls`](../../TruthCore/Results/Modal/RevisionUptake/Solution.lean)
already show why sensitive inversion, echo and noise do not establish
correctness or competent uptake.

For a second independent active support route, a simple aggregate can retain
q when either contribution slot is true. With both true, removing the first
leaves q accepted but removes that route's contribution. This is an exact
structural explanation of why a demand that every reason be indispensable to
the final answer is too strong for this model. It does not certify an arbitrary
human causal attribution or the rational aggregation of conflicting reasons.

This finite computation can be implemented by deterministic registers,
comparators and gates. It is a positive physically realizable case of applying
the specified formal rule under a supplied interpretation, including its
matching and omission behavior. The corresponding positive physicalist proposal
is that a conscious person's activity realizes these operations while satisfying
the four conditions. Whether a particular
physical system possesses the relevant content and personal understanding is
a further question. The circuit demonstrates neither consciousness nor the
complete naturalization of those properties; it prevents simple determinism or
physical implementation from being treated as the failure mechanism.

The bypass and two-basis descriptions are **specified comparison cases**, not
reports of experimental discoveries. Their force is conditional: they identify

bare suggestion that a mysterious alternative might exist.

### A provisional warrant judgment and its limits

For ordinary short reflective inferences, **actual exercise of a content-sensitive
capacity is provisionally favored as the working basing account**. Its grounds
are the intelligibility of familiar premise-to-conclusion activity, the
specific operations that preserve the inferential relation, and the contrastive
explanation of bypass, mismatch and correction. An agent's evident grasp and
use of a simple argument can provide prima facie support for an actual basing
attribution. It need not await a complete theory of mentality or proof of every
background assumption. A merely conceivable hidden bypass is not evidence
against an otherwise well-supported attribution.

This judgment favors the specified exercise conditions over bare behavioral
reliability, mere causal ancestry and retrospective endorsement. It does not
settle whether the personal exercise is reducible. A strong surviving rival
treats awareness of support as intrinsic to a primitive rational act. It explains
the personal unity of reasoning directly, but needs an account of how that act
is identified and how it produces or sustains the particular belief. The
capacity proposal owes a substantive account of content and of the connection
between processing and the agent's activity. The finite construction discharges
neither rival's complete metaphysical burden.

The accounts need not be exclusive: a primitive rational act could also satisfy
the proposed exercise conditions. The judgment favors those discriminators
over weaker basing tests; it does not rank a fully naturalized implementation
above an irreducible intellectual act when both preserve the same personal
content and activity. A reflective episode may also include a support-belief
without making one necessary for every inference.

The evidence supports a limited further positive argument: where the agent
understands and actually uses warranted premises through this valid procedure,
and the evidence is sufficient and undefeated, there is a candidate instance
for `SomeEligibleReasonUptake`. Applying the already registered warrant result
still requires the same separately justified eligibility-to-warrant calibration.
No new proof or floor marking follows from this provisional assessment. A
particular accepted basing account can strengthen the case for that instance
without establishing every instance or eliminating the direct prima facie route.

### Connections and the next discriminating step

- **#930 and #943:** rational basing adds actual use to the content and
  epistemic-support questions. The content interface fixes what is being
  considered; the basing account fixes its role in this inference; the warrant
  bridge assesses whether that use suffices. The existence of a semantic
  evaluation does not itself establish the person's understanding.
- **#945:** present reason response and acquisition history are insufficient
  without the actual uptake route. The existing screening result isolates the
  effect of later uptake; the proposed personal conditions give that surrogate
  a more precise application target. Deterministic acquisition can coexist with
  this kind of guidance. Whether historical manipulation invalidates ownership
  or basic desert is not decided by basing.
- **CC100 item 116 and intellectual operation:** the distinction between
  intentional content and effective mental activity sharpens the subject matter
  of `45d46cbf-0d36-5c3c-85bb-8685d895c2f1`. It does not yield the distinct
  personal-explanation-only restriction
  `05fb0e59-18f8-519d-bf58-12bdc952367c`. Both still lack Lean declarations and
  registered argument/result evidence at this baseline. A personal-level
  explanation of a person's inference does not imply that its realization or
  ultimate source must be an additional person. Connecting an actual episode
  to q.75's intellectual exercise and then to its bodily-sharing premise also
  remains explicit work.
- **#1459:** knowing a content and possessing a capacity do not yet show that
  the content guided a particular effect. That is a useful comparison for the
  proposed transition from divine knowledge of goods to actual willing. Applying
  it to divine action requires an appropriate account of intentional exercise;
  temporal deliberation, learned capacities and retraction tests from human
  inference cannot simply be transferred. Basing supplies no inference to
  recipient-directed creation or an ultimate personal source.

The next discriminating investigation should use a **matched inference and
bypass protocol**: fix premise content, conclusion, competence and observed
accuracy; establish which route actually operates; vary an antecedent mismatch,
an irrelevant presentation feature, and withdrawal of one of two active supports.
Assess whether the person's own correction and use of contents follow the
inferential route. An output transcript alone cannot decide this when the two
mechanisms produce the same answer. Process and intervention evidence must be
interpreted alongside evidence of understanding. A successful real case would
strengthen the application premise; demonstrated bypass or a failure of transfer
would weaken the particular attribution.

The present slice supplies a discriminating positive proposal and an executable
finite construction of its rule-processing
component. The remaining concrete blocker is evidence joining that component to
personal content and control in an actual case; the stipulated model cannot
provide such evidence. Formal certification of a larger construction is also
pending the unavailable Lean/verifier environment, and would still leave that
application burden. The ordinary prima facie case remains available with these
revision conditions. No current formal root premise is added, removed or
discharged by this note and its attempt record.
