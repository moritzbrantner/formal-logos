# What a finite thinker contributes to an idea of infinity

## The positive content test

A finite thinker can specify **all even natural numbers**. Start a switch in the accepting state and flip it at each mark of a unary numeral. Accept the input when the switch ends in the accepting state. Change only the final instruction to “always accept” and the specification picks out **all natural numbers**. For every proposed bound there is a greater length accepted by both rules whose successor only the second accepts. The two instructions therefore differ over arbitrarily large inputs even though “there is no last accepted input” describes both. The alternating rule conveys a positive structure of membership, including the predicted answer on a fresh input. The Lean companion `finiteRulesDiscriminateAboveEveryBound` checks that contrast. [Solution](../../TruthCore/Results/Causality/CartesianIdeaOfInfinite/Solution.lean)

Under the ordinary interpretation with all natural numbers as the domain, each extension is a determinate infinite set now. Testing particular numerals takes finite time and no run visits every member. The thinker can design the recognizer, choose an acceptance condition and explain why adding two marks preserves the answer. Changing that choice while keeping the carrier, marks, transition and conventions fixed changes *which* set is represented. That is evidence of a contribution to content beyond starting a token. It presupposes the meanings of number, iteration and general quantification. The formal model compares functions on a supplied domain. It does not demonstrate the psychological history of a particular episode, create the numbers, or establish a finite **total** cause of their intelligibility.

**Repeat the comparison at the proposed perfection.** Suppose a thinker has learned what it is for one subject to know a truth, including the difference between knowledge and a lucky true guess. Compare “for each truth, someone knows it” with “one and the same subject actually knows every truth.” Moving the subject quantifier changes the content. Distributed knowledge can satisfy the first condition while each subject misses something. A learner who could eventually settle each question can still lack present knowledge of a truth. Both contrasts survive when attention is directed to an actually unlimited knower. One can specify these application conditions, recognize mistakes about them and intend *one actual knower*, without knowing the answers oneself. This is a finite contribution to a positive intellectual aspect of the intended object. The content can be considered even when nothing satisfies it. It uses meaningful knowledge, actuality, unity and unrestricted quantification as inputs.

The parity check supplies an independently understood mechanism for a **partial** cause of discriminating content. The knowledge example applies composition to the closer subject matter. Neither example by itself explains how a thinker acquired all of those concepts, nor makes unlimited knowledge coextensive with the fullness of infinite substance (`INFINITE_3`). Even an ideal knower's power or goodness does not follow from the quantifier over truths. To extend the rival, a theorist could quantify over genuine pure perfections, preserve a single subject of all of them and exclude every respective limit. That proposal owes an interpretation of *pure perfection*, of unlimited realization for each kind, and of their joint unity. Defining the domain as whatever the infinite substance has would simply import the content to be explained. The comparison in this paragraph is philosophical, beyond the mathematical companion.

## What adequacy would explain

The strongest Cartesian reply can grant every verdict on the numeral and knower examples. A finite specification can differentiate extensions while relying on intelligibility it did not originate. The proposed experience is of a unified positive standard of perfection against which the meditator recognizes its own deficiency. Merely repeating a finite act yields a series of improvements and supplies no completed standard of adequacy. Treating the positive standard as given makes sense of the direction of comparison: recognizing an error as a lack of knowledge already involves a distinction between attaining truth and falling short. A demand for proportion between a total source and what it explains has prima facie force. It asks what gives the episode that particular intelligible character. The determination need not resemble its cause if the cause has the relevant perfection eminently.

This reason supports looking for an adequate explanation. Two further steps matter. A determinate *local* standard already permits recognition of error in one answer. The two-state case even fixes correct answers throughout its domain. To establish that recognition of a particular imperfection presupposes a positive grasp of **unlimited substantial perfection**, the proponent needs a feature of the judgment that these local or compositionally extended standards cannot supply. And explaining why a representation has a determinate correctness condition is a different demand from possessing the excellence that would satisfy that condition. The finite knower example shows how an input can make a difference to the former while the thinker acknowledges lacking the latter formally.

This bears directly on objective adequacy P3 (`3845020d`), which includes **every** cause of the content, even a partial one. If the meditator helps determine that its actual episode presents this perfection as unified rather than distributed, and if it lacks the perfection in the selected act sense, P3 rules its contribution out. The positive reason to take that challenge seriously is the exhibited content-sensitive operation. Its application to an irreducible first-person presentation of the whole of `INFINITE_3` remains open. On the stronger Cartesian reading, composition may only *articulate* a content whose grasp was supplied independently. P1 (`d3fcdeeb`) now requires evidence that this very episode presents that richer content. First-person occurrence supplies good warrant for there being an episode and for some discriminations in it. Neither fluent use of a term nor the mere name of an infinite referent settles the stronger P1.

P2 (`0747e4f3`) can retain direct explanatory warrant on either account. If the adequacy principle is restricted to an efficient **total** source, changing the output rule no longer directly challenges its possession of the perfection. Then coverage must supply a total source of this content. P2 supplies some contributor. A total source might incorporate antecedent concepts, other teachers or conventions. The worked example supplies no independent test showing that its total source is a single being with the represented perfection.

P4 (`4a9ede84`) concerns the meditator alone. Experienced ignorance supports lack of *formal* unlimited knowledge. Under `ActFrame`, inclusive possession is formal **or** virtual. Merely being able to describe an unlimited knower gives no evidence of an actual power to realize its knowledge in another. Conversely one's own presently missing an answer does not establish lack of all such eminent possession. A proponent may argue from the limits of the meditator's productive power, provided the relevant power is specified independently of its capacity to form the idea. The existing `GradesActIsInclusive` calibration takes IN_ACT_4 to the inclusive disjunction. With it, excluding **both** disjuncts suffices for P4. It supplies neither exclusion and does not identify the grades primitive with that disjunction. [Act calibrations](../../TruthCore/Causality/CartesianAdequacy.lean)

One proposed discriminator is that the subject comprehends each truth *through its own understanding* rather than receiving a correct verdict about each from a teacher. Hold the subject, actual scope, and truth conditions fixed. This introduces a real difference in the **ground of the knowledge**. But a learner already distinguishes understanding a proof from knowing its conclusion on trusted testimony. Quantifying that distinction over every truth again lets a finite thinker state why a wholly dependent knower misses the proposed condition. This further test favors the compositional account of that distinction. It still leaves open whether the meditator also experiences the many perfections as internally one, and whether that experience has determinate content beyond applying multiple predicates to one subject. Common reference by itself explains only the latter predication.

**Provisional assessment.** Composition has the better demonstrated explanation of the contrasts just tested, including a single unlimited scope of actual knowledge. Objective adequacy retains a substantive primitive or total-source option for a positive presentation of unlimited substantial perfection. The observed discriminations have yet to distinguish that source from a finite contributor working with already intelligible contents. The unrestricted P3 therefore has weaker warrant here than the demand to account for content. The residual test is to identify a specific correct judgment about the unified positive perfection on which the finite compositional reading gives a different answer, fix which inputs it has antecedently, and explain why its correction requires a source with the perfection formally or eminently. A difference produced by changing only the subject quantifier or time of possession has already been explained. Conversely, a justified reduction of the remaining judgment to independently grasped constituent meanings would extend the finite account. The mathematical control alone cannot decide either result.
