The best candidate is uncontracted subsistent act as a positive mode of actuality, not a count of effects, absence of incoming arrows, a source-indexed thisness, or a stipulated numerical ranking.

- A selected actual esse-root plus fixed-esse all-maxima reach identifies **every** maximum with that root. No asymmetry premise, comparative ascent, composition of causal chains, or modal necessity is needed for that identity step. The underlying pattern already exists in `DirectEsseRootRoute.maximumEqualsRoot`; this is an application audit for the world-free `CommonEsseIsEssenceSource` interface, not a claimed new philosophical theorem.

**Provisional conclusion:** source-scoped ascent remains better motivated but not established. The practical advance is to separate strict comparison from the causal-order and individuation jobs previously performed by it, and to isolate a testable positive-content condition for strict ascent.

### Positive P and rival ¬P

P: On the fixed productive/constitutive interpretation of receipt, actual identity sources strictly exceed their distinct actual recipients in perfective esse, in the intended same `MoreIn` relation (`8176a284`).

¬P: Some actual identity source causes the esse of a distinct actual recipient without strictly exceeding it in the same perfective comparison. This includes a tie, reversed comparison, or incomparability. A rival should retain the source's and recipient's actual roles, rather than remove the production relation or interpret a different respect as esse. A surviving formal assignment alone does not establish that ¬P is metaphysically possible.

The comparative crux is no longer whether a received act is limited. Grant that limitation as in the existing bundle and test whether it is a *loss of actually realized positive actuality* in the same intrinsic perfective order.

## 4. Lane A: source selection before comparison

### A1. New same-dictionary anchor audit

Assume a supplied actual bearer `n` with no incoming **distinct actual esse cause**:

```
A(n)
∀ c, c ≠ n → A(c) → ¬ C(c,n)
G : ∀ m, Max(m) → ∀ x, x ≠ m → A(x) → C(m,x)
```

For arbitrary `m` satisfying `Max(m)`, suppose `m ≠ n`. Since `A(n)`, G gives `C(m,n)`. Since `Max(m)`, `A(m)`. This contradicts the no-incoming clause. With explicit excluded middle on equality, `m = n`.

Consequences:

1. `5a594b1e` (at most one maximum). No need for maximum existence or positive comparison.
2. If a maximum exists independently, it is the selected `n`; then the same `n` is maximal and has G's universal causal reach. No comparative ascent or chain composition is needed for that identification.
3. Retaining H additionally extends the no-incoming clause to **all distinct causes** of a maximum, including originally untyped candidates. This reaches `18561596`. Without H, a nonactual candidate could satisfy the primitive causal predicate and remain outside the anchor's actual-cause exclusion.
4. With `70e98bb5` (simply prior entails being-cause), reuse `d27dd642` to reach `eaa4c679` (every maximum in being is first). Do not assume that a primitive priority relation is equivalent to existential dependence.

`c35b1a5c` already contains the actual `n` and `∀ c, ¬ ActualEsseCause p c n`; thus A1 can consume this existing proposition's unused root clause and actuality, without consuming its represented identity or paths. Do not register those unused parts as newly essential. A general weak anchor proposition may already exist; inspect the corpus before proposing one. The q.3 variant avoids an unnecessary selected-world bridge for this narrow identity task. When a world-indexed root is the actual input, import the established `maximumEqualsRoot` route instead of duplicating it.

**Circularity gate:** G may be supported by `d9fad785`, by other order/causal bundles, or directly. `0609389b` uses the comparative ascent being avoided. That expansion cannot be credited as an ascent-free route. A1 cannot derive G from `c35b1a5c` by itself: an uncaused universal source can coexist with a tied non-source maximum in a relational model where G fails. Avoid counting same-witness consequences as independent premise warrant.

## 5. Lane B: the intrinsic-perfective gap

### B1. Exact new consequence of the pure-act thisness package

Use the same real determination carrier `D` and predicates `Has`, `Pure` from `PureActRoot`. Here `Has` concerns real determinations, which can include real relations as well as intrinsic constituents. The intrinsic-positive restriction for a comparative order is therefore an additional interpretation check, not automatic from `Has`.

For a supplied actual root `n`, let `S(x) = {d | Pure(d) ∧ Has(x,d)}`. From `fe67d766`, `S(n)` is the entire pure inventory, so `S(x) ⊆ S(n)` for any x. From `3b39e0ba` and `f8b58a9b`, choose a real thisness d of n: `Has(n,d)` and `∀ y, Has(y,d) → y=n`. Strict purity gives `Pure(d)`. Thus d belongs to `S(n)` and is missing from `S(x)` for every distinct x. Hence `S(x) ⊊ S(n)`.

This is a constructive consequence of the existing hypotheses. It neither assumes nor establishes causal relations, world-free identity of esse and essence, or the `MoreIn` calibration. The registered proof `688e42fc` uses the package for root uniqueness, not this exact comparative endpoint. Search for equivalent existing consequences before formalizing. Do not claim that the result is independently warranted merely because the package is already in the graph.

**Thisness price:** if a supposedly pure d is nothing more than being identical to n, or a finite individuating restriction, it is not a valid positive determination under the existing semantics. Strict purity plus actual real thisness makes a strong claim: some pure perfection is incommunicably realized in n. A general principle that every pure perfection can be shared by a distinct actual bearer would conflict with that claim; that shareability principle is not granted here. No mere logical identity predicate, arbitrary source-specific label, or cause-of-all relational designation may be inserted as a new pure determination. A real-thisness candidate must be independently defended. See the prior `pure-act-thisness-fork` attempt. Consequently this is a useful conditional connection and a test of a proposed package, not the preferred independent warrant for `fd2c8710`.

### B2. Weaker strictness requirement: pairwise positive loss

The thisness condition is stronger than the comparison requires. With S(n) complete, strictness against every distinct actual recipient requires only:

```
∀ x, A(x) → x ≠ n → C(n,x) →
  ∃ d, Pure(d) ∧ Has(n,d) ∧ ¬ Has(x,d)
```

The witness may differ from one recipient to another. This avoids demanding a single pure perfection that can belong only to the source. The finite test with `S(n)={p,q}`, `S(x)={p}`, `S(y)={q}` exhibits strict inclusion against both recipients but no common missing witness. This is a structural omission test, not a metaphysical classification of p and q.

If the pure inventory is only a list of kinds (e.g. whether a being has life or goodness), a recipient might realize every kind in a finite degree. Strict degree differences can exist even when binary kind coverage ties. The inventory may therefore require independently justified **actual positive modes or grades**, with their own purity classifications and substantive possession conditions. Do not manufacture degree-sensitive predicates using the conclusion `MoreIn esse n x`. Do not use physical quantities, finite specializations, or possible effects as substitutes for the positive intrinsic content being compared.

### B3. Pure indiscernibility does not solve the strictness gap

Replace real thisness by `bd0a956c`: purely determined real indiscernibles are identical. With root fullness and strict purity, `Has(n,d) ↔ Pure(d)` for every d. For any distinct x, classical excluded middle applied to the complete real determination profile gives the following disjunction:

```
(∃ d, Pure(d) ∧ ¬ Has(x,d))
∨ (∃ d, Has(x,d) ∧ ¬ Pure(d)).
```

Proof: if neither horn holds, x has all pure determinations and no nonpure determinations. It then has exactly the same real determinations as n, which are all pure, and `bd0a956c` identifies x with n. The second horn alone is not a positive pure-content difference: a derivative recipient could have all the same pure determinations plus a nonpure receiving principle. The finite control explicitly retains that horn and a pure-coverage tie, satisfying the supplied indiscernibility rule. Consequently one cannot pass from root uniqueness by pure indiscernibility to strict perfective superiority by the same proof.

**Open refinement:** test whether a nonpure *genuinely limiting* real determination excludes at least one actual positive pure realization. Not every nonpure determination is a restriction on every perfection. A receiving principle's being nonpure alone is insufficient. This is the precise remaining connection rather than an argument against all constitutive participation.

### B4. Capacity contraction versus actual realization

Distinguish an independently described admissible intrinsic-content profile `K(x)` from the actually realized positive content `V(x)`, with `V(x) ⊆ K(x)`. This is a test interface in the assessment, not a proposed parallel repository vocabulary or a new claim about metaphysical possibility.

Suppose a receiving principle restricts x's admissible content: `K(x) ⊊ K(n)`. The difference need not occur in actual content. For example `K(n)={p,q}`, `K(x)={p}`, `V(n)=V(x)={p}` satisfies real constraints on capacity and equal realized profiles. Thus a bound on what can be received is not alone proof of strict actual inferiority.

If the source actually realizes the entire relevant positive intrinsic profile (`V(n)=K(n)`), however, then `V(x) ⊆ K(x) ⊊ V(n)` entails strict actual content inclusion. The same proof works with a local missing actual witness instead of full saturation. This is an explanatory mechanism: the receiving principle excludes content that is not merely possible in the source, but actual there. The new finite test preserves independently assigned capacities and realized contents; no `Limited` flag is used as a disguised degree scale.

**Do not equate saturation with:** the realization of every possible external effect; all mutually incompatible creaturely forms; possessing merely the productive power to cause every perfection; or possessing every binary kind of pure perfection. None establishes realization of each relevant positive intrinsic grade. A passive potency argument can explain why an actual source lacks no intrinsic actuality its nature admits, but a finite immutable nature may exclude grades altogether. The unresolved issue is why subsistent esse itself admits the particular positive realization in question and why receipt excludes it. The strength of the saturation proposal lies in isolating the two facts that need warrant rather than renaming them `Unlimited` and `MoreIn`.

### B5. Preferred metaphysical witness: subsistent act itself

Investigate the following noncognitive candidate. The supplied n exercises actuality not as a subject that receives an act distinct from its own essence, but as subsistent act. This is a positive account of what the source *is*, not simply a negative causal graph property. A recipient exercises an act in a receptive constitutive principle. Could the uncontracted mode be a positive pure determination (or grade) rather than an extrinsic independence description?

The candidate must pass three tests:

1. **Intrinsic positivity:** identify the mode in the constitution of the actual being; exclude the trivial predicates 'is n', 'is uncaused', and 'causes x'. Identity of represented essence and esse provides a starting contrast, not automatically a positive determination. Subsistent act is not self-causation and does not imply necessity by definition.
2. **Independent purity:** justify that this precise actuality involves no intrinsic potency, limitation or reception, without declaring a distinct finite form absent merely because it is finite. Ordinary subsistence in one's own nature is not identical to subsistent esse. A non-pure mode, or a description of absence alone, is not the required witness.
3. **Differential realization:** for the same actual recipient, show that genuinely receiving esse excludes full realization of that mode. The same mode is available as a candidate for every relevant identity source; it is not indexed to the selected source's identity. An actual recipient can be a substance and still lack this mode. A formal loss must be shown to be substantive under the relevant possession sense; no reversal of the existing receipt/identity implications is permitted.

If successful, a pure-mode witness d combined with actual pure-content completeness (or just source possession plus containment of the relevant positive content) supplies B2. A stronger argument could ground the same comparison directly in an independently defended analogical order of actualizing fullness. Both still require the order's connection to the existing `MoreIn esse`.

This proposal reuses `94271978` for its mode contrast where the full input bundle applies. Do not duplicate the existing own-being/infinity/reception argument. It adds the missing *positive characterization* as the substantive research target. It does not repeat the separate exemplar-to-noesis or intellect-to-will work. The parallel intrinsic-perfection investigation can help with actual containment but may not be assumed solved.

### B6. Analogical comparative calibration

A partial order of sets of positive actual realizations does not classify being as a homogeneous genus. The relata can realize a mode according to their distinct natures; incomparability between arbitrary finite bearers is allowed. A strict upper bound from the source can be defined via inclusion in this independent representation. The burden is to defend the representation: a purported degree must track positive actual perfection in the relevant sense, not explanatory priority, number of effects, capacity, or arbitrary labels.

`fd2c8710` says limited esse has a lesser degree than unlimited esse on a supplied preorder. `9eaa3b6e` says higher represented degree implies `MoreIn`; both use broad interfaces, with the former not actuality-scoped by its type. A local pairwise positive-content result does **not** prove these universally quantified originals. Either justify their full scope or propose a faithfully restricted sibling applied only to actual identity-source/recipient pairs. Register a strength relation only when it is verified on the same dictionary. A new witness sufficient for strict actual-content inclusion still needs a warranted `Has`/inclusive `InAct` or degree/`MoreIn` adapter, as appropriate. In particular formal absence cannot be equated with absence of eminent possession.

### 12.1 The new discriminating question: isolated act or whole bearer qua act?

The previous B5 search asked for a *positive pure determination* that is actual at the source and excluded from its recipient. That remains a sufficient path, but it is not the only defensible notion of strictness. There is a substantive fork between:

- **Content-only perfection:** compare positive intrinsic actualities in abstraction from their constitutive mode of realization. A recipient and source with all the same positive actual content can tie, even when one receives its esse.
- **Constitutive perfection:** compare the actuality of the whole bearer as being. The mode of actuality is relevant: that which subsists as its act of being is not actual through a distinct intrinsic receiving principle. A recipient is actual, but its actuality is the actualization of an intrinsic receptive principle. This is a difference of real constitution, not merely of explanatory precedence or number of effects.

The second account does **not** require that an actualized receptive potency is an unrealized potency. It does not claim the recipient fails to exist, that it is defective relative to its own nature, or that it cannot possess the same positive perfection *kinds*. Compare two ways of being actual, not a complete being with a partly nonexistent being. The source's positive characterization is that the entire subsistent being is its own act, rather than a subject actualized by another intrinsic principle. Treat this as a mode of the act, not an extra accidental constituent or a bare identity predicate. Whether this mode belongs to the relevant perfection order is substantive.

**Important correction to B4's scope:** the current `DEF-ESSE-LIMITED` (`6ced5205`) explicitly describes finitude in *formal perfection*, not just a reduced range of potential admissible contents. The tied-capacity fixture in B4 tests a rival or omission of the intended interpretation; it does not certify satisfaction of the stronger metaphysical claim `LIMITED_ESSE_1`. Likewise `OwnBeing` is primitive in `WholePowerPerfection`, and represented esse–essence equality is not by itself a real-constitution proof. The exact same-profile adapter remains a burden.

### 12.2 A candidate independent comparative criterion

On one existing `Has : B → D → Prop`, `Pure : D → Prop` dictionary, consider a fixed independently selected inventory of intrinsic positive determinations relevant to perfection of being. It must preserve any relevant actual grade differences, not just name the kinds of perfection. Let `P(x)` be the pure content actually possessed by x in that inventory. Let `R(x)` be the set of real intrinsic restrictions on x's act of being, identified independently of any `MoreIn` comparison; for a structural realization using the same carrier these are nonpure real determinations of x. Do not put source identities, causal graph relations, neutral individuation or all nonpure determinations into `R` automatically. `Has` includes real relations, so intrinsic application is an explicit restriction.

Define a **candidate preorder** on actual bearers by

```
a >=_const b  iff  P(b) ⊆ P(a) and R(a) ⊆ R(b).
a >_const b   iff  a >=_const b and not (b >=_const a).
```

This is product inclusion of positive actual content and reverse inclusion of real restrictions. It is not a new definition of `MoreIn`, not a quantitative measure, and not a competing repository vocabulary to install before the existing interfaces have been checked. The proof of preorder laws is ordinary inclusion transitivity and reflexivity; its strict part is asymmetric/transitive. Distinct bearers can have equal profiles. Arbitrary finite bearers may be incomparable, particularly if one gains positive content but also carries a new intrinsic restriction. No global genus, totality, cardinality, causal monotonicity or injectivity is required.

For the **same** actual source c and actual recipient x, assume:

1. `P(x) ⊆ P(c)`: positive content coverage at c (sufficiently, the source actually has every relevant pure determination). This is actual intrinsic possession, not power-only eminence. A grade-sensitive reading must justify coverage of the relevant grades as well as kinds.
2. `R(c) = ∅`: the source's actual constitution is free of restrictions on esse.
3. `R(x) ≠ ∅`: a genuine intrinsic receiving limitation belongs to the actual constitution of x.

Then `c >_const x`. In particular, `P(c) = P(x)` does not defeat strictness, because the reverse inclusion of restrictions fails. Neither a uniquely possessed positive determination, a universal missing witness, nor an identity-of-indiscernibles premise is needed. A more local sufficient condition would require only inclusion of the recipient's positive content and proper reverse inclusion of restrictions. Conversely, with tied positive content and no relevant restriction difference, no strictness is obtained. Extra neutral nonpure content alone is insufficient.

The new philosophical commitment is **constitutive non-compensation**:

> Holding fixed or improving relevant positive intrinsic actual content, removing a genuine restriction on the actual mode of esse strictly improves perfection as being; a restriction of esse cannot itself compensate for that loss in the same respect.

This is not a claim that every removal of a feature improves every entity. A form's positive determination of matter can perfect the composite; bounded form, individuating difference and ordinary finite excellence are not automatically defects. It concerns restrictions of the actual act of being and comparison qua being, not excellence qua a finite species. Nor does it follow simply from a definition of `Pure`, which says which determinations involve potency/limitation, without ordering whole bearers. Its independent warrant is the conception of perfection as actualization of the whole, in which the positive unity of the wholly actual constitution is not an additional countable perfection and the actual receptive principle cannot contribute pure actuality *as a restriction*. A mere capacity restriction, or a real relation that does not qualify the act itself, does not pass this test. Stronger claim rejected: that every intrinsic constitution with receptive potency necessarily contains less realized act when the act is considered in isolation.
