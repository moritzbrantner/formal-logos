## 1. Comparative warrant, not another receipt theorem

Fix the same esse, the same productive interpretation if applicable, and the same actual source and actual recipient. The issue is not whether the source precedes, or whether the recipient is limited. Grant source identity, receipt of the target, the relevant limitation and no limitation of the source. Ask what follows about their actuality.

Three comparisons must be kept distinct:

1. **Existential independence:** the source supplies actuality the recipient does not supply to itself. The source has no relevant incoming actual dependence, and the recipient does. This is a difference in explanatory roles.
2. **Constitutive mode:** if receipt is a real intrinsic constitution rather than a merely external grounding relation, the recipient's actuality is exercised in a receptive principle; the source's act is not so received. This is a difference in how the act is possessed, not necessarily a comparison of quantity.
3. **Perfective fullness:** an act is strictly greater if the actual positive content of the lesser is possessed by the greater in the relevant perfective respect and the greater possesses an actual positive determination or degree that the lesser lacks. This is a proposed sufficient rationale for an analogical comparison, not a replacement definition of the existing MoreIn.

### Constitutive restriction argument

A strong positive rationale for `fd2c8710` is that a real contraction is more than an explanatory condition. It must restrict what actuality the recipient intrinsically realizes. Consider two candidate models of receipt: external sustaining of an otherwise identical intrinsic act, and internal reception which delimits the act. The first can preserve equality of intrinsic act while varying the source of its occurrence. The second need not. If receptive potency is a real constitutive feature and absence of reception means that esse is possessed as act itself, the intrinsic modes cannot be identical in the respect of reception. But a difference in intrinsic constitution does not yet supply a strict order: a mode can be an alternative rather than a privation.

The positive strictness case needs both a **preservation** claim and a **non-loss** claim: every positive actuality relevant to the comparison that the recipient has is contained in the source (in an intrinsic, not merely productive sense), and at least one actual restriction of the recipient excludes a positive perfective mode or degree realized in the source. A numerical magnitude, common genus and comparability between arbitrary finite essences are unnecessary. The source is a strict upper bound in a partial order of actual perfective content. This uses the prior coverage audit as a sufficient-condition template rather than claiming it proves the esse comparison. The candidate order is non-indexed: it cannot be generated by inserting "is n" or "caused x" into the inventory. It also cannot be generated simply by setting the degree to 1 for unreceived and 0 for received; that would conceal the comparison in the calibration.

A promising strictness witness is *subsistent possession of actuality* as a positive mode of exercising act itself, not the external relational fact of lacking a cause. It would have to be independently classified as pure and intrinsic in the chosen determination dictionary. The existing `94271978` supplies a same-source/recipient mode contrast; it does not supply that classification. Even if this succeeds, the source must retain the recipient's relevant intrinsic content, and the absence of the positive mode in the recipient must be absence in the chosen substantive possession sense, not merely absence of its finite formal realization. The separate whole-power/containment investigation therefore remains relevant, but must not be smuggled into this argument. In particular, the source's ability to produce something cannot supply preservation by itself.

A second defensible direct option: `fd2c8710` is a primitive claim that a constitutively contracted existential actuality is less perfective than unrestricted existential actuality. Its positive warrant is that contraction is a real restriction of the very act being compared, not the act's external location or provenance. The rival can deny that the restriction of act constitutes a loss of positive actuality relative to the other bearer. The primitive option is narrower and better motivated than the blanket all-causes principle; it is not analytic from the names or an independent corroboration of itself. The calibration `9eaa3b6e` is acceptable only on the same perfective order, not a scale fitted to the conclusion.

### Standing rivals and comparative assessment

| Account | What explains receipt/dependence? | What explains comparative grade? | Assessment |
|---|---|---|---|
| Thomistic constitutive participation | Receiving principle really delimits act; source supplies it without receiving it | Strict intrinsic preservation plus a genuine positive actual restriction; alternatively direct contraction-to-less principle | Positive explanatory rationale for source-scoped ascent, with the comparison warrant still open |
| Extrinsic existential dependence | Source maintains a self-contained intrinsic nature; dependence concerns occurrence rather than qualitative constitution | Same intrinsic grade can obtain in the source and target, or different grades; the sustaining law need not order them | Coherent as a structural alternative to the comparative inference; whether it satisfies real constitutive reception is open; not a counterexample to that stronger account |
| Internal but non-ordered receipt | Recipient is genuinely constituted by received act; the source is not, but this is a different mode rather than a lesser positive content | Positive perfections can be distributed independently of the receiving mode, allowing a tie or incomparable profile | Strongest rival after granting the reception/limitation contrast; owes an account of why real contraction does not restrict the relevant perfective content. Conversely participation owes an actual positive restriction/containment rationale. |

A superior recipient in a finite specific respect (e.g. a richer realized structure) does not itself refute source superiority at esse. A superior recipient *at esse* requires the same independently warranted sense of the comparative. No actual or metaphysically possible case of equal or higher esse is established here. A full-quality profile tied on every positive feature is inconsistent with a **positive intrinsic** feature absent from the recipient, if such a feature is established; it is not inconsistent with different dependence roles. This is the discriminating burden, not an argument that all possible alternatives to participation are incoherent.

**Provisional judgment:** `8176a284` has moderate positive conditional warrant under real constitutive participation; retain the primitive and the existing absolute-limitation derivation. Do not establish it. `cffad050` is materially broader because a received intermediate could cause another received act without being absolutely unrestricted. `d5a94eb1` has still broader scope (including every caused respect) and needs its own positive comparative account. The source-scoped argument is no justification of either universal principle. A justified intrinsic-content interpretation of contraction and a same-dictionary strictness witness would improve the judgment; a positively warranted equal-esse constitutive receipt account would weaken it.

## 2. Proposed substitution: causal exclusion without comparative ascent

All below uses the existing `CausesIn` at esse and `IsMaximumIn` (actuality AND no superior among *all* candidates). Write A(x) for InAct x esse, M(x) for IsMaximumIn, C(c,x) for CausesIn c x esse. Let:

- G = `da09703b`: M(m) ∧ x ≠ m ∧ A(x) → C(m,x).
- H = `4a5a43a5` (`CauseOfABeingHasBeing`): C(c,x) ∧ A(x) → A(c). This is weaker than `4ccb319f`. Its recorded rationale is for extrinsic causes of esse. Applying H to the mode-unspecified C in the consumer requires warrant that the relevant edges satisfy that interpretation.
- K (candidate local policy, not a new registered proposition): for distinct actual c,m, if M(m) and C(c,m), then ¬ C(m,c). This is reverse-edge exclusion at the actual maximal target, not global causal asymmetry. It concerns the tenseless CausesIn dictionary; existing `bcf79e03` is at a selected world on actual concrete grounding and must not be silently substituted for K.

**Proof of `18561596` (MaximumHasNoDistinctEsseCause).** Suppose M(m), c ≠ m and C(c,m). M supplies A(m), H gives A(c), G gives C(m,c), and K contradicts that reverse edge. Given G, K is equivalent to exclusion of distinct actual incoming esse causes of maxima, since that exclusion already rules out the antecedent of K. With G and H it is equivalent to the full `18561596` scope. This equivalence supplies no separate warrant for exclusion without a defense of K’s asymmetry. If the defense concerns selected actual concrete grounding, transporting it requires cause-to-ground and actuality/concreteness bridges in the same world. In particular, `cb1cdea9` takes an actual concrete target. The q.3 actuality premise supplies only `InAct`.

**Proof of `5a594b1e` (AtMostOneMaximeEns):** Given distinct maxima m,m', G gives C(m,m') and C(m',m), contradicting either K instance. Both are already actual, so H is not needed. Alternatively G plus `18561596` suffices. Use explicit `ee4ab504` for the final equality in the existing constructive interface. This proposed G/K sibling gives at most one maximum, not existence of a maximum or superiority over arbitrary recipients.

On one bearer carrier write `n` for the supplied De ente identity source, `m` for a bearer satisfying MAXIME_1 at esse, and `g` for an arbitrary bearer satisfying GOD_4. If the grounding profile is `F` at world `w`, `ActualRoot F w n` concerns this same `n`. None of these roles by itself identifies its bearer with either of the others. The selected-world proof and its two directional bridges appear in [A1](PRD-esse-comparative-ascent.md#a1-new-same-dictionary-anchor-audit).

**Source-selected variant:** Given `c35b1a5c` and `b4987085`, the same actual identity source n is uncaused in the actual-esse order and supplies each other actual bearer (existing chain composition). If G is already available by a non-ascent route, every actual maximum m must equal n: otherwise m causes n, contradicting its no-incoming-actual-cause clause. Alternatively, if `18561596` is independently available, n causes every distinct actual maximum m, contradiction; hence `da09703b` follows from c35b1a5c/b4987085/18561596/EM. Do not use the G-dependent proof of `18561596` in this latter derivation; that would cycle. An actual source's exclusion of an incoming *distinct* CausesIn edge requires H if all candidates are quantified over.

**Boundary:** an existing common universal cause and causal asymmetry alone do not exclude a tied maximum. They say nothing about an incoming edge to the source in a degree order or the strict order from it to its target. Existing G or independent source/maximum exclusion must do real work. In particular using `0609389b` as the only derivation of G merely trades the universal principle for `8176a284`; using a direct G option or a genuinely separate G derivation avoids comparison, but remains conditional on G's substantial causal coverage. The existing maximal-grounding-asymmetry investigation (`2247dfc4`, `e9efc513`, `924e859a`) already proves related actual-concrete root consequences; this proposed substitution targets the original *world-free* no-distinct-esse-cause and maximum-uniqueness endpoints. Do not claim new verified results or replace the existing world-indexed arguments.

They do not exclude necessary nonroots or name GOD_4. A global recursive frontier avoidance claim would require the corpus derivability audit, not inspection of these immediate theorem premises. Intrinsic fullness may justify a partial order of positive intrinsic content but ties remain possible without a distinct positive missing determination or a genuine intensity contrast. No adapter from real Has to inclusive InAct is assumed. No proof that an ultimate source intrinsically possesses all pure perfections is smuggled in from productive power. This is compatible with the separate research scope on `64498e7e`.

## 5. Standalone finite verification and self-challenge

`esse_ascent_controls.py` independently checks the proposed G+H+K substitution on all 262,144 assignments of three bearers (arbitrary active subsets, arbitrary MoreIn including inactive candidates, arbitrary distinct C edges). 100,044 satisfy the three hypotheses, 5,004 of those have a maximum, and none violates either `18561596` or `5a594b1e`. This is a finite scope control, not a Lean proof or a metaphysical model; the general proof is given above. Self-cause edges are omitted because both endpoints and K are distinct-scoped. The omission of H is exposed by an inactive cause of an active maximum; the single-witness omission is exposed by an active universal source tied with its effect. The separate degree fixture has the entire bearer carrier `A={0,1}`, with both bearers stipulated actual. A stipulated edge runs from the represented identity source to the distinct recipient. The recipient’s receiving principle is distinct from its received act, and limited and received have the same extension in this fixture. The equal, inverted and ordered degree assignments use the strict part of the represented numerical preorder. Only the ordered assignment satisfies `fd2c8710` over that two-bearer carrier and the corresponding source-ascent formula. These structural surrogates leave the real interpretation and applicability of `fd2c8710` open. Finally, an inactive superior defeats positive maximum despite source ascent over all actual recipients and asymmetric MoreIn, exposing the need for `c11369aa`.

Self-sweep: (i) no model asserts actual metaphysical possibilities; (ii) rival and participation use the same criterion of intrinsic content; (iii) no new historical attribution or witness materialization; (iv) one universal witness is not the all-maxima law; (v) disclose empty strict relation in tie, omitted self edges, receipt interpretation and represented identity; (vi) keep comparison/ground/priority and actual-world/tenseless senses separate; (vii) keep the original independent challenge and the targeted acceptance distinct. The strongest unresolved challenge on the comparative side is that form can actualize a receptive principle and thereby contribute positively to the unity and actuality of the composite even when real pure content ties. Current main has already tested the H.6 challenge at equal grade-sensitive content. Its transverse-lock control distinguishes loss of available motion, preserved actual forward displacement and improved heading stability. A real constraint needs a criterion of privation in the very respect compared. The subsistent whole has no constituent needing a distinct act, whereas the recipient’s principle is actually perfected through such an act. This gives positive warrant for comparing modes of whole-bearer actualization. Subtracting an essential receiving principle would change the recipient. At fixed actual contents the content-only rival still earns a tie, and the strict whole-bearer judgment remains conditional on calibration. See [the equal-content comparison](../maximum-definition-fork.md#can-a-receiving-constitution-itself-make-a-bearer-less-in-esse). The strongest attack on the causal replacement is that G supplies an otherwise unwarranted universal causal scope; no circular derivation through the conclusion is permitted.

## 6. Remaining research

Fix the same actual giver and recipient throughout. Identify independently how each realizes the recipient’s actualizing contribution from form and receptive principle. On a degree measured by a least upper bound, give an independently justified ceiling on **every** relevant realized recipient level and exhibit a giver-realized determination above it on that scale. If all grade-sensitive positive contents tie, supply a separate application in which completeness of whole-bearer actualization distinguishes degrees of esse with that inventory fixed. Neither test may use the pair’s `MoreIn esse` as input or classify “unreceived” as a higher grade by definition. A successful test warrants a comparison for this pair; extending it to every qualifying identity giver requires coverage. The registered ceiling argument and its scope are given in [B4](PRD-esse-comparative-ascent.md#b4-capacity-contraction-versus-actual-realization). For causal exclusion, the outstanding question is independent warrant for K at actual maxima or a justified same-world cause-to-ground adapter. The G/K and AggregateEsseCause siblings remain proposals.
