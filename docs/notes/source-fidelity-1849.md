Material indexed but not read is explicitly **unreviewed**. Interpretive fidelity is a human judgment, and a valid Lean implication is only a consequence of its formal premises. No full-corpus or periodic source materialization is required.

## Deliberate separation and outstanding work

- **Located**: source IDs and conventional locators on the selected records. **Reproducible**: manuscript/edition revision with selector, or hashed website snapshot. **Actually consulted here**: the six original-language Greek/Latin witnesses in the table, plus the SCG live Latin page; missing cached bytes say nothing about earlier readings. **Not consulted here**: Pruss's pinned 2009 snapshot. None of these categories implies the next.
- Aristotle's `TRUE_7` is only a thin respect. Aquinas's `TRUE_3` is an additional conformity-to-intellect analysis. The historically attributed claim now uses the first; the Lean `truth` parameter at `5a948c37` is likewise tagged with thin `TRUE_7`. The `TRUE_3` link records a Thomistic interpretation. Applying the result in that stronger sense would require an argued interpretation bridge.
- The source-faithful q.14 a.1 proposition does not prove `¬ ComposedOfMatterAndForm x ∧ (∀ y, ¬ ComponentOf x y) → HasIntellect x`. Candidate graded bridges are already recorded as open statements `44000fce` and `0bdb0436`. The universal rule still needs a reason why the two exclusions reach the highest grade throughout its intended domain. Aquinas's application to God by itself gives no such scope argument.
- Existing `supports` relations, rather than a new attribution database, record when the witness bears on but does not state the stronger proposition. These are not formal derivations or verified source claims. The new Rust regressions protect the two precise source-to-analyst boundaries.
- **Existing maximality metadata:** `101bdd62` carries both the general `MAXIMAL_1` relation and its esse-specific `MAXIMAL_6` specialization. The two labels are reported together by the sense diagnostic. This audit preserves those links and the proof; resolving their legacy bookkeeping is separate from the Greek-to-Thomistic interpretation.
- **Open scholarly disputes:** the unrestricted reach of Aristotle's final *each* in 993b31; whether the Fourth Way's `verum` is fully Aquinas q.16 conformity; the semantics of `alia causa` in II.15; q.14's generalization from God's immateriality to all conceivable noncomposites; Pruss's fidelity pending access to the selected pinned section. Follow-up contexts: #1849, #1731, #1855, #1873, #1864, #1890 and #1892.
- **Proof impact:** No Lean declaration, inference, assumption list, materialization command, or source pin changed. Two former *stating* links to strong analytic premises are removed and replaced by *supports* links; they are therefore not established historical statements. All original conditional proofs remain exactly as before; root-path/source-support status must be computed by `truth frontier-diff --base <merge-base>`, not inferred from file counts.
