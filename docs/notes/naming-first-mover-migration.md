### Strongest positive identification warrant

A causal/remotive concept can identify an object through its effects without
first enumerating its intrinsic nature. The substantial warrant here is that
this one cause accounts for the very actuality of every other bearer and all
its perfections, while no other maximum rivals it and it is not a constituent
of its products. If instantiated in the intended metaphysics, this is an
ultimate existential role, not an interchangeable office among local agents.
It motivates the q13 reference to what is above and principle of all. Failure
to describe its intrinsic nature exhaustively need not defeat successful
identification: direct reference and subsequent discovery of properties are
legitimate possibilities. No endlessly prior proof of each primitive is needed.

But successful reference to a unique role-bearer and successful identification
with the existing classical-God predicate are different achievements. For a
reference-fixing defense, what is needed is an independently supported account
that this role is constitutive of the intended GOD_4 referent, rather than a
weaker role shared by something lacking an essential classical commitment.
Merely declaring "GOD_4 means this antecedent" changes the target fixed by the
existing definition. Merely citing q13 usage establishes neither reference
success nor the classical attribute interpretation. The retained source
readings explain why the bridge is a serious positive proposal, but not why
a rival substantive interpretation is excluded. The source/signification and
analyst application distinction is preserved.

Two support options therefore remain legitimate. Directly judge that this
ultimate role warrants proper divine identification, recording its defeaters;
or support that judgment cumulatively by the existing arguments for simplicity,
perfection, infinity and intellectual actuality of this same bearer. The latter
need not prove every attribute before any provisional use of the name. It must,
however, preserve the open premises and cannot count the nominal equivalence
as an independent strand. The result is provisional warrant, not new closure.
