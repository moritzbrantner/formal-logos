# Logical psychologism: correctness and psychological realization

Continues the psychologism candidate in [#1989](https://github.com/moritzbrantner/formal-logos/pull/1989) and the consequence program [#1007](https://github.com/moritzbrantner/formal-logos/issues/1007). Research checkpoint, 2026-11-25; analyst formulations, attribution deferred. This is a linked research note, not a new truth-status authority.

## Fixed comparison and existing evidence

Hold the meanings, truth conditions and class of admissible valuations fixed. Distinguish an inference type's truth preservation from its occurrence, habitual acceptance, psychological realization and the subject's reason for drawing it. Do not infer a denial of noncontradiction from a theory about the ground of logic. F1 `c425f0c5` and thin F6 `f56b2ee7` are proposed destinations, not asserted new graph edges.

For a first classical propositional test, compare modus ponens (p, p → q therefore q) with affirming the consequent (q, p → q therefore p). In the valuation p=false, q=true, the latter's premises are true and its conclusion false. Suppose one population accepts both inferential habits universally. The habits have identical prevalence but different truth-preserving status. Conversely, two populations can vary in their dispositions toward one fixed inference without thereby changing its truth preservation. This excludes an exact criterion based solely on that prevalence, conditional on the shared semantics, not every psychological explanation of the ability to reason.

Reuse [identity](../../corpus/results/e03147fa-7a1f-48d9-bac0-7aec0ee2b645.json), [validity control](../../corpus/results/b203c461-805a-4572-b966-ba3a7886d561.json), [fallible correction](fallible-correction-931.md), [epistemic authority](epistemic-authority.md) and [rational basing](epistemic-underminer-boundaries.md#rational-basing-the-particular-transition-must-exercise-the-capacity). The first result concerns a semantic sequent class including identity, not universal assent or a comprehensive account of logic. The second witnesses validity and invalidity in one profile. Neither by itself supplies the metaphysical independence thesis.

## Live branches

- Descriptive laws, actual habits and counterfactual dispositions versus a truth-directed or idealized psychological account.
- Empirical self-application: deriving evidence-based conclusions versus merely reporting the occurrence of those conclusions.
- Evolution, functional realization and error correction: explanatory target is validity, not just competence, reliability or performance.
- Connect only the exact denials and same-witness bridges to F1 and F6; preserve F6's thin existential scope and stronger authority claims separately.

## Discrimination of positive rival accounts

In every row hold fixed the target question, the meanings of p and q, and all four classical valuations. In actual application the adequacy of those semantics must be defended rather than assumed from the name of a logic.

| Account | Positive replacement and strongest response | Outcome of matched test |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Actual habit or frequency | Right inference is what the population actually accepts or regularly performs; the law may be statistical or deterministic. | A stipulated unanimous invalid transition and unanimous valid transition share acceptance. Even deterministic occurrence is not truth preservation. Frequency is neither necessary (unperformed valid inference) nor sufficient (performed invalid inference). |
| Counterfactual disposition | Right inference is what a competent reasoner would accept after reflection, with distractions removed. | Two stable policies can differ at the same countervaluation. If ideality means mere stability, unanimity, consistency of one belief set or absence of distraction, the failure persists. If ideality means correct answers in all relevant cases, truth-sensitive ideality, not disposition alone, does the discriminating work. This is not an objection to every fully specified psychological law: a richer input could distinguish the two procedures. |
| Actual practice or constitutive rule | The rule constitutes the content, or fixes the practice of giving and asking for reasons. | A different meaning or consequence relation changes the comparison, not the validity of an unchanged inference. Within a fixed practice, correct execution of its rule is distinct from the rule's truth preservation. A coherent rule-defined consequence relation is a live account, but an identification with truth preservation needs the semantic bridge. Logical pluralism is not thereby excluded. |
| Evolutionary/functional truth-directed rationality | Selection or learning builds a capacity with the function of obtaining accurate beliefs, and a malfunction can be widespread. | This has resources to distinguish normal performance from successful performance, and to explain why agents track validity. Local adaptive advantage or actual-world success alone cannot license universally truth-preserving inference. A semantic success condition can explain why the capacity is good without implying that correctness is a nonphysical substance. |
| Realization, identity or truth-sensitive naturalism | Contentful cognitive processes implement valid operations; perhaps a complete physical/functional account realizes the truth relation as well. | This survives the test: the psychological implementation can vary while the same interpreted rule stays valid. A theory that identifies or grounds semantic facts in a wider physical/functional base is not refuted by holding semantics fixed. It owes an account of content and truth across the relevant comparisons, not an independent nonphysical entity. |
| Eliminative global denial | No transition is genuinely correct, no matter what the contents and truth conditions are; all apparent warrant is merely an occurrence. | Denial of all semantic entailment in a class containing singleton identity is excluded by the existing theorem. Denial of all epistemic reasons is self-undermining and conflicts with F6. A mere causal report or a reasonless true denial is not thereby internally contradictory. |

The strongest live rival is the truth-directed realization/grounding account, not crude statistical psychologism. It can grant all local correctness judgments. The discriminating issue becomes whether the supplied semantic/normative relations genuinely reduce to the proposed natural facts or merely accompany them. The equal-frequency test establishes underdetermination by a projection; it cannot establish ontological irreducibility, conceptual independence of every psychological fact, or the modal possibility of minds with the same *total* psychological facts and different validity.

### Why a change of dispositions is not a change of validity

The fixed inference is an ordered premise set and a conclusion, with its interpretation kept fixed. On those inputs, `Entails S Γ q` does not take population or psychological states as arguments. Invariance in that representation is therefore a definitional feature, not a theorem about the possibility of psychology-independent abstract objects. The nontrivial comparison is whether a rival's proposed data can recover the correct classification of two inferences in that very interpretation, not whether a rigidly defined slot stays rigid. If the rival says contents or truth conditions change with the population, require a translation preserving content before calling the judgment a changed validity. A world in which no one performs MP is not needed as a metaphysical possibility: the finite control only omits its acceptance while preserving semantic input.

### Empirical self-application and the regress boundary

Let H be the claim that an empirical pattern of acceptance is the sufficient standard of logical validity. Let a researcher infer H from observed data D using inference R. Distinguish (i) D caused acceptance of H, (ii) R was the rule normally used, (iii) D actually supports H through R, and (iv) the researcher has warrant to conclude H. A causal or sociological explanation of (i)-(ii) does not supply (iii). An empirical survey might support a generalization about actual acceptance, but this narrower conclusion is not H. The inference from observations to unrestricted validity needs both a generalization principle and a semantic bridge. Merely applying the favored rule to certify that it is favored does not provide the missing discrimination against a stable, differently favored rule.

The objection is not that every reasoning procedure must be proved by a prior argument. A primitive rational grasp, reliable externalist entitlement or a rule-guided capacity can be a starting point; a complete survey can also be evidence about the populations surveyed. The objection is to a sufficient criterion failing its own proposed truth-directed task, and to H borrowing a notion of epistemic support it claims to eliminate. If its advocate offers no reasoned defense of H, H might still be true; that concession loses the status of an epistemically warranted rival, not its logical consistency. If the advocate keeps real support but identifies it with a truth-sensitive functional role, the rival is no longer an elimination of correctness. A psychological explanation of how a reasoner discovered a valid argument is perfectly compatible with the argument's validity.

### Evolution, ideal competence and objective success

Under fixed classical semantics, restrict observed trials to p=true (so q is true whenever the conditional premise is true) and the two rules need not exhibit a wrong conclusion on any eligible trial. More directly, a training set containing only p=q=true makes both rules succeed when their premises are true. Add p=false, q=true: AC now has a counterexample; MP has no true premise pair there. Repeated success, fitness or convergence on the initial distribution cannot differentiate the policies as universally valid. The missing condition is coverage of truth-preservation failures, or a proof from content that extends beyond training data. This is a deduction-specific application of #931 and the already registered benchmark/responsiveness distinction; it is not a new evolutionary debunking result.

An account that makes accuracy or fitting attitudes constitutive of the function can classify a prevalent error as a malfunction. It thereby preserves a contrast between a token's happening and its meeting a standard. The contrast does not logically entail a separate realm of logical facts, a categorical duty for a person indifferent to truth, or an intelligent ground of the standard. The remaining positive challenge is to give a non-question-begging reduction of truth-preserving validity, or a reason that no such reduction can work; the present finite comparison does neither for arbitrary reductions. Grant the same status to a realist capacity account and a naturalized truth-directed capacity account on the fixed examples. Selection history alone is not a defeater of either.

## Exact root destinations and denial consequences

**F1** is not the proposition that all logical correctness is irreducible. The exact record [c425f0c5](../../corpus/statements/case-non-contradiction.json) binds `CasePredicateNonContradiction`: for all sorts of beings/respects and predicates P, for all x,r, not (P x r and not P x r). The rival recorded there is an actual same-predicate contradiction, not psychologism. A valid MP sequent is not a proof of this case-predicate claim in a rival semantics. The existing Lean encoding makes its denial impossible with Lean negation; it does not encode a paraconsistent negation. See the existing F1 record and D-L. A psychologistic theory could accept F1 and say human dispositions explain access to it. The useful conditional consequence is: if a psychological rule makes P(x,r) and its same-sense negation jointly correct as truth assertions, and correctness is factive in those asserted contents, it conflicts with F1. Absent that bridge, acceptance of both by everyone shows error or inconsistency of attitudes, not the truth of both. No new `alternative_to` relation to F1 is justified.

**F6** [f56b2ee7](../../corpus/statements/there-are-epistemic-reasons.json) binds `ThereAreEpistemicReasons Reason := ∃ a p, Reason a p`. Its exact denial is `EpistemicNihilism Reason := ∀ a p, ¬ Reason a p`. If the radical thesis excludes every instance of the same reason relation, its conflict with F6 is the existing [f6ExcludesNihilism](../../corpus/results/146b502b-e5fd-40cf-8a39-9ed2d45e59bb.json). The existing retorsion separately excludes a reason for its own denial; it leaves a reasonless denial satisfiable. Denying irreducibility is not equivalent to denying the existential: a goal-relative or truth-tracking reason can be genuine. A validity fact is not yet an agent's reason to believe a content. Even if an inference is valid, the premises may be false, unknown, unwarranted, ungrasped, or not actually used. None of these conditions is supplied by truth preservation alone.

For a constructive F6 application, use the short proof selected in #1989 and #1987: universal falsity cannot include True without contradiction; distinguish this from trying to derive False by applying the universal statement at False (which yields only ¬False). Ask whether one actual subject grasps the same content and valid transition, accepts the conclusion on surviving support, and has that support as a reason for believing it. A valid proof object by itself is not an actual investigator's uptake. Reuse [reasonsFromWarrant](../../corpus/results/b3ddb7c4-683a-4127-8221-b30be7858f92.json) with exact bundle `6280fbf0` (some warranted acceptance), `0e2bbd59` (accepted warrant needs surviving basis), `6b4e3331` (surviving basis gives reason). Its output is F6; eligibility via `1db47fd9` already includes a reason and cannot independently warrant F6. A fresh actual observed/assessed proof uptake, not the reason-dependent route, is the intended next test. The #930 authority result and controls do not upgrade F6 to preference-independent force.

The existing rooted consumer [populationFromReasons](../../corpus/results/b50727e1-04de-488c-819f-2d5b9993c485.json) requires F6, reason-bearer presence at the selected world `ada25623` and the existing esse contract `0436de86`; yields `56f68b40`. The direct-esse stage-one argument `72391d4d` requires all its further metaphysical premises. Nothing here directly derives GOD_4 or excludes an impersonal necessary source.

## Provisional assessment and next test

At the baseline and in the fixed classical inference task, bare frequency/actual practice as the entire truth-preservation criterion is inadequate: the countervaluation is an explicit checkable failure, not a preference for a different school. The same is true of any proposed classifier that ignores that distinction while making the two inferences observationally identical. This favors a standard of correctness not exhausted by the examined descriptive features. It gives cumulative warrant for the ordinary-role F6 interpretation insofar as valid content-sensitive inquiry really supplies favorable bearing; it does not establish the actual existential or categorical authority from a mathematical truth table. F1 remains unchanged. No substantive objection to the finite comparison within its semantics is identified; the strongest scope objections are the fixed-meaning restriction, richer truth-sensitive inputs, and nonclassical or rule-constituted contents. A reduction that independently explains those semantic constraints and correct error discrimination would weaken the stronger anti-reduction judgment; a genuinely complete comparison that preserves all proposed reducing facts while correctness differs would strengthen it. Such a comparison has not been supplied.

Next: instantiate the existing criterion characterization on the two sequents, test the omitted semantic/evidential and adoption conditions, and use the #1987 short proof as a selected actual proof-uptake case. Preserve the contrast between absence of reasons and lack of categorical force. This is a proposed assessment, not independently reviewed.
