# Spatial translation, individuation and sufficient reason

The question is whether unchanged relations among bodies settle the identity or
explanation of their spatial placement. This bounded comparison supports the
foundational inquiry without assuming either relationalism or absolute space.
The analyst geometry and the historical arguments have different authorities. [Research record](../../corpus/attempts/space-translation-boundary.json)

## Consulted evidence and actual argument

`LEIBNIZ-CLARKE-CORRESPONDENCE` pins the Newton Project's normalized transcriptions
of the 1717 English exchange. Both selected ranges, Leibniz's Third Paper §§1–8
and Clarke's Third Reply §§1–8, were materialized and read in full before claims.
The selected Clarke range ends before numbered §9: its combined “7 and 8” is one
HTML paragraph, so HTML paragraph IDs are not treated as philosophical locators.
The modern page's footer/chrome bytes differ from the historical archive copies;
selected text matches exactly. The 2019 Leibniz and 2018 Clarke archive fallbacks
were each fetched twice with matching bytes. No long source text is committed.

- `LEIBNIZ-III-4-RELATIONAL-SPACE`: space is an order of coexistences.
- `LEIBNIZ-III-5-7-SPATIAL-REASON`: given uniform absolute space, a relocation that
  preserves mutual situations allegedly lacks a reason for preferring this
  placement. Leibniz invokes his reason principle and rejects mere will as an
  answer to that contrast. This is his argument, not a proved consequence here.
- `CLARKE-III-2-5-DISTINCT-PLACES`: perfectly alike places can remain distinct;
  unchanged internal order need not mean the same absolute place.
- `CLARKE-III-2-5-8-VOLITIONAL-REASON`: for indifferent or equally good options,
  intrinsic volition may suffice without an externally distinguishing reason.

Thus Clarke does not merely abandon reason. The dispute includes what a
sufficient reason must provide. The source context also prevents identifying
Clarke's space with a separately existing material substance or with God: §3
expressly describes a different account. That context is not needed by the
mathematical certificate and no existence-of-God result is claimed.

## Exact formal certificate and reuse

`SPACE-TRANSLATION-BOUNDARY` concerns labeled configurations `B → ℝ`, with
`shift a x i = x i + a` and selected data `relative x i j = x i - x j`.
Mathlib supplies real additive-group arithmetic and the metric; this does not
introduce a hand-rolled geometry. Existing `HistoryContrastive`,
`SelectedEffectBridge`, the static correction-criterion factorization and the
arrangement-dynamics semiconjugacy results were inspected: they concern
explanatory histories, conditional effects, correctness data or interventions,
and none is a translation-orbit/covariance theorem. Their philosophical meanings
are not identified with spatial placement. The elementary additive cancellation
also used by the parallel measurement-calibration investigation is mathematical
reuse, not an identification of calibration with physical space.

The frozen Challenge advertises one certificate with these clauses:

1. With a supplied body `b : B`, equal signed relative data hold exactly when
   the two labeled configurations differ by one common translation.
2. Every common translation preserves all pair distances. No converse from
   unsigned distances to a translation is advertised: signed differences encode
   more information, including the chosen line's orientation.
3. An inhabited configuration is fixed by a translation exactly when its
   displacement is zero.
4. There is no deterministic function from the selected relative data to an
   absolute configuration that is covariant under **every** common translation.
   The input is unchanged while covariance would move its output, forcing a
   nonzero translation to fix that inhabited output.
5. Subtracting the supplied body's coordinate gives a translation-invariant
   representative with the same relative data. This is a normalization, not a
   claim to discover an actual absolute origin.
6. Two labeled bodies at `0` and `1`, translated to `1` and `2`, have unchanged
   relative data and distance `1`, but unequal absolute coordinate functions.
7. With an empty body type, a covariant selector exists. Inhabitedness is a real
   condition on the obstruction, not an implicit nonvacuity assumption.

The certificate freezes no historical PSR as a hypothesis. Its covariance
condition is an explicitly chosen analyst norm. It also assumes a deterministic
single-output selector; a set-valued or nondeterministic account is a different
problem. The general signature does not include trajectories, acceleration,
rotation, fields, force laws, observability or all physical quantities.

provided that the *same* selected world has a contingent actual concrete

Both `ActualConcreteWholeAt` and `ContingentWholeAt` are independently open.
No modal possibility of a physical translation, global simultaneity, or real

`SPATIOTEMPORAL_2` non-occupation, not absence of spatiotemporal relation,

### Exact mathematical and ontological distinctions

The existing Leibniz–Clarke profile proves translations preserve signed relative
displacements and pair distances; its no-selector theorem depends on a
deterministic, covariant absolute-position selector. The identity criteria prove
equivalence of relational, orbit and represented identity only with additional
data-preservation, orbit-completeness and triviality assumptions. Neither result
supplies `ExistsAt`, `ContingentAt`, numerical identity or a `Profile.grounds`
edge (still less esse in the DEF-ESSE sense).

The Lorentz boost certifies invariant interval and `GeometricBefore` order
while changing remote simultaneity for a spacelike pair. A coordinate event,
a `TemporalHistory.IsAt` predicate and a whole-history possible world are
different carriers. A light-cone comparison is a **geometric ordering
constraint**, not `Ontic.Causes` physical/efficient causation, and neither of
those is `Profile.grounds` existential support. The `PriorSimply`
metaphysical-priority relation is separate again; existing
`DependenceGroundingPriority` supplies explicit bridge hypotheses between
dependence, actual grounding and simple priority. `EverlastingAt` expresses
presence through the selected times, not cross-world necessity or proper
Boethian timelessness. The new result never uses any of these as implicit
ontological bridges.

### Nonvacuity, omission and root integration

**Joint model.** `jointModel` (a companion Comparator theorem of the same
result) checks that the four premises of `derive` are jointly satisfiable
with a located occupant distinct from the whole. In `jointProfile`/`jointFrame`
the root exists at both worlds, is ungrounded and occupies no region; the
whole and the occupant exist only at the selected world `false`, both occupy
the one region, and the only grounding edge is whole-to-occupant at `false`.
Like the omission control it is a structural surrogate (`related` and
`partOf` empty), not a claimed actual or physically possible spacetime.

**Omission.** The same `NecessaryConcreteUnderived.witnessProfile` supplies a
populated finite control. At world `false`, the actual concrete spacetime whole `true`
is contingent, while the existing root `false` is necessary and underived.
The root actually grounds the whole, `grounds false false true`, and every
candidate occupies the one region. There is **no** ground from the whole
to the root, so `OccupantsDependOnWholeAt` is false and no nonlocated
necessary-underived witness exists. This rules out inferring the bridge from
the other premises. The model is structural, not a claimed actual or physically
possible spacetime interpretation.

The result reuses the original root-route support node without replacing it.
Its output is an additional **conditional attribute option**, not a new
proof of `STMT-GOD-EXISTS` or discharge of any root premise. The existing
`NecessaryClassicalGodRootFromUnderived.derive` carries one root witness
through stage-two attributes, but the public existential result does not
identify the newly unlocated witness with the classical `GOD_4` bearer, and
conjoining two different existential conclusions is invalid. The gap is
narrower than rethreading `derive`: `selectedUnlocated` is pointwise and is
the reusable entry point. A `GOD_4` consumer needs only that its own bearer
`n` satisfies `UnderivedConcreteAt m w n` in the same `m` and `w`, together
with the whole, contingency and bridge premises for the same `F`; then `n`
itself is unlocated in `F`.

**Exact remaining philosophical question:** Does the actual spacetime whole
exist as a contingent *concrete* individual, and does dimensive occupation by
a distinct concrete being imply that the selected whole grounds that being's
existence in the selected `Profile.grounds` relation, rather than merely
furnishing geometric or physical conditions? (This is not the question whether
spacetime causes the occupant's act of being, DEF-ESSE.) Any answer must also
justify the restriction to located occupants, since the unrestricted form is
inconsistent with the other premises. Give the grounding direction and a same-relation applicability
argument, or reject this conditional route and retain the relational or
derivative alternative. Neither translation symmetry nor relativity can
resolve it. Proper divine eternity remains on the independently open
Boethian-recognition route.
