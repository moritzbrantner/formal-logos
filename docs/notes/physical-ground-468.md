# A necessary physical ground against a classical ground (#468, #537)

Outcome: **conditional separators with an omission model on each side**. The theorems are
`core` and `verdicts` in `TruthCore.Results.Alternatives.PhysicalGround`, over
`TruthCore.Alternatives.PhysicalGround`. They establish conditional consequence and model
verdicts. They do not show that either ground exists or is metaphysically possible, and no
root premise changes. A necessary being does not by itself mean God: that is a checked result
below, not a concession in prose. [Research record](../../corpus/attempts/physical-ground-468.json)

## Investigation brief

- **Thesis under test.** A necessary impersonal physical reality is a coherent ultimate ground,
  and only further premises separate it from a classical one. Downstream: which steps from a
  necessary ground to the classical God are owed.
- **Strongest case for the rival.** Necessity, underivedness and capacities cost the same on each
  balance sheet (Oppy, below), physics supplies a rich structure, and a composite necessary whole
  needs no outside cause if composition is not itself a dependence.
- **Rivals.** Classical ground; part-free impersonal ground; lawlike structure with no bearer; a
  contingent brute root; two necessary roots.
- **Reused.** `CausalPowers` (#435), `NecessarilyUnderived`, `godIsSimple`, `godHasIntellect`,
  `godHasWill`, Papineau's frame and `allBased` (#419), `ExplanatorySymmetry` (#742),
  `ExplanatoryAdequacy` (#750), `RootMultiplication` (#697).
- **Decisive fork.** Whether composition needs a cause in a necessary being, and how respects are
  classified as had formally.

## The ladder: what separates the grounds

The five tables `noMaximum`, `compositeRoot`, `simpleImpersonal`, `willless` and `classicalRoute` share
`Signature`: the root exists, necessarily, unsupported at every world, with
a capacity at every world, esse-causing a contingent other. The carrier has two beings, so the
common source is over one other being. The rows in parentheses are on the same carrier or scale and
are not asserted to have `Signature`.

| Rung | Model | Premise it drops | Root |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Maximum | `noMaximum` (and `endless`) | a maximum exists | no intellect, all route premises and q.14 a.1 kept |
| Simplicity | `compositeRoot` | `STMT-COMPOSITE-HAS-A-UNITING-CAUSE` | composite, matter-form, maximum, uncaused |
| Immateriality | (deletions of composition kinds, no component, first being) | each premise named | no intellect |
| Intellect | `simpleImpersonal` | `STMT-IMMATERIAL-IS-INTELLECTUAL` | noncomposite, not matter-form, maximum, no intellect |
| Will | `willless` | `STMT-WILL-FOLLOWS-INTELLECT` | intellect, no will |
| None | `classicalRoute` | nothing | intellect and will |

So the strongest composite rival rejects exactly one premise, the uniting cause, and it keeps
necessity, underivedness, capacity and maximality. A part-free impersonal ground rejects exactly
one other. `Core` shows these premises are jointly sufficient given a maximum, so each rung is a
real obligation of the classical route. The first rung matters most: necessity supplies no maximum.
`necessaryPerSeIsMaximeEns` gives identity with any given maximum and not its existence.

**On the other side**, the premises that exclude the classical ground are also explicit. With
Papineau's three premises (the third distributive), composite physical bearers and same-bearer
realizers, a mental occurrence on a noncomposite bearer is inconsistent; one witness per deleted
member shows each is needed. So the classical ground survives by denying a named member, as the
rival survives by denying the uniting premise.

**Containment.** ST I q.4 a.2 alone does not separate. Under `onlyEsse` both survive; `withMental`
keeps only the formally mental ground and `withBodily` only the bodily one, so a classification
chosen for a candidate decides for it. `IndependentlyPure` does not consult the candidate, and its
verdict depends only on which simple beings the table has: a simple mind favours the classical
ground, a simple body the rival, and both exclude both. Whether a simple mind or body exists is
therefore what this route owes.

**Findings.**

1. *Spinoza's package is outside every ladder table.* Every ladder table has `Signature`, which
   includes an esse-caused **contingent** other. Spinoza denies that conjunct outright (I 29, I 33).
   His ground also keeps what the first rungs ask for: a maximum (I def. 6), indivisibility (I 13),
   essence–existence identity (I 20) and uniqueness of substance (I 14). It is therefore not the rival of `noMaximum` or
   `compositeRoot`. Which attribute rung it denies depends on a reading (finding 4). On contingency
   it is the **necessitarian** branch of #555:
   `necessitarian` and `necessitarianAct` in `SIMPLICITY-BRANCH-COSTS`
   (`TruthCore.Results.Modal.SimplicityCollapse.branchCosts`). That branch keeps every reason
   necessitating and gives up all contingency here. The #555 table and this reading agree on the
   cost without any new model.
2. *On reading A the established separator from the classical ground is contingency, not intellect.*
   On that reading Spinoza grants thought, and an idea of God's essence, at the level of the ground (II 1, II 3).
   What he denies is that anything follows contingently (I 29) and that the ground acts by free
   will (I 32 cor. 1). The classical side's #555 nonnecessitating branch is where the two part.
   Whether they also part at q.19 a.1 is unresolved (finding 4). So the strongest historical rival
   puts its weight on the rung that #555 isolates, and less on the q.14 a.1 rung than the ladder's
   `simpleImpersonal` suggests. On reading B intellect separates them as well.
3. *"Physical" does not mean composite even for its strongest historical exponent.* I 13 cor. and
   I 15 note assert an extended substance that is not composed of parts. This supports
   `DISAMB-PHYSICAL-GROUND` in refusing to define physical as composite. It also shows that
   `PhysicalBearersComposite` is a substantive premise that Spinoza rejects for extended substance.
   It is not a gloss on extension. His extension is not Papineau's "physical" either: it is an
   attribute expressing divine essence, not what completed physics describes. That is a third
   reading, and no `DEF-` record for it is added because no statement here uses it.
4. *"Impersonal" splits.* Reading A: `HasIntellect` (DEF-HAS-INTELLECT: intellectual cognition with
   the form of the known in the knower) holds of Spinoza's God because thought is an attribute and
   God has an idea of his essence (II 1, II 3). Reading B: it fails, because intellect in act is a
   mode in *natura naturata* (I 31), and if it belonged to the essence it would share only the name
   (I 17 note). Both are defensible, and the text supports each against a different clause of the
   definition. Neither is formalized. On A, whether the ground also has `HasWill`
   (DEF-HAS-WILL: intellectual inclination toward apprehended good) is **unresolved**. Spinoza
   denies free will (I 32 cor. 1) and that will "appertains to God's nature" any more than motion
   and rest do (I 32 cor. 2). But `DEF-HAS-WILL` and `WillFollowsIntellectReading`
   (`HasIntellect x → HasWill x`) require no contingent choice or freedom from necessitation, so
   denying free selection is not denying intellectual appetite in this repository's sense. Nor is
   it shown why reading A's grounds for admitting intellect (an idea of God's essence, II 3) would
   not equally admit an inclination following it. So reading A does not place the Spinozist ground
   on the `willless` rung; that needs a further reading establishing the absence of intellectual
   appetite, which is not supplied here. On B, it is on the `simpleImpersonal` rung,
   denying intellect to a noncomposite maximum, with the additional necessitarian cost.

5. *Spinoza supplies the collapse objection that #555 tests.* I 33 note II argues from premises he
   ascribes to his opponents: intellect, will and essence are identical and the decrees are
   eternal. A different order would then need a different intellect and will, so a different
   essence. This is the source shape of #555's route from bearer necessity to act necessity. There
   the step needs `BeingCarriesAct`, and the identity of act and being would give it
   (`docs/notes/simplicity-collapse-555.md`, finding 3). The classical reply in q.19 a.3 ad 5–6
   (below) denies that the willing's relation to creatures inherits the necessity of the act. In
   #555 terms that is the `optionalAct` or `relocated` exit, which moves the gap rather than
   closing it. So the decisive exchange between the strongest rival and the classical ground is
   read from both pinned texts, and it is already model-witnessed on both sides. This reading does
   not make I 33 note II an attributed premise of any argument.

**Not read as Spinoza's.** The esse-support relation, `ConcreteAt`, the capacity criterion and the
accessible-world semantics of `NecessaryAt` are not his vocabulary. His "could not have been produced
otherwise" is necessity from the divine nature (I 33 note 1). It is not identified with truth at
every accessible world of an analyst frame. His conclusion that God is the sole *free* cause (I 17
cor. 2, def. 7: acting from the necessity of its own nature) uses a sense of free opposite to the
classical one and is not equated with `HasWill`.

**Classical side, same question.** q.19 a.3 corpus and ad 5–6, already read in
`docs/notes/selected-effect-bridge-462.md`, is Aquinas's answer to exactly I 33's argument: necessity
of the willing in itself, not of its relation to creatures. That note records why this is a
proposal and not a demonstration that alternative decrees are possible. Both sides are therefore
read from pinned witnesses on the decisive question. The question remains open, with the
nonnecessitating and necessitarian branches both model-witnessed in #555.

## Semantic grounding

**Formal and virtual containment** (`Table`, new). *Meaning:* a fire is hot formally; a thing that
makes heat without being hot has it virtually. *Examples:* a sculptor's hand has a statue's shape
virtually; a heater with a cold casing has heat virtually. *Counterexample to identifying them:*
the two rows are independent in `table`. *Distinct from* causing, which is a third row. *Properties:*
none is assumed. Whether cause must have what it gives formally is the classification, left open.
*Abstraction:* moving from ordinary production to the first cause is exactly what is not assumed.
*Competing accounts:* a causal-powers view has no formal requirement; an emergentist denies it for
mental respects; a classical account requires it for pure perfections. **OPEN:** an independent test
of which respects are pure.

**Esse-causation read as support** (`Route.supports`). An identification for these tables only.
*Distinct from* the general relations kept apart by `DISAMB-EXISTENCE-ESSE-SUPPORT`. No structural
property is assumed. **OPEN:** whether the capacity's response and the esse-cause row are one
relation; the table has them side by side, which is compatible with either.

**Physical bearer** (`PhysicalBearersComposite`). *Example:* a body. *Counterexample to the Papineau
reading:* a part-free field state may be specifiable without mental vocabulary. *Abstraction:* the
inductive base is composed things, and whether fundamental physical entities are composed is
contested. See `DISAMB-PHYSICAL-GROUND`.

## Same-source wisdom to intrinsic self-understanding (2026-11-22, #1731)

**Research question and destination.** Does eminent possession of an intellectual
perfection establish the first source's own intentional understanding, or
only the power to produce understanding in secondary beings? This is a follow-up
to the asymmetric causal-containment investigation in draft PR #1855 and its
recorded next test. Existing target: `STMT-GOD-HAS-INTELLECT` (29818edd,
HAS_INTELLECT_1), followed conditionally by the existing verified intellect→will
consumer (090be689 → 66aecdd7). This investigation leaves those existing
statements and the GOD_4 existence witness unchanged.

**The stronger positive starting point.** The already available
`WholePowerPerfection.OwnBeingLacksNoPerfectionOfBeing` (d164b1ff) and
`PurePerfectionsPertainToBeing` give `Has g wisdom` for the *same*
`OwnBeing g`, if wisdom is independently classified as a pure perfection.
`rootPerfectionFromWholePower` has an existing conditional actual-root
application. This is independent of claiming that the source specifically
causes a cognitive episode or particular cognitive power, and may have a
stronger positive rationale than the productive-power interpretation of
"eminently": wisdom is a positive excellence of being, not an efficiency
measurement. But the generic `Has` parameter has no intrinsic-mental
semantics. `Pure wisdom` and `OfBeing wisdom` are explicitly load-bearing:
assigning that label to a determination does not establish the classification,
and an impersonal source can reject it or reject intrinsic cognitive uptake.

**Exact new Lean-only bridges** (in
`TruthCore.Causality.WholePowerPerfection.WisdomSelfUnderstanding`):

1. `WisdomSelfCapacity`: for every *OwnBeing* bearer possessing the selected
   wisdom determination, there is a correctly self-directed content and an
   intrinsic capacity to apprehend that content. The source's identity as
   subject and aboutness target is retained: `About content g` and
   `CanApprehend g content`. `CanApprehend` is a declared primitive: it means
   that g itself can apprehend the content (an exercise would be g's own act),
   not that g can cause some being to apprehend it. Nothing in its type excludes
   the productive reading, so on the *intended reading of the primitive*, a
   power to produce an *other* being with that ability does not count.
2. `SelfCapacityExercisedOrPotent`: for that same content, the subsistent
   source either actually apprehends it or has potency at the *self-understanding*
   respect. This is not the broad claim that every active productive capacity
   must continuously produce every possible effect. The separate `PureAct
   InPotency g` hypothesis eliminates the second branch. `InPotency` is
   POTENCY_2 of `DISAMB-POTENCY` (`DEF-INDEPENDENT-POTENCY`); under it the
   bridge is substantive and the dormant-capacity case below is a model. Under
   POTENCY_1 (capable and not actually at the single respect `understanding`)
   it is still not automatically excluded middle: `CanApprehend`/`Apprehends`
   are content-indexed and the respect is not, and nothing identifies them with
   `CanBe`/`InAct` at that respect. A source that apprehends one of two
   self-directed contents is actually understanding, so not in adopted potency,
   while the bridge fails for the other content. Only with a further OPEN
   identification of content with content-indexed respects (`CanApprehend g c`
   gives `CanBe g (respect c)`, `InAct g (respect c)` gives `Apprehends g c`)
   would the bridge reduce to excluded middle and the weight move into
   `PureAct`, which under POTENCY_1 forbids every unexercised `CanBe` capacity
   of the source, productive powers included if they count as `CanBe` respects.
   The generic-respect fork is recorded for
   `UnderstandingCapacityExercisedOrInPotency` in
   `DeiFiliusOperationFromIntellect`. In particular,
   `InActFormallyOrVirtually g understanding` is **not** used in place of
   actual apprehension: the inclusive-`InAct` boundary of the earlier
   operation consumer (`intellectualPureActOperatesFromCapacity`, c55e2ce2,
   which takes the same exercise-or-potency step at a generic respect of
   understanding) remains open. The new variant is content-indexed so that the
   subject, the aboutness target and the grounding relation are the same `g`.
3. `TrueSelfApprehensionGrounded`: a correct self-directed apprehension by this source
   is intrinsically grounded in the intelligible object, rather than a correct
   register reading. This does not require discursive reasoning, a distinct
   mental habit, or passive acquisition. This is an OPEN epistemic/intentional-norm application. A supplied
   truth condition and successful output alone do not establish it.

Under these premises, `intrinsicSelfKnowledgeFromWisdom` concludes
`∃ content, About content g ∧ Correct content g ∧ Apprehends g content ∧

### A positive noncognitive constitutive candidate, tested at matched scope

Continue #1984's first-agent example. Fix A, C and a bystander B;
C is the patient, with an original claim against the specified
unjustified harm. A and C have no earlier finite intentional grantor
of that initial standing. Both actions are physically feasible.
Assume the particular right is waivable by C, that a consent token
by C is valid and one by B on B's own behalf is not, and that no
competing justification is present. This is a bounded prospective
claim, not a universal ethics of consent or a permission verdict
about every other norm.

A strong impersonal account offers:
- a cognitive basis K in the creature's organized activity: its
  representational roles have correctness conditions anchored in
  the actual circumstances, can be incorrect, and the relevant
  process uses those representations and an inferential procedure
  in directing the creature's own action;
- a normative basis J: a substantive standing principle linking
  the patient's rational status and good to a right to refuse this
  treatment. This is a constitutive normative claim, not an inference
  from the patient's ability to utter words or exercise physical force.

The prior [stable factual-guidance test](intentionality-943.md#multiple-functions-and-stable-factual-guidance-2026-11-14)
gives a concrete functional account of accuracy which need not
identify truth with a single benefit. The present test adds
claimant-sensitive authorization, misrepresentation and the
provenance of an actual decision. It is not a second generic
accuracy-versus-reference theorem.

Let actual harm and consent be separately fixed facts. The selected
norm says there is a directed duty to refrain iff the harm is present
and valid consent is absent. Represented harm and represented consent
may each differ from the actual facts. A can prefer proceeding,
without that preference changing the actual duty. Compare a mechanism
which explicitly computes the represented duty and uses it to decide,
a bypass directly keyed by the same inputs without a separately
tokened duty conclusion, and a benefit-only mechanism ignoring
authorization. All have access to the same represented facts where
relevant. This does **not** encode consciousness, original meaning
or metaphysically valid rights: the content interpretation and J
are supplied to the program. It tests the positive replacement's
functional contribution and its remaining burden rather than
pretending to implement genuine thought in a small program.

```python
from itertools import product

def case_duty(harm, consent):
    return harm and consent != "C"

def reason_route(represented_harm, represented_consent, wants_proceed):
    valid_consent = represented_consent == "C"
    conclusion = represented_harm and not valid_consent
    return conclusion, (not conclusion and wants_proceed)

def bypass(represented_harm, represented_consent, wants_proceed):
    return wants_proceed and (
        not represented_harm or represented_consent == "C")

def benefit_only(represented_harm, wants_proceed):
    return wants_proceed and not represented_harm

rows = []
for harm, consent, cue, report, wants_proceed in product(
    (False, True), ("none", "B", "C"), (False, True),
    ("none", "B", "C"), (False, True)
):
    actual = case_duty(harm, consent)
    believed, action = reason_route(cue, report, wants_proceed)
    assert action == bypass(cue, report, wants_proceed)
    accurate = (harm, consent) == (cue, report)
    if accurate:
        assert believed == actual
        assert not (actual and action)
    rows.append((actual, believed, action,
                 benefit_only(cue, wants_proceed), accurate))

assert len(rows) == 72
assert sum(x[4] for x in rows) == 12
assert sum(x[0] != x[1] for x in rows) == 32
assert sum(x[2] != x[3] for x in rows) == 6
assert reason_route(True, "C", True) == (False, True)
assert reason_route(True, "B", True) == (True, False)
assert reason_route(True, "B", False) == (True, False)
assert reason_route(False, "B", True) == (False, True)
```

Executed all 72 configurations locally. The reason-route and bypass
outputs agree in every row, including errors. In all 12 accurate
input rows the reason-route decision respects the stipulated duty.
In 32 of the 72 rows the represented duty differs from the actual
one. The benefit-only mechanism differs from the reason route in
six rows: it fails to distinguish the relevant authorization in its
choice policy. That failure is **not** proof that it commits a moral
wrong in those rows; it refrains where the selected claim does not
require refraining, and may do so for other good reasons. Counts
describe the fixture, not frequencies in nature or world probabilities.

The two routes have identical extensional functions. The displayed
intermediate variable is evidence of a distinct *explicit
factorization* of the implemented computation, not sufficient
evidence of an apprehended reason, nor a proof that direct evaluation
cannot be intellectual. Compiler inlining would not automatically
destroy understanding; whether a real process exercises the
subject's own content-guided power requires causal and semantic
evidence beyond variable names. The implementation has an actual
human/analyst designer; do not treat it as a demonstrated designer-free
genesis, a metaphysically possible noncognitive first source, or a
reduction of genuine understanding. This extends the prior basing
distinction at a narrower matched moral target.

### 3. Support the purity/compatibility premise without assuming a personal source

Use an actual subject's immediate self-apprehension as the positive
content, rather than the acquisition of information. In familiar
cognition, acquisition, use of a sensory intermediary, and understanding
what is acquired are distinct jobs. A learner who succeeds in
understanding does not cease to understand upon completing the search.
The positive content is the subject's intelligible apprehension,
not its prior ignorance or the transition out of that ignorance.
This gives prima facie reason for considering nonacquisitive
understanding rather than classifying all intellectual perfection as
essentially receptive.

At the ultimate-source level, the nonacquisitive candidate is an immanent
act of understanding whose own primary object is already actual in the
subject. The source need not make a second bearer actual in order to
know itself. This avoids the inference from the capacity to produce a
knower to the source's own intellectual actuality. Reuse the existing
[whole-power wisdom result](../../TruthCore/Results/Causality/WholePowerPerfection/Solution.lean)
`actualRootSelfKnowledgeFromWisdom` and the
[primary-object route](../../TruthCore/Results/Causality/IntentionalExemplarExtension/Solution.lean);
neither derives this interpretation from a Boolean flag for cognition.
Both require the explicitly supplied cognitive bridges.

The strongest relevant objection says that real noesis is intrinsically
articulated: a subject grasps an intelligible object, possibly under
distinct contents, and such articulation is incompatible with strict
simplicity. The existing C4 controls separate the *constitutive* version
of this objection (`SourceNoesisRequiresRealPlurality`) from the broader
Plotinian-style challenge. The present extension tests the roles more
directly; it does not import the historical One into the Thomistic
ActualRoot domain.

Fix one actual source in a toy interpretation, identify its subject,
primary object, form and operation references, and retain one real
constituent. Use two distinct self-directed contents with separately
specified truth conditions: the absence of receipt and the absence of
composition. On the selected actual profile both are true; across
comparison profiles their truth conditions differ. The probes are
alternative assignments for semantic comparison, not accessible worlds
of this necessary source. Both contents can be associated with the same
act reference without identifying the contents, truth conditions, or
their distinct intentional roles. All references are actual by
stipulation in the selected interpretation. The source's own
apprehension, veridicality and nonreceptive realization are also
stipulated; no proof of genuine noesis is obtained from these assignments.

A general claim that different true apprehended contents require
different real constituents entails an injection from the apprehended
content inventory into the real-constituent inventory. In this
interpretation the two content references map to the one act; the
injection fails while the indicated roles and truth-condition
distinctions hold. This does not prove the broader claim false of
real noesis: the opponent may supply a metaphysical necessity of
distinct intentional modes or constitutive constituents which the
assignment does not enforce. The test identifies the needed additional
bridge and makes its target more precise than "there are two words, so
there are two things." Merely distinguishing the primary object's
truth conditions or the subject and object roles does not establish
real composition.

There is a symmetric positive challenge to the blanket articulation
objection. A putatively simple impersonal source is also intelligible
under different descriptions and may exercise one power with several
determinate effects. If every distinction of intelligible role as such
implies real internal multiplicity, that general reason threatens the
simplicity of the impersonal producer as well. An opponent can instead
defend a specifically *intrinsic cognitive* articulation principle,
distinguishing it from extrinsic causal relations and a finite
observer's descriptions. That is a serious substantive reply. The
personal account must then explain one act's intrinsic apprehension
of distinct intelligible aspects, not merely appeal to multiple
descriptions by observers. The toy control neither equates intentional
roles with causal roles nor shows either account metaphysically possible.

### 4. Executed local diagnostics

The following JavaScript reproduces the checked comparison. Bit masks
only encode the selected inventory. The same-realizer assignments test
only structural consistency of the displayed conditions, not metaphysical
possibility, actuality of a proposed first being, or any existing Lean
theorem's validity.

```javascript
const assert=(b,msg)=>{if(!b)throw Error(msg)};
const vals=[0,1,2,3,4,5,6,7], wisdom=4;
const subset=(a,b)=>(a&b)===a;
let cases=0,strict=0,local=0,cross=0;
for(let mask=1;mask<256;mask++){
  const F=vals.filter(x=>mask&(1<<x));
  for(const b of F){
    const max=!F.some(t=>t!==b&&subset(b,t));
    const ext=F.some(t=>subset(b|wisdom,t));
    const elsewhere=F.some(t=>t&wisdom);
    assert(!max||!ext||!!(b&wisdom),"maximal + local compatibility implies wisdom");
    if(!(b&wisdom)&&max&&elsewhere&&!ext)cross++;
    if(!(b&wisdom)&&ext){assert(!max,"extension not maximal");local++}
    if(max)strict++;
    cases++;
  }
}
const probes=[{received:false,composed:false},{received:true,composed:false},
  {received:false,composed:true},{received:true,composed:true}];
const contents=[p=>!p.received,p=>!p.composed];
assert(probes.some(p=>contents[0](p)!==contents[1](p)),"distinct truth conditions");
assert(contents.every(f=>f(probes[0])),"actual both true");
const n="source", bearer=n, object=n, form=n, operation=n, constituents=[n];
const aware=contents.map((f,i)=>({content:i,truth:f(probes[0]),
  subject:bearer,object,form,operation,realizer:n}));
assert(aware.every(r=>r.subject===r.object&&r.object===r.form&&
  r.form===r.operation&&r.truth),"same bearer, act, object, form");
assert(aware.length===2&&new Set(aware.map(r=>r.realizer)).size===1&&
  constituents.length===1,"non-injective content realization");
console.log({cases,strict,local,cross,probeRows:probes.map(p=>contents.map(f=>f(p)))});
```

Results: cases = 1024, strict (maximal profile cases) = 369,
local = 376, cross = 80; truth-condition rows TT, FT, TF, FF.
All assertions passed in the analyst JavaScript execution.
