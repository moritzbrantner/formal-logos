# Goodness definitions: due actuality and the explicit remainder

The owner’s current direction in [#1116](https://github.com/moritzbrantner/truth-core/issues/1116)
is to formulate “goodness is the actuality due to a thing’s form and end” as a
proposition and investigate rival definitions. It supersedes the earlier idea
of adopting the definition. No floor item or definition is adopted here. [Research record](../../corpus/attempts/goodness-form-end-forks-1116.json)

The analyst reconstruction is `STMT-GOODNESS-DUE-TO-FORM-AND-END`:

```
∀ x p, GoodIn x p ↔ (DueFor (form x) (endOf (form x)) p ∧ Has x p)
```

`GoodIn` remains independent, so this can fail. The form/end standard is an
explicit supplied interpretation, not an inference from whatever is actual or
approved. `DueFor` expresses appropriateness of a perfection, not an efficient
causal relation. Whether natural kinds have these forms/ends and why their
standards govern real evaluations remain open applicability questions.

This is a per-perfection, extensional reconstruction. It does not identify
property intensions, define aggregate degrees, determine good simpliciter,
or derive categorical practical authority.

## Two different coverage questions

`GOODNESS-FORM-END-DEFINITION-FORK` proves, under explicit excluded middle:

1. Goodness coextends with due actuality; or
2. some goodness exceeds due actuality (`SurplusGood`); or
3. some due actuality is omitted from goodness (`OmittedDueGood`).

The last two cells can overlap. Excluding both entails the first, but their
exclusion is equivalent to the target in this profile, not independent
philosophical support. This structural partition is not a list of named
worldviews.

The separate named investigation uses three explicit proposals: due actuality,
a feature-based criterion, and a fixed-assessor approval criterion. These are
analyst definition propositions, not full accounts attributed to historical
schools. `DefinitionSpace` records their extensions and the chosen admissible
class. The weakest sufficient coverage premise says every admissible proposal
is **pointwise coextensive** with one of them. A renamed or intensionally
different but coextensive account is already covered. Strong candidate-identity
coverage is preserved separately with a checked `strengthens` registration;
the core elimination does not need it.

`STMT-GOODNESS-ADDITIONAL-SEMANTIC-DEFINITION` explicitly says that an admissible
proposal has an extension different from every named account.
`GOODNESS-SEMANTIC-NAMED-COVERAGE-FORK` proves semantic coverage or that explicit
remainder under excluded middle. It establishes neither philosophical branch.

`GOODNESS-FROM-NAMED-DEFINITION-ELIMINATION` then derives the owner’s proposition
from exactly four open inputs:

- semantic named coverage;
- at least one admissible account correctly describing the profile’s goodness;
- actual correctness failure of the feature proposal;
- actual correctness failure of the approval proposal.

The correct-account input prevents a vacuous “only due actuality remains” when
nothing admitted is correct. Mere disfavor, unexplained authority or an
unanswered objection is not the correctness negation used by this theorem.

Both scope branches have fixed models. In the covered model the due account is
correct. In the remainder model an explicit fourth proposal is correct, the
feature and approval proposals fail, and the due-actuality thesis fails too.
That fourth extension is knife-mass only: it differs from due actuality at knife
mass, and from both other accounts at counterweight mass. It is an actual
formal witness, not an unspecified possible millionth option or an alias.
Finding no further proposal in a source search would not prove coverage.

## Exact connection to the current root leaf

`STMT-GOODNESS-IS-ACTUALITY` means unrestricted `GoodIn x p ↔ Has x p`. Given the
new thesis, that holds **iff every actual perfection is due** (`AllActualitiesDue`).
The finite due-actuality model has actual knife mass outside the cutter/cutting
standard, so the new thesis holds and the old unrestricted coextension fails.
The all-actuality model gives the converse omission control: unrestricted
coextension holds while the new thesis fails.

`GOODNESS-DUE-ACTUALITY-WITH-DUE-BRIDGE-ENTAILS-UNRESTRICTED` registers this
exact two-premise connection to the existing root leaf. Its openly assumed
inputs and the underlying named-account premises become visible on the root
frontier; they are not omitted to keep bare counts unchanged.

Thus the new proposition is not silently substituted for the old route leaf.
The old `AQUINAS-ST-I-Q5-A1-GOODNESS-FROM-DESIRABILITY` proof remains intact:
it composes three open biconditionals. Neither that source-derived reading nor
its premises are replaced by a new analytic definition. A route using the
restricted thesis must discharge the due-status bridge or explicitly restrict
its perfection domain. That is the exact remaining route connection.

## Ideal consensus comparison (#1116, 2026-11-08)

`GoodnessFormEndIdealConsensus` compares the existing due-actuality thesis with
an analyst ideal-attitude reduction: aspect goodness iff every supplied ideal
assessor approves. This uses universal agreement, rather than a current urge or
one arbitrarily selected person's approval. The class and its idealization are
stipulated inputs; no historical theory is attributed this definition.

The sufficient bridges are `Acceptance` (due actualities receive every approval)
and `SoundWitness` (each aspect has some assessor whose approval implies its
due actuality). To derive due actuality from goodness, instantiate unanimous
approval at that aspect's sound assessor. For the reverse direction, acceptance
supplies consensus and the reduction supplies goodness. The sound assessor may
vary by aspect; universal assessor infallibility is unnecessary. None of these
open bridges establishes a real ideal observer, a categorical reason, or property
identity. This is a structural conditional comparison, not an independent
argument establishing the due thesis.

The unchanged knife/counterweight standard supplies the positive model. A
permissive assessor agrees with unrestricted actuality but approves the knife's
non-due mass, so acceptance and reduction do not imply soundness or the thesis.
A rejecting assessor omits the knife's due existence: reduction and soundness
do not imply acceptance or the thesis. An assessor tracking due actuality with
surplus goodness satisfies both semantic bridges but fails reduction and the
thesis. Finally, an empty class approves everything unanimously but cannot
supply the sound witness on this nonempty domain. These are explicit finite
interpretations, not real-world or metaphysical possibility claims.

Rejected inference: form/end semantics by itself refutes ideal-attitude
reduction. The positive model permits both accounts to agree extensionally.
Rejected shortcut: take unanimity alone as a correctness guarantee; the empty
class and permissive model block it. No new coverage or exclusivity claim about
all philosophical accounts is made: the existing definition space's `.other`
option and open coverage remain. The existing arbitrary coextension bridge
still requires `AllActualitiesDue`; no generic is/ought result is repeated and
no practical-authority bridge is supplied. Real warrant for due standards and
for the idealization/correctness bridges remains the investigation's blocker.

It does not define the independent `GoodIn` relation or establish
`DueActualityThesis`.

### Why the organization could matter to due status

The explanatory proposal has three steps. First, identify a continuing bearer
and the differentiated processes by which it renews its own constraints: a
boundary preserves usable resources, catalytic structures replenish them, and
those resources finance replacement of boundary and catalysts. A functional
description then explains their reciprocal dependence instead of merely
attaching an evaluator's favored label to an observed output.

Second, use interventions and environmental challenges to distinguish the
capacity of a part from its present output and from the survival of the whole.
A failed part can coexist with a surviving bearer if another pathway takes
over. Conversely, a capable part can be inactive because its output is not
currently needed. A standard that calls both cases healthy merely because the
bearer survives loses an explanatory distinction about its future responses.

Third, the candidate normative step is that a capacity sustaining this
bearer's characteristic, differentiated activity is an appropriate perfection
of it, within the stated developmental and environmental scope. The mechanism
gives that step a reason: losing the capacity removes a demonstrable way the
bearer maintains itself across relevant conditions. It does not make the step
true by definition. Which activities belong to the appropriate reference class,
and why their support is a good for this bearer, still require defense. The
next example makes the causal part inspectable and exposes that remaining
choice.

### Minimal mechanism: one bearer, renewal, reserve and compensation

Fix one modeled bearer `o` throughout each history, without fission, fusion or
a switch from an individual to its population. Track

```
state = (reserve, barrier, native_site, backup_site, recruited)
reserve ∈ {0,1,2,3,4}; the other entries ∈ {0,1}
```

The environment supplies substrate and replacement material throughout. A
separate input `help ∈ {0,1,2}` supplies usable support from outside this bearer;
the bearer does not produce or maintain its source. Native capacity
`cap ∈ {0,1,2}` and recruitment availability `regulator ∈ {0,1}` are fixed within
a history. `drive ∈ {0,1}` distinguishes exercise from possession of native
capacity. An intervention can remove `loss ∈ {0,2}` units of reserve. An emitted
signal has no causal return path within this environmental scope.

One step is:

```
inflow = cap * drive * native_site + 2 * recruited * backup_site + help
drain = 2 + 2 * (1 - barrier)
reserve' = min(4, max(0, reserve - loss + inflow - drain))
rebuilt = 1 if reserve' > 0 else 0
barrier' = native_site' = backup_site' = rebuilt
recruited' = regulator * rebuilt * (1 if reserve' <= 2 else 0)
```

This is an analyst's finite structural surrogate, not a fitted biochemical
model. Drain includes the stipulated cost of operation and renewal. Replacement
requires positive remaining reserve; recruitment responds with a one-step
delay. A defective native site can be rebuilt with the same deficient capacity,
as the capacity parameter is held fixed. The state records role occupancy,
while replacement tokens can carry distinct time indices. Thus the example
does not preserve a timeless constituent set. Its assigned bearer identity is
an input, not a conclusion about substantial unity; after collapse, tracking
the record does not assert that a living organism remains.

The roles differ within the equations: the barrier prevents
leakage, the native and backup sites replenish reserve, and recruitment changes
which pathway operates. Reserve in turn supports their renewal. This is the
minimal causal content missing from a bare `SelfMaintaining` predicate. It is
still a coarse description: applying `ArrangementDynamics` to a biological
realization would require evidence that omitted microstates preserve these
transitions.

Starting at `(4,1,1,1,0)`, with no reserve loss, gives:

| Case | Inputs differing from `cap=2, drive=1, regulator=1, help=0` | Reserve at times 0 through 5 | What the comparison discriminates |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Normal native role | None | `4,4,4,4,4,4` | Native inflow meets maintenance demand. |
| Uncompensated failure | `cap=0, regulator=0` | `4,2,0,0,0,0` | The loss has a causal consequence under a stated regime. |
| Partial performance | `cap=1, regulator=0` | `4,3,2,1,0,0` | Deficit begins before collapse; binary survival misses its onset. |
| Compensated failure | `cap=0` | `4,2,2,2,2,2` | Recruitment preserves this bearer despite lost native competence. |
| External help | `cap=0, help=2` | `4,4,4,4,4,4` | Support restores reserve without restoring native competence. |
| Healthy inactivity | `drive=0, help=2` | `4,4,4,4,4,4` | Zero native output does not entail lost native competence. |

The last case and `cap=0, drive=0, help=2` have identical state traces. A native
demand probe, setting `drive=1, help=0, regulator=0`, separates them. The relevant
capacity is therefore a response disposition of the specified mechanism, not
its currently observed output. No invisible normative variable is doing this
work.

Compensation also has limits that the equations expose. Starting with reserve
2 and an unrecruited backup, `cap=0` collapses before recruitment can help.
Starting with reserve 4, the compensated trace survives indefinitely without
losses, but a loss of 2 at the third step gives `4,2,2,0,0,0`. The same shock
with normal capacity gives `4,4,4,2,4,4`. More strongly, with normal capacity
the set

```
{ (4,1,1,1,0), (2,1,1,1,1) }
```

is closed under either permitted loss at every step. Its four transition cases
support an induction over arbitrary finite shock histories in this toy model.
Native competence thus contributes to a specified robustness capacity even
when it is unnecessary for undisturbed persistence. This supplies a positive
reason for retaining its performance standard after compensation.

Changing the incidental signal leaves every transition unchanged. Its current
emission therefore supplies no maintenance role in this scope. If an observer
uses it to adjust external support, the enlarged system has a new return path;
the earlier conclusion does not survive that scope change automatically.
Similarly, external help is useful to `o`, but its source is not one of `o`'s
renewed structures. A broader organism–device or ecological system would be a
different bearer and requires a new argument.

### A biological discriminator already available

The distinction between preserved growth, native competence and regulated
inactivity is empirically tractable. Wakao et al., [*Discovery of
photosynthesis genes through whole-genome sequencing of acetate-requiring
mutants of Chlamydomonas reinhardtii*](https://journals.plos.org/plosgenetics/article?id=10.1371/journal.pgen.1009725),
2021, the results for LPA3/PSBP4, Fig. 5 and the accompanying methods,
report acetate-requiring `lpa3` mutants with reduced photosystem-II efficiency.
Complementation restores the relevant growth/efficiency phenotype. Nutritional
support and restoration of photosynthetic competence are therefore
distinguishable outcomes. The reported growth comparison explicitly changes
acetate and illumination conditions; it is not evidence that environment is
irrelevant.

The contrasting control is Heifetz et al., [*Effects of Acetate on Facultative
Autotrophy in Chlamydomonas reinhardtii Assessed by Photosynthetic Measurements
and Stable Isotope Analyses*](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC58978/),
2000, abstract and methods describing growth conditions. Under
their specified acetate, light and carbon-dioxide conditions, the authors report
reduced photosynthetic carbon fixation and oxygen evolution without reduced
growth or photosystem-II efficiency. This supports a live alternative to
“lower current output means damaged capacity.” Only the abstract and methods
were consulted for this paper; no independent reanalysis of its measurements
is claimed.

These reports distinguish strain-level capacities and environmental responses;
they do not follow one identical cell through all interventions or establish
metaphysical persistence. Nor does acetate literally instantiate the toy's
already usable reserve input: its use involves endogenous metabolism. The
comparison tests the distinction, not the numerical recurrence. A relevant
next experiment would retain a defined cell class and developmental stage,
track the same bearer where practicable, and measure both challenged capacity
and continued maintenance under support and its withdrawal.

### Candidate interpretation of `DueFor`

Fix the toy reference class `K`: mature, nonreproducing bearers with the three
renewable sites, reserve bound, costs and renewal law above. Members can differ
in native capacity and recruitment availability while retaining that
architecture. Material and substrate supply are fixed. The environmental
challenge family removes either 0 or 2 reserve units per step; supplied support
is specified separately. No developmental transition is modeled.

The native reference regime has `drive=1, help=0, regulator=0, loss=0, recruited=0`, with
barrier and both sites present. Its broader regulation regime permits
recruitment and the stated reserve challenges. These are two regimes of the
same stipulated class; compensation is not permission to change the bearer or
redefine its class after observing failure. A biological application needs its
own independently characterized class and stage.

Interpret the biological part of `form` as that class and its organization;
interpret the relevant `endOf` as continued differentiated maintenance of this
bearer across the stated challenges. Consider the perfection `native_capacity`
whose possession means capacity 2. In the reference regime, inflow 2 exactly
meets drain 2; capacity 1 or 0 depletes any positive reserve. In the regulation
regime, capacity 2 also sustains the explicit shock invariant. The candidate
`DueFor` judgment is that native competence belongs to the appropriate capacity
standard for this organization in that scope. The threshold is calculated
from the mechanism once the class and scope are fixed.

For this candidate perfection, `Has` concerns actual possession of a capacity.
It does not require present exercise. A separate perfection concerning current
performance would need its own conditions; otherwise healthy inactivity would
be mislabeled a defect. An aided defective bearer can accordingly lack this
due capacity while possessing other due capacities and continuing to exist.
No aggregate verdict that the bearer is wholly bad follows.

This is a substantive proposal for interpreting a restricted part of an
existing frame. It has not been installed as a Lean definition or attributed
to an author. If `GoodIn` independently tracks the appropriate biological
standard, `DueActualityThesis` identifies the corresponding due, possessed
capacities as aspect goods. The mechanism does not independently prove that
biconditional, settle property identity or show that every actuality is due.
The existing `AllActualitiesDue` bridge to unrestricted actuality remains
additional. The [natural-goodness/function-and-authority
investigation](natural-goodness-function-and-authority.md) retains the separate
work on human goods, rational regulation and categorical practical authority.

The positive organizational case improves on pure actual-success criteria:
it explains why compensation does not erase every loss and why inactivity need
not be dysfunction. The selected-effect reply survives and can complement it
by stabilizing the reference class. Neither current organization nor historical
selection wins merely because the other requires an applicability premise.
Their potential disagreement is precise: whether due role persists when
historical lineage and present organizational contribution come apart.

In particular, the `SelectedFor` relation in the existing Papineau consumer
formalization concerns etiological selection for a response. It is not supplied
by a current replenishment loop. Bird's argument suggests a further connection
to powers and laws in #743: selection can help explain why a capacity realized
in different ways forms a scientifically useful unity. The toy recurrence
alone establishes neither that historical premise nor the essential identity
of a power. This connection is retained as an explanatory question, not a new
bridge theorem.

The provisional judgment is positive but scoped. Present organization can
provide substantive, intervention-sensitive grounds for some biological
good-for judgments. In the tested mechanism, native capacity preserves a
robustness profile that compensation alone does not preserve, so its evaluation
is more than a report of current survival. The biological reports distinguish
nutritional support and restoration of measured photosynthetic competence,
and show reduced photosynthetic output with preserved measured PSII efficiency
under specified conditions. They do not by themselves establish reversible
downregulation of the whole capacity, the complete `DueFor` interpretation or
the general goodness thesis.

The live blocker is independent warrant for the reference class, environmental
challenge set and bearer. The next test should compare, within one specified
class and stage, a supported defect, a reversible downregulation and a stable
endogenous reorganization that performs equally well under the same challenges.
If the reorganization retains the historical native role but removes every
maintenance disadvantage of losing it, organization and selected effect may
give different verdicts; if the relevant disadvantage remains, the organizational
case gains empirical support. Capacity probes and causal interventions can
resolve that issue without treating current output as the standard.

This individual-maintenance scope also does not determine reproductive,
lifespan or interpersonal goods. Expanding it requires an explicit end and
bearer argument; a shift to lineage maintenance must not silently replace
`o` by its descendants. Nothing in this investigation closes a root premise,

### What material organization positively explains

The positive local account specifies how a capacity serves its bearer without
making a representation of that service part of the mechanism; it leaves
ultimate representation open. In the existing recurrence, replenishment
supports the renewal of sites and barrier; those structures enable further
replenishment. Removing native competence changes the challenged performance
of this same bearer. Altering the incidental emission does not. These are
different causal roles before anyone approves their outcomes. Calling the
first role good for the bearer has a substantive proposed basis: it sustains
the bearer’s characteristic activity under independently specified demands.
The remaining evaluative and applicability commitments stay explicit in the
earlier `DueFor` proposal.

Historical selection supplies another explanation. An effect can account for
the reproduction of a trait because ancestors producing that effect left
descendants. The effect need not be anticipated. Wright's *Functions* (1973),
[pp. 156–162](https://mechanism.ucsd.edu/bill/teaching/w10/wright.functions.%201973.pdf),
connects a function both to a trait's causal presence and to an effect of its
presence, distinguishing pumping from incidental heart noise. Millikan's
*In Defense of Proper Functions* (1989),
[pp. 288–289 and 294–295](https://joelvelasco.net/teaching/3334/millikan89-in_defense_proper_functions.pdf),
adds reproductive history and derived functions, preserving functions of
malformed tokens unable to perform them. These inspected primary ranges are
consultation evidence, not new pinned source claims.

Neither explanation is merely an assertion that useful results happened.
But they answer different questions. Selection of an effect presupposes
relevant prior performances or a producing mechanism with an appropriate
history; it cannot make the future benefit cause the first occurrence.
A novel token can nevertheless have a derived function, and a newly organized
capacity can immediately contribute to present maintenance. It would therefore
be wrong both to credit selection with explaining all initial directedness
and to deny functions to every novel token. Nor does the historical account
explain only origins: the earlier Neander consultation records how selected
functions organize explanations of present mechanisms and defective variants.

Material realization is also weaker than metaphysical exhaustiveness. A
physiological explanation can be accepted by a physicalist, a powers realist
or a hylomorphist. Its success does not establish that no irreducible formal
principle or ultimate intelligence exists. Conversely, specifying an actual
organization and its causal capacities is substantially more than describing
bare matter without properties and asking how it could have ends.

### The bearer, the standard and the competing good

Three restrictions prevent this positive account from becoming an unrestricted
purpose claim.

**Persistence alone is insufficient.** A stable equilibrium is not thereby a
functioning organism. The existing recurrence distinguishes continued
replacement, differentiated causal contributions and competence under demands;
it does not reward every stable state. Mossio, Saborido and Moreno's already
consulted §§4.1–4.2, pp. 822–827, likewise distinguish minimal self-maintenance,
including a flame, from the differentiated organization required by their
account of component functions. Even that richer organization does not make
its preservation an unconditional good. The bearer, developmental stage and
challenge set must be warranted without selecting them simply because they
deliver the desired verdict. The earlier equally robust reorganization test
remains relevant to that obligation.

**Contribution is weaker than optimum.** In
`Article3QuintaVia.lean`, `Best x e` means that e is what is best for x.
The renewal account supports a specified contribution or capacity standard;
it does not establish that this end is best for the same x under the Fifth
Way's selected standard. Best must not silently weaken to beneficial or
strengthen to the greatest feasible good overall.
Historical reproductive advantage is likewise neither universal optimality
nor an all-things-considered benefit to every individual. The local Fifth Way
still needs its actual `Best` interpretation and evidence. Moreover, x must
stay fixed. Moving from the heart's role to the organism's benefit, or from
an organism to its descendants, requires an explicit relation between those
bearers. The scope of the good cannot change unnoticed.

**Different organizations can have conflicting goods.** Tumor growth makes
this a concrete challenge to the inference from causal maintenance to an
unqualified good. Kim and colleagues (1993),
[*Inhibition of vascular endothelial growth factor-induced angiogenesis suppresses tumour growth in vivo*](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/7683111/),
abstract, report that VEGF-specific antibody treatment reduced vascular
density and growth of transplanted human tumors in mice without reducing
the tumor-cell growth rate in vitro. The consulted abstract supports a
specific causal role for vascular support in that setting. It does not
establish that every tumor is an organizationally closed individual or give
the tumor's maintenance normative priority.

The analyst application is conditional: where a tumor's organization has
maintenance-serving processes, their success can support that organization
while damaging the host. This does not make the relative causal facts
subjective. It shows why specifying the beneficiary is indispensable. Host
development, integration and capacities can ground a biological diagnosis of
defect relative to the host, while somatic proliferation is successful at a
different level. Neither verdict alone ranks all competing bearers or yields
categorical moral authority. Relative dysfunction can be real independently
of anyone's desire without making maintenance the highest, morally required
or greatest feasible good. A pathological but stable organization exposes
the missing beneficiary and priority premises in the simple *whatever
maintains itself is good without qualification* shortcut; it does not defeat
every biological good-for judgment.

Reproduction requires the same discipline. Saborido, Mossio and Moreno's
*Biological Organization and Cross-Generation Functions* (2011),
[§3.3, pp. 598–602](https://www.ias-research.net/wp-content/uploads/2019/02/Saborido_41241903.pdf),
proposes an organization extending across individual lifetimes through
causal transmission and material continuity. This is an inspected positive
extension beyond the individual-maintenance account, not a newly pinned
claim or an established identity of organism and lineage. Its wider temporal
bearer must be defended explicitly. It cannot silently replace the fixed o
of the earlier recurrence.

### Why the same explanation does not transfer unchanged to the source

The organizational explanation concerns a dependent bearer: its parts help
maintain the organization that produces and maintains them. The proposed
ultimate source n has a different relation to its recipients. On the retained
underived, invariant-source reading, n does not persist by replacing its parts
through a return from contingent recipient x. Necessity alone is not being
used to prove independence; the source's underived status and the specified
productive relation do the relevant work. Applying the biological return
loop to n would change that account.

This identifies a particular failed transfer. The heart's contribution to
the organism's persistence can explain its local function. That explanation
does not show why n produces an organism, why this recipient's good governs
n's production, or why n favors that good over a conflicting one. The
beneficiary and causal role have changed. This limitation does not subtract
anything from the local explanation or require it to explain the cosmos
before it can explain circulation.

The strongest impersonal reply preserves the difference. Its concrete nature
actually produces organisms with differentiated powers and their own
conditions of flourishing. On its organizational account, their ends are
intrinsic to the resulting organization, rather than labels externally
assigned by an observer. In this sense the source gives things finality by
producing beings whose activities
have such organization. It need not benefit from them, and it need not
apprehend their goods for those goods to be real. Granting genuine reasoners
among its effects also prevents an automatic slide from a noncognitive source
to merely simulated reasoning in its recipients.

This reply answers how recipient finality can be materially realized and
causally produced without a represented purpose in each component. It leaves
a further ultimate claim: n's own productive nature is selectively directed
toward independently identified recipient goods. That profile needs positive
content and warrant, beyond naming whichever output occurs good. The local
examples motivate noncognitive directedness; they do not establish this
underived productive profile, its full simplicity or its actual existence.
The rival may defend that nature directly or cumulatively and may take its
actual nonnecessitating exercise as primitive. Equal permission to stop does
not imply equal evidence for every proposed stopping point.

### Comparison and revision conditions

Actual intellectual direction offers a distinct positive explanation: the
source apprehends a recipient's good and that apprehension supplies the
formative measure of its giving. Ordinary practical production gives this
explanatory form defeasible warrant. The additional relation is informative
where it is actually warranted; neither the creature's benefit nor the
creature's own reason use establishes it at n. It also need not consist of
an endless sequence of prior decisions. The personal and impersonal accounts
receive the same opportunity to defend one actual directed exercise.

The revised comparison is therefore more specific than generic parity.
Materially embodied functions have positive local support from causal
organization, historical explanation and discriminating interventions.
Their transfer to an ultimate recipient-good-directed source remains open.
Intellectual authorship has positive local support from actual practical
agency; its transfer to this source and this productive exercise remains
open too. Neither local account excludes the other at the ultimate level.
The existing teleonomy countermodels establish their stated formal
non-entailments; governed-mechanism compatibility is not a causal model of
