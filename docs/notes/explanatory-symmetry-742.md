# Selecting one outcome over another with a reason true at both (#742)

Outcome: a **certified incompatible core with surviving countermodels**, not a defended
standard. The theorems are `core` and `comparison` in
`TruthCore.Results.Explanation.ExplanatorySymmetry.Solution`, over
`TruthCore.Explanation.ExplanatorySymmetry`, the #755 `Arena`, the #750 rivals and the #736
`SameEvidence`. Each permits the Mathlib three, because the module uses Mathlib preorders and

standard, exclude no position unconditionally, and no model's metaphysical possibility. [Research record](../../corpus/attempts/explanatory-symmetry-742.json)

## Investigation brief

- **Thesis under test.** A nonnecessitating ground can adequately select one outcome over
  another. Downstream, this is the crux between modal collapse and brute contingency.
- **Strongest case for it.** Ordinary favouring does not necessitate: reasons incline without
  compelling, and weights favour without excluding.
- **Strongest rivals.** Leibnizian renewal of the demand at every contingent detail; a brute
  stop; a noncontrastive reading of explanation; necessitarianism.
- **Reused.** `Arena`, `tokenExpand`, `ReasonClosed`, the #750 rivals and `core_vary`;
  `SameEvidence`; the #755 `ground`. `CollapseCore` is compared and not consumed.
- **Decisive fork.** Whether favouring may vary with the outcome across worlds that agree on the
  whole base.

## Findings

1. **Selection in every history gives what necessitation gives, without saying so.** The core
   has no clause saying a reason necessitates. Yet with a base true at all accessible worlds,
   base-fixed favouring, and net selection and exactly one true field member at each, no field
   member is contingent. This does not define adequate as deterministic: the bar comes from the
   rival's favouring reason being present in the actual history too.
2. **Nonnecessitating selection is available, at the favoured world.** `oneWay` net-selects the
   target at `0`, with its reason true and the target false at `1`. It does not net-select the
   rival at `1`. So a nonnecessitating ground can meet this standard, at a price: some history
   has no net selection.
3. **Opposite pro tanto reasons keep two-way answers and lose net selection.** `acting` has a
   pro tanto reason for each outcome at its own world, and no net selection at `0` or `1`. This
   holds for any frame with base-fixed favouring whose two worlds agree on the base.
4. **Favouring settled by the outcome keeps net selection and reports the outcome.** `settled
   ground` has net selection at every world. Under factive favouring, at a selected world, some
   base reason favours `f` over `g` exactly when `f` is true and `g` false. So this favouring is
   modally coextensive with the outcome pair. That is a modal result: a hyperintensional account
   could still claim direction, and nothing here refutes that.
5. **Interposing an act moves the gap to the act.** On any reason path from a fact true at a rival
   to a fact false there, not every reason link preserves truth there. If the act suffices for the
   outcome at that rival, the act is false there while the first reason is true.
6. **Modal difference-making does not help a necessary reason.** A fact true at every accessible
   world enables every fact, and so does a token. Nonvacuous enabling makes the reason contingent,
   and then reason closure, a contingent explanandum and foundedness cannot all stand, by the #750
   core. `brute` has a nonvacuously enabling reason that does not necessitate. This gives the #750
   note its certified difference-making model and also its limit.
7. **Nothing tested separates personal from impersonal.** See below.

**Ranked against per-reason is a real formal separation**, and it does not sort persons from
setups. A single good order and a single chance weight are alike ranked in form. Competing reasons
and competing tendencies are alike per-reason in form. These readings are the analyst's: no
theorem certifies any of them.

Hence a dilemma, stated in prose and not a theorem. If choice consists in these marks, then
anything with them chooses, including frames read as competing tendencies. If choice is more, the
further content is not among the four marks, and it must say how it bears on which outcome obtains.
Further marks may separate them; this tests four. Self-awareness or normative rightness as such is
unformalized.

## Strongest indeterminist reply and the exact open bridge

The strongest reply rejects net selection at both worlds as the demand. A reason explains an
outcome when it favours it, a true rival reason notwithstanding, and no answer to "why this rather
than that" is owed. `acting` models that, and no theorem refutes it.

**Open bridge.** A constraint on `favours` that excludes `tokenFrame`, keeps some reason true at
both worlds, and is defended independently; together with a decision whether the PSR at stake asks
for net selection or for pro tanto favouring. Nothing here supplies either, for a divine, impersonal
or lawlike ground alike. Under one favouring `ground` and `law` have one net-selection verdict, so
the relevance premise of #750 is not discharged. It is now one named parameter.

## The relation and structural properties

**Favouring.** Ordinary meaning: a consideration counts for one option over another. Examples: a
motive favours the shorter route; a die's weighting favours six over one. Counterexamples: a fair
coin's balance favours neither face; a truth about something unrelated favours nothing.

**Distinctions.** Not `explains`, since a reason may be admitted and favour nothing. Not
necessitation, since `oneWay` favours and does not necessitate. Not probability, since only a
ranking is modelled and no measure. Not causation: `Causes` is untouched. Not evidential support,
`DEF-EPISTEMIC-SUPPORT`, which bears on accepting a fact and not on why it obtains.

**Structure.** No transitivity, asymmetry or well-foundedness is assumed of favouring. `Asymmetric`
and `BaseFixed` are named hypotheses. The case for `BaseFixed`: a die's weighting does not change
because six came up. The case against: for a choice, the opposing account says the choosing is
what makes a reason weigh more. Either is an interpretation, and the core leaves that fork open. A
base can always be enlarged by the fact that varies, which deletes the agreement member instead.

**Generalization.** Nothing generalizes from dice or routes to ultimate explanation. Those examples
motivate the form of the parameter. The theorems are about frames.

### Three explananda

Keep distinct: why acting is supported; why a particular option is supported
over its rival; and how the actual production implements an agent's rationale.
A consideration can answer the first while supporting both options. A process
can determine a unique output while remaining unrelated to that consideration.
Consequently, unique prediction and intentional governance are different
explanatory achievements. The basing investigation supplies a parallel question
about content-guided transitions, without identifying belief with divine action.

Clarke's [Fifth Reply](https://www.newtonproject.ox.ac.uk/view/texts/normalized/THEM00235),
§§1–20, especially printed pp.281–287, gives a positive historical account:
motives and an agent's active power have different roles, and equally reasonable
ways of acting can coexist with good reason to act. His application to God is
part of that defended position, not a theorem certified by this repository.
The adequacy of its contrastive explanation remains the substantive dispute.

## Did the reason guide production of this order? (#1570)

A reason can explain the production of a particular outcome under a description
that also fits another outcome. The distinction turns on what the producer
used. A reason that explains why an order was worth producing has a further
job to do if it is to explain why the source produced it. The following test
separates these jobs while holding the advertised reason and the ordinary
outcome fixed.

### One dispatch, two explanations of its success

An editor is to send an instruction in a language its recipient can read.
Two versions, A and B, give the same instruction. Both can always be sent, and at least one is readable in every case tested.
The recipient's confirmed languages determine which versions meet the end.
The booking code normally agrees with the recipient's language. In one
implementation the editor's instruction, issued on inspecting the confirmed
languages, releases the version. In the other an automatic dispatch uses
the booking code. The editor can give the very same accurate explanation of
the version's suitability after dispatch. The booking can determine both the
automatically dispatched version and the language shown on the editor's initial brief.
Even perfect agreement across bookings could have this shared upstream cause.

Lean checks both aligned bookings and their readability reports. It
also checks the disconnected instruction, the booking mismatch, and both
tie selections. The simulated dispatch returns the version sent, or
absence if the editor's instruction is disconnected.
[Aligned cases](../../TruthCore/Explanation/DispatchControls.lean#L24) · [Interventions](../../TruthCore/Explanation/DispatchControls.lean#L31)

In the default A case all three can truthfully report “A was readable.”
Only the first dispatch used the editor's instruction. Disconnecting it
prevents that dispatch while the other two proceed. The second test changes
the confirmed language independently of the booking, as when the addressee
changes after booking. It separates reliance on the booking from reliance
on actual readability. It leaves the editorial and direct impersonal policies
together. At a tie either version serves the very same reason. The supplied
`tie` settles execution in the simulation. Its value can record a particular
endorsement or an agenda entry. The calculation explains neither its origin
nor why an agent had to endorse that version. A purposive choice could
terminate the contrast there, as could a chance realization under a policy.

These are results about the displayed implementations. The editor's personal
basing has additional positive grounds **in the case as described**: the
editor understands the instruction and the recipient's need, checks a
correction against that need, and uses the resulting assessment in issuing
the command that was executed. Disconnecting a button alone would also stop
a clerk pressing it blindly. Program execution tests the routing part of
the account; the description supplies the human understanding. The direct
selector has precisely the same rule for readable versions. It earns equal
credit for delivering a readable instruction without requiring apprehension.
If a system independently satisfies an account of understanding and practical
control, its physical implementation gives no reason to withhold that credit.

## Why did Darwin give up the marine explanation of the Parallel Roads?

**One error, one recipient.** Darwin argued in 1839 that marine action had formed the high shelves of Glen Roy during successive stages of land elevation. Fix that proposition, Charles Darwin himself continuing to exist through the correspondence, and his eventual abandonment of the proposition in favor of former ice-dammed lakes. Past marine submergence elsewhere, or even here at another time, is compatible with this correction. Later work on lake sediments gives independent grounds for calling the broad correction true. Those sediments as analyzed today were unavailable to Darwin. [1839 paper, pp. 39–41 and conclusion](https://darwin-online.org.uk/converted/manuscripts/Darwin_C_R_CUL-DAR139.9.1.html) · [Palmer et al., 2020](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.quascirev.2020.106548)

Jamieson's September 3, 1861 field report traced ice moving out of Loch Treig across Glen Spean from the scores on the rock. Ice reaching the opposite side could have obstructed the Glen Glaster outlet. A lake at the proposed level needed a way to retain water against escape. The direction and reach of the ice supplied a local mechanism where open drainage would have been a difficulty. The marine account can admit ice and explain a shelf without a local dam. Evidence that makes this obstruction credible therefore answers a particular objection to the lake account. Its comparative weight depends on how independently likely that pattern of ice movement was on the marine account. It establishes neither the date of blockage by itself nor the impossibility of a sea beach. On September 6 Darwin wrote to Lyell and told Jamieson, “Your arguments seem to me conclusive.” The two statements come from the same man responding to the same report. [Jamieson, September 3, paragraphs on Loch Treig and Glen Glaster](https://www.darwinproject.ac.uk/view/letters/DCP-LETT-3242A) · [Darwin to Lyell](https://www.darwinproject.ac.uk/view/letters/DCP-LETT-3246) · [Darwin to Jamieson](https://www.darwinproject.ac.uk/view/letters/DCP-LETT-3247)

The next exchanges are more informative about **basing** than the announcement alone. On September 15 Darwin explained why small catchments could account for missing upper deltas on either theory, disputed Jamieson's climate argument by appeal to South Georgia, and raised difficulties about pebbles and landforms. On October 20 he still questioned how an ice barrier alone could work. The enclosure identified by the editors with the later exchange describes undisturbed Glen Nevis moraine deposits above the low coastal terrace, a shell bed in that terrace, and proposed answers to Darwin's particular terrace difficulties. On October 14, 1862, Darwin said Jamieson “has removed all my difficulties”. This supports a considered revision over time, with field testimony mediated through correspondence. Agreement driven solely by the reporter's authority fits the initial concession less poorly than it fits Darwin's subsequent selective use, criticism and requests for explanation. Mixed motives and other sufficient grounds remain possible. Neither a private sequence of belief strengths nor an indispensable single observation can be recovered. ### Two accounts of this same exercise of judgment

| Question | A necessary impersonal concrete ground with productive powers | A necessary intellectual giver acting for a recipient's good |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Why did this very subject exist throughout? | On this proposal there is necessarily an actual nonthinking physical base. Its material constituents or excitations continually compose and replenish the animal. His parents and the conditions of his development fix which organism begins this history. Its tissues take in materials and repair themselves, preserving that individual through the exchanges of letters. This explains later stages conditional on the realized lineage. Why the base realized that particular history remains open. On the proposed constitution account this organized, experiencing animal **is** the bearer. | How could Darwin detect the error? | An embodied agent retains his earlier claims, reads a report, reconstructs what water escaping an unblocked outlet would mean, compares alternatives and asks for further evidence. Inherited and learned capacities for tracking environments, language, geological training and public criticism make this a plausible extension to an unusual scientific problem. On the impersonal proposal the source of continued actuality is the concrete base in operation. The proposed identity of the animal with the subject connects this to the bearer, while particular memories and inferential skills depend on a narrower organization which can be lost during the animal’s lifetime. On the purposive proposal the whole existing animal and its material basis receive actuality from the same giver. Intending a capacity in abstraction would equally fit replacing Darwin with an equally skilled geologist. The de re qualification rules out that substitution as fulfillment of the proposed intention. The impersonal account's central primitive is concrete productive nature, with a constitution principle identifying **this** continuing animal as a subject. Ordinary dependencies of cognition on the living body and continuity through changing tasks motivate the proposal. They leave open whether organization suffices for first-person ownership. The purposive account's central primitive is the giver's **actual exercise under a de re specification**. Making understanding possible for another is intelligibly good, and familiar intentional provision for learners explains why an agent would sustain the conditions for their own inquiry rather than dictate an answer. A good that could motivate a giver does not identify an exercise as actually motivated by it. Neither side derives why this exact lineage had to be realized. Both grant objective evidential bearing. If pro tanto authority itself needs further grounding, the impersonal side owes an account of normative force beyond cognitive function, and the theist owes the corresponding bridge from the intended good to a reason *for Darwin*. For a geologist who expressly sought the truth, both can also explain his responsiveness relative to that aim. This case cannot adjudicate categorical authority for an indifferent believer. ### What this correction warrants

The strongest positive finding is local. Evidence of a feasible obstruction answers a real geological difficulty. Darwin's selective response and later explanation give good defeasible grounds for crediting him with using evidence in correcting a particular error. Both accounts explain how that can occur on the same ontic and epistemic assumptions about the organism's ordinary activity. If the giver adopted Darwin’s exercise of judgment as an end and that end directed its giving, the theistic account can explain Darwin’s continuing life and access to evidence as intentional provision for him. This explanation takes that adoption as a premise. Why the giver adopted that end, and whether it did so, are further questions. The impersonal account explains continuing life and access without a separate adoption and accommodates the very same reason and its actual use. The historical evidence favors basing over blanket assent. It gives neither origin a comparative advantage about the actuality of that adoption. The configurations above distinguish use of a supplied instruction from bypassing it, conditional on the two stipulated production mechanisms. Matched accuracy also permits copying a report-sensitive verdict. The letters supply the additional evidence about Darwin’s selective engagement. They provide no probability for the two origins or model of necessary being. Does the giver’s practical knowledge of the particular supply the missing link? Grant all three to the common-source control. Each remains intelligible if the intellect accurately understands the power’s independent operation. Adding infallible agreement removes the possibility of a mistaken forecast without changing what determines the exercise. Adding a de re practical specification would suffice, but only because it asserts the missing direction of dependence. The recipient substitution test sharpens the difference: a distinct equally able successor could preserve the kind of good while failing the adopted end for Darwin. A disposition preserving *this* individual through organic continuity also rejects that substitute. Even recipient specificity, therefore, fails to discriminate on its own.

A further discriminator would be a ground for individuating **this giving of being** by its practical object independently of reading the realized effect backwards. Try the existing exemplar proposal on the pair *sustain Darwin so that he may judge* and *sustain the very same Darwin while foreseeing and welcoming his judgment*. Grant the same giver, understanding of the same recipient, rational power, produced history and good in both. Does the proposed account of actual exercise explain why the apprehended recipient-good supplies fulfillment conditions for the giving itself, and why the second description fails those conditions **even on success**? Ordinary agency has independent evidence of adoption, including the agent's uptake of instructions and use of them to direct an exercise. The letters provide such evidence about Darwin's inquiry alone. Merely assigning a different intention in the two descriptions stipulates the distinction. If a warrant for the practical-object relation can be derived from the giver's actual operation without assuming recipient-directed adoption, the first proposal gains an explanation of authorship missing in the second. If that relation is fixed solely by the attended and produced effect, the common-source account reproduces its grounds. Under strict intrinsic invariance, explain also how this determinate relation to a contingent recipient obtains. Additional examples of correct belief leave this issue untouched. ## What evidence distinguishes a producer's target from its forecast?

The Glen Roy letters identify whose inquiry the geological evidence guided.
The Apollo 13 records permit a further test. On the same rescue operation we
can distinguish the return the controllers expected from the return they
were trying to bring about. A successful splashdown alone would fit both
descriptions.

NASA's flight-control report records the alternatives considered before the
principal return maneuver. The team rejected an earlier Pacific return that
would have relied on a service module of uncertain structural integrity.
A longer Indian Ocean return put consumables and recovery at greater risk.
The adopted plan targeted a Pacific return at about 142 hours. Before the
maneuver, ground communicator Joe Kerwin corrected pilot Fred Haise's
understanding of one alignment contingency. Haise repeated the correction
and the intended return target, and Kerwin confirmed it. This exchange
documents uptake of the plan while implementation could still go wrong.
[Flight Control Division report, III-22–23](https://www.apollojournals.org/alsj/a13/A13_MissionOpReport.pdf) · [Air-to-ground exchange at 070:55:52–070:57:32](https://apollojournals.org/afj/ap13fj/11day3-minimise-power.html#0705552)

The same report records a revealing disagreement before the final course
correction. One controller accepted the spacecraft's orientation. The
retrofire officer objected because it would reduce the effective component
of the planned maneuver. Guidance advised the flight director to use the
prescribed orientation. A corrected estimate alone would have improved
the forecast while leaving the trajectory where it was. Here the team
used the discrepancy to direct the crew's action. The recovery constraints,
discussion of options, transmitted target, objection and subsequent
execution together warrant attributing a purposive contribution to the
human participants. Their roles were distributed. No inference requires
one controller to have chosen the end, communicated it and executed the
maneuver alone. [Flight Control Division report, Appendix B, p. B-12, §E.1–2](https://www.apollojournals.org/alsj/a13/A13_MissionOpReport.pdf)

The records have different evidential roles. The flight directors’ joint postflight narrative describes the
selection meeting. Haise's own readback distinguishes Earth/Sun from Earth/Moon
alignment and identifies the intended Pacific return. This is participant
evidence of his understanding of the instructions. It gives little reason to
credit him personally with choosing the Pacific option. The retrofire team’s joint
postflight report gives the on-duty officer’s operational reason for the no-go
call. The four signatories do not identify which of them wrote that passage. These are distinct
perspectives on a shared operation, and both written accounts belong to one
official report. A well-calibrated automatic controller can use the target in
steering, while a human who understands the target and accurately predicts the
burn can contribute nothing to steering. The reason to credit the officer with
a practical contribution is what he **did with the estimate** in his assigned
role. Even a perfectly executed burn would leave that evidence intact.

Nor did the choice of trajectory select these three people for existence.
“Return whoever is aboard alive” identifies the beneficiaries once the roster
is fixed. A change of roster preserves that mission objective while changing
the people who benefit. The evidence supports concern for the actual crew.
It does not settle whether a particular crew member's identity was itself
among the reasons for selecting this trajectory.

### What would make this recipient the practical object of a simple giver?

Fix the same ultimate giver, Darwin, his continued existence and his exercise
of geological judgment. Grant for this comparison that the giver originates
the whole order, including the conditions that distinguish Darwin from another
knower. The good in question can be specified independently of the successful
correction. It is an opportunity for Darwin to weigh evidence himself. A true
verdict copied from somebody else misses that good, and another geologist's
exercise of the same skill leaves Darwin out. These tests distinguish the
content of the proposed end. Both producers can meet it. What would make this
content a reason **on which the giver acts**?

The strongest argument from original adoption begins with this constraint.
If something is an instruction *for this exercise*, some feature of the
producer or its circumstances must account for its authority over execution.
The existence of a correct description of the result cannot confer that
authority. An executive can inherit it from a planner, as in the rescue.
At an ultimate source of the entire order that answer runs out. **If the
giving has the character of carrying out an understood specification**, the
giver must itself own that specification as practical. Another command to
adopt it would simply reopen the same question about the authority of the
command. Original adoption can therefore be a primitive exercise of agency.
Its positive warrant comes from the intelligibility of an agent settling
which eligible good to pursue and thereby explaining what its means are for.
The local evidence supports that form of explanation. The conditional's
antecedent is the disputed step at the ultimate source.

There is a stronger way to defend that step than projecting successive
planning onto God. Aquinas argues that effects proceed from their cause in
the mode in which they pre-exist there. Given his identification of divine
being with intellect, this makes the conceived form directive in production.
He distinguishes direction by knowledge, command by will and execution by
power, adding “But in God all these things are one.” His next article locates
the order of means to end within what is willed in one act. It requires no
earlier volition causing a later one. The account of productive knowledge
likewise requires an inclination toward the effect. This yields an argument
about **how** the source gives, stronger than the bare numerical identity of
its powers. [ST I q.19 a.4, third argument and reply 4](https://www.newadvent.org/summa/1019.htm#article4) · [a.5, answer and reply 1](https://www.newadvent.org/summa/1019.htm#article5) · [ST I q.14 a.8](https://www.newadvent.org/summa/1014.htm#article8)

Test the inference by granting a contemplating source as much as possible.
Suppose its one productive nature grounds both the actual order and its
complete understanding of how that order proceeds from itself. It then knows
Darwin through its own actuality, without learning anything from an external
creature or waiting for a result. That answers the objection that a forecaster
must borrow its information from elsewhere. The remaining dispute about the
mode of pre-existence is exact. Does the intelligible content specify the
production **insofar as understood**, or does the source understand precisely
what its nature specifies productively? Both explanations put the ultimate
ground in the giver. Only the former makes the understood good a measure
of the giving. Even that answer needs a further distinction. A giver can
intentionally produce the complete order, know that Darwin benefits, and aim
at a good of the whole for which his benefit is incidental. To infer provision
*for him*, the order of means to end within the act must include his good as
a reason for sustaining him. This neither requires him to be the sole end nor
makes his goodness a prior cause of the giver's willing.

The impersonal proposal owes an account of why its disposition is sensitive
to the difference between genuine and copied appraisal. A rule fitted only
to the actual successful verdict supplies none. A disposition directed to
preserving the exercise of the recipient's capacity has a principled answer:
supplying an answer can defeat that exercise even when the answer is correct.
That same distinction constrains the personal proposal. An intention merely
to bring about true belief cannot explain provision *for Darwin's own
judgment*. With several eligible recipients, protecting the good of whichever
exists also leaves open why this person receives existence. Both sides may
take the determinate exercise as primitive. Each must say what it settles.
A general ranking tied between recipients cannot by itself settle the identity
on either account.

**Provisional judgment.** Original practical authority is a genuine
discriminator if there is independent reason to identify the exercise as
production according to an understood end. In Apollo 13 the announced objective,
readback and reason given for a control decision supply such evidence for
particular human contributions. Perfect success would preserve the distinction
between a forecast answerable to the burn and a burn directed by the target.
No divine error, temporal revision or separately existing executive is needed
for that distinction either. But removing the external planner eliminates
*delegated* authority, leaving open whether production has an intentional
standard at all. The comparison supports the intelligibility of ultimate
authorship and its conditional explanation of action for someone's sake. It
gives neither ultimate account an evidential advantage from successful
recipient-specific provision alone.

Reopen on a reason to accept or reject transmission of the **intellectual
mode** from source containment to actual production, and on evidence that
this recipient's own good belongs among the ends of the act. Under strict
intrinsic invariance the account must locate what makes that relation
determinate for this contingent person. A relation specified only by which
person actually exists obtains equally on the impersonal account. Conversely,
if a constitutive account of practical understanding is independently warranted
that the value-sensitive producer itself satisfies, count it as exercising
such understanding. Calling it impersonal would cease to distinguish it on
that criterion. The unpublished comparison of original specification and
source containment supplies the starting alternatives for this test.
