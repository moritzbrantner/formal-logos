## Can an infinite series explain a one-way transfer by forwarding alone?

Fix one positive transfer from a sender to a recipient. Entries measure a
common quantity over the same interval or at the same instant. Add up what
passes over **edges**. A unit routed through ten stages counts ten times in
throughput even if it belongs to one conserved inventory. Allow splitting,
merging and cycles elsewhere. The question is whether every supplier upstream
of this particular sender can cover its exports from its receipts.

**Cut argument.** Take the sender and everything that reaches it by a finite
path of positive transfers. Every positive receipt into that set comes from
inside it. If the recipient cannot reach the sender, the selected transfer
leaves the set. Consequently exports from the set, counting internal transfers
on both sides, strictly exceed receipts when total edge throughput is finite.
If each member exported at most its receipts, summing those very inequalities
would give the opposite result. Some identifiable upstream bearer therefore
exports more than it receives. The argument tolerates loss, perfect forwarding
and infinitely many contributors. It needs neither a fixed percentage loss
per stage nor an assumption that all chains terminate. The path establishes
reachability. In a mixed pool it does not trace an individual unit of excess
to the selected recipient. [Cut proof and exercise bridge](../../TruthCore/Results/Causality/FiniteThroughput/Solution.lean)

**Test the strongest rival on the same series.** In the [Second Way successor
model](distinct-dependence-strict-ground-route.md#the-infinite-series-at-the-efficient-cause-consumer),
each stage has a further cause. Assign one unit to each transfer from stage
n+1 to stage n. Each sender receives exactly the unit it forwards; no transfer
admits a return path. This mathematical account pays an infinite sum of edge
throughputs, despite a finite unit at each stage. Choosing geometrically
smaller amounts upstream can make the sum finite, but then some stages must
contribute beyond receipts. For example, on edge n+1 to n put
2 raised to the power −(n+1). Stage n+1 receives half what it exports. These
are additional ledgers on the model's immediate-cause graph. The original
model assigns no amounts. The earlier two-node swap gives the rival another
option: finite balanced throughput with a real return edge. The obstruction
discriminates among these accounts without ruling out infinite causal order.

**Where this helps the existence argument.** For a fixed ledger over actual
concreta at one world, suppose each bearer with an actual concrete ground
covers its exports from receipts. Then the upstream net exporter has no actual
concrete ground in that same relation. This supplies the existing actual-root
premise directly. The hypothesis does substantive work. Over a fixed interval
a discharging battery can deliver electrical energy through its two-terminal
port without receiving charging energy, while its existence depends on its
components. Its chemical store decreases. Counting the opening store explains
the output, but changes the ledger. To apply the proof to existential giving,
identify an independently motivated additive measure, positive one-way exercise,
finite summed throughput, and a reason grounded bearers cannot originate any
net amount of that quantity. Strict priority by itself gives no magnitude.
Even for an ordinary incompressible steady flow at a positive rate, inserting
more bookkeeping cross-sections increases summed throughput while leaving
the physical stock fixed. A metaphysical application needs a reason for which
distinct transfers count. The weaker exercise result only needs an interpretation
of positive transfers as productive links and of net exporters as intrinsic
contributors to the selected activity. [Exact premises](../../TruthCore/Causality/FiniteThroughput.lean)

**Can a fitted ledger count as independent evidence?** With complete freedom
to choose amounts, the existence of a fitting ledger is equivalent to a root
and a second, distinct actual bearer. Given those beings, put one unit from
the root to the other and zero elsewhere. Every grounded bearer then exports
zero. Conversely the cut supplies the root and the nonreturning edge ensures
two distinct endpoints. Retrospective fitting thus repackages the desired
root together with plurality. This is a reason to favor an independently
specified source of input over mere forwarding in a measured, finite,
one-way system, and to withhold an evidential preference between subsistent
esse and a primitive or infinitely ordered concrete reality on this test
alone. A ground-relative root can itself be an impersonal whole. Common
coverage, necessity, intellect and will require further same-bearer premises.
Reopen the comparison on an independently calibrated instance of existential
transfer or on a non-quantitative account of participation which distinguishes
derivative exercise from local origination. [Ledger equivalence](../../TruthCore/Results/Causality/FiniteThroughput/Solution.lean)

## Do degrees of being give an independently warranted measure of transfer?

The participation argument gives an order comparison. If the principal giver
possesses the participant's relevant determinations and these calibrate each
bearer's own degree by a least upper bound, the giver ranks at least as high
as that participant. This is positive warrant for a comparison of **individual
bearers** under its premises. Could those same comparisons justify covering
the total exports of a grounded intermediary? [Participant bound and its four
premises](maximum-definition-fork.md#when-does-what-is-communicated-fix-the-givers-own-degree-of-esse) [Assessment record](../../corpus/attempts/1975ad3e-ebdf-4095-a422-4753c9ae2326.json)

**Keep the production and the bearers fixed.** A relay receives from a
principal and gives to two recipients. Both upstream bearers possess a low
and a high determination. Each downstream recipient possesses the low one.
On every edge the giver possesses all the recipient's selected determinations.
Assign degree by their greatest realized level, which exists here. Give the
low level value two. Test the proposal that each edge conveys the recipient's
degree. If the high level is three, the relay receives three and exports four.
If it is five, the relay receives five and exports four. Both calibrations
preserve every rank, every tie, and the very features that ground those
comparisons. Only the numerical gap changes. This checks the preservation and
least-upper-bound reasoning on a specified toy inventory. It assumes no
physical units or universal giver. The result rules out inferring a signed
balance from those rankings and this booking rule alone. It leaves the
participant bound intact. [Model and proof](../../TruthCore/Results/Causality/ReceiptMatching/Solution.lean)

**What can settle the sign without fixing the distances?** Fix the transfer
occurrences and their finite ordered grades in advance. Count one occurrence
at every threshold it reaches. For example, the relay above has two outgoing
occurrences at or above the low threshold against one incoming, and none
at or above the high threshold against one incoming. Each numerical gap
between successive grades contributes that gap times the count reaching
its threshold. Hence exports are covered on *every* strictly increasing
positive-integer scale with absence fixed at zero exactly when outgoing counts
never exceed incoming counts at any threshold. If one count fails, enlarging
that gap alone eventually reverses the inequality against coverage. This
criterion tells us what ordinal information can establish *once occurrences
are independently counted and their values added*. It gives no principle for
counting one occurrence twice or treating possession by a giver as expenditure.

**A route using identifiable receipts.** Fix a class of transfers, such as
transactions in separately serialized single-use items during a specified
interval. Suppose a marked recipient has no path back to its sender in this
class, and there are finitely many selected exports from bearers that can
reach the sender. If every such bearer could pair each export with a different
receipt, these pairings would give an injection of the finite set of upstream
exports into itself: take an export to its matched receipt. The marked export
would have no preimage because its recipient is outside the ancestry. Hence
some upstream bearer lacks enough distinct receipts. This tolerates other
classes of transfer and infinitely many bearers elsewhere. It neither
requires summing degrees nor identifies the particular origin of a chosen
item. If **every actually grounded bearer admits the matching in this same
class**, the unmatched bearer supplies the existing actual-root conclusion
on that grounding relation. [Precise premises and transfer class](../../TruthCore/Causality/ReceiptMatching.lean)

[Fixed-amount test](../../corpus/results/74c69bf9-9eaa-482e-9f77-158ef5761349.json)

An impersonal whole can play the source role
if it is an uncaused bearer in the same profile and has an actual supply path
to this recipient. Taking the populated whole as primitive leaves that
productive path to be shown if claimed. A proposed independent divine source
owes the same account of actual supply, in addition to its existence or
capacity. Reopen the comparison on an independently motivated existential
quantity and a bounded loss law for its actual transmission. Ordinal
comparisons of esse leave that quantitative interpretation open.

## Which conditions exclude endless supply in one actual grounding cone? (2026-11-26)

Fix one actual concrete recipient, one world, and the complete cone of that
recipient under the immediate `Profile.grounds` edges. Upstream indices count
links. An incoming edge says that its source supplies the selected bearer's
existence under the productive reading being investigated. Identifying a
real-world edge requires evidence that the proposed supplier contributes to the
existence of the recipient itself. A condition for an operation, formation of
the bearer, or an earlier instance of the same kind has a different scope.

| Condition | Exact consequence in this scope | Test of the converse or omitted premise |
| --- | --- | --- |
| No infinite upstream walk starts at the recipient | Equivalent to `ConeWellFounded` in classical Lean. Every cone member is reached by a finite prefix and can inherit accessibility. Equivalently, require acyclicity within this cone and deny an injective regress from the recipient. | The existing two-cycle passes injective-chain denial and fails accessibility. Acyclicity, including the strict-order and no-strict-return criteria of #2025 and #2026, permits an infinite acyclic ray. |
| A natural-number rank strictly decreases on every upstream edge | Well-foundedness. The existing real-potential proof gives such a rank by flooring the normalized potential with a common lower bound and a uniform positive drop. Its original assumptions range over the actual roster, so restriction supplies a rank on the cone. | The `WithTop Nat` cone under strict order is well-founded at the top, with arbitrarily long finite paths below it. A natural-number rank at the top would bound all those lengths. The existing varying-gap ray has a lower-bounded, strictly decreasing real potential and no uniform positive drop. |
| One finite source path and one-step joining | The existing source-path proof gives accessibility. In fact the length of a maximal path ranks every member of the same cone and decreases by exactly one at each step. A rank alone permits an immediate fork into two distinct leaves with no common supplier. | Mere finite joining, even with a common reachable source for every member, allows the infinite common-source ray (#2022). ### Derivative power and the rival explanations

- **Essential ordering.** Fix the effect and the respect in which it is
  produced. Removing the present driver of a stick removes the stick's
  instrumentally supplied motion in that same respect. A father whose father is
  absent can still beget, since the present exercise uses a power he retains.
  That difference supports dependence of exercise on an active source. The
  further candidate primitive is that an actual, exhaustively derivative
  exercise has a finite productive provenance in some underived contribution
  in the same respect. Its positive warrant is the difference between an
  exercise actually being supplied and a set of relays each able to forward an
  input. Upgrading that principle to well-foundedness of *every* actual
  existential-supply branch requires further coverage. The formal Second Way
  premise concerns `CausesAsCaused`. The formal Third Way premise concerns
  `NecessityCausedAmongNecessaries`, a narrower relation with a separate
  necessity dictionary. `perSeRegressExcluded_on_cone` requires each relation to
  be represented on this cone and included in its actual grounds. Applying
  either result beyond this carrier requires a coverage or transport argument.
  [Aquinas, ST I, q.46 a.2 ad 7](https://www.newadvent.org/summa/1046.htm).
- **Source-free infinitism.** The analyst's Oppy-style rival takes a lawfully
  organized beginningless network of occurring exercises. Each relay actually
  receives its operative input from its predecessor. It grants dependence of
  exercise and denies finite productive provenance. The prior all-on and
  all-off witnesses explain the force and limit of this reply. The same local
  transmission equations support both, so a local law can account for each
  exercise given neighboring activity, while the presence of positive activity
  still depends on a populated boundary condition or an independently
  justified global selection principle. An explicitly productive global law
  selecting an active state, with warrant independent of that state's
  actuality, would weaken the source inference. Oppy's published challenge to
  Koons is narrower than this analyst model. He argues that evidence from
  caused events also supports a defeasible rule restricted to non-first
  spatiotemporal events. This challenges extending event-level warrant to the
  whole and gives no actual case of per se existential supply.
  [Borrowed exercise and its fixed-state controls](borrowed-exercise-741.md#three-rivals).
  [Oppy, “Koons’ Cosmological Argument,” §2](https://philarchive.org/archive/OPPKCAv1).
- **Hume–Edwards distribution.** Explaining each member does all the work
  when the purported whole is merely the members collected in thought.
  Cleanthes's twenty particles give the positive comparison. This challenges
  the premise that there is a further aggregate object requiring a cause. The
  shared residue on the strong productive reading is the actuality of the
  inputs to each relay. The objection can succeed against a demand for an
  extra object even while the activation problem survives. Conversely,
  adding a name for the collection supplies no reason to expect a further
  productive input. The test is whether the member-level explanations
  account for the same determinate positive activity without borrowing its
  actuality as part of the explanans.
  [Hume, Dialogues Concerning Natural Religion, Part IX, printed p. 167](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Page:Dialogues_Concerning_Natural_Religion_-_Hume_(1779).djvu/173).
- **Grand PSR approaches.** In Koons's argument, given a contingent fact,
  Lemma 3 makes the aggregate of wholly contingent facts wholly contingent,
  Axiom 7 (causal principle) supplies its cause, and Axiom 6 (*Separate
  Existence*) excludes mereological overlap between that cause and the
  aggregate. Without Axiom 6 the externality of the cause is unsupported.
  In the joint Koons–Pruss case the noncircular PSR instead covers
  pluralities of basic natural facts and demands an explanans external to
  the plurality. Their positive argument is epistemic: uncaused experiential
  evidence threatens the counterfactual reliability invoked by empirical
  knowledge. Oppy's restriction leaves causes for ordinary evidence while
  treating initial histories differently. Koons and Pruss test that very
  restriction in §4: even if ordinary experiences have temporal
  predecessors, a subject cannot know *a priori* that present experiences
  are non-first without relying on knowledge of the past, so on their
  account the restricted rule cannot secure empirical knowledge. The
  remaining dispute is whether the rule's applicability must be knowable
  *a priori* for empirical knowledge to be possible. This reply bears on
  epistemic warrant for the PSR and supplies no concrete grounding edge.
  At matched scope, these approaches oppose the sufficiency
  of merely distributive explanations for the eligible facts. An external
  fact explanation would still need to identify an actual concrete
  productive contributor on the fixed recipient's supply path. Even that
  would leave open the existence of a different infinite grounding branch.
  [Koons, “A New Look at the Cosmological Argument,” §§5–6.2](https://lionandlambapologetics.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/A-New-Look-at-the-Cosmological-Argument.pdf).
  [Koons and Pruss, “Skepticism and the Principle of Sufficient Reason,” §§2–4, esp. n. 7](https://robkoons.net/uploads/1/3/5/2/135276253/koons-pruss2020_article_skepticismandtheprincipleofsuf.pdf).

Provisional assessment for review. Finite productive provenance is plausible
as a direct commitment for an exhaustively derivative exercise in a fixed
respect. The present driver tests that dependence in clear instrumental
examples. The reason against a source-free exercise is the unresolved
selection of positive activity in the strongest locally supplied rival, where
the same rules also allow inactivity. The same test applies to an alleged
underived source whose mere existence permits both states. The existential
application and full-cone foundation remain open. For numerical descent,
identify a measure where every actual grounding step consumes a common positive
amount of available depth. For one-step joining, provide grounds for immediate
common suppliers even at genuine forks. For accounting, identify actual
existential transfer instances whose receipts are distinct or whose positive
amounts obey the stated coverage inequality. Reopen the derivative-power
comparison if an independently warranted rule selects the positive exercise
in a source-free productive cone, or if an independently fixed mode of
transmission rules out the positive limitless case.
