feature really entails actuality. PHIL231 and Meinong are navigation leads only;

`Interpretation` separates a predicate on features (nuclear characterization),
a candidate/feature relation (exemplification), and two candidate predicates
(actuality and actual existence). These are explicit primitive semantic inputs.
A nuclear golden feature is a description; a candidate can exemplify it in a
fictional interpretation without existing. Physical gold exemplification is a
different possible interpretation and requires an explicit reality bridge.

sorts differ. No structural metaphysical law is built into the signature.

The three sufficient commitments are Transfer, Anchor and ActualityExists.
Anchor is extension inclusion for at least one selected feature, not the
conclusion disguised as a Boolean flag. The proof chooses that feature, transfers
its predication to the selected candidate, obtains actuality, then existence,
and preserves the whole profile of the same witness. It is a modest conditional
bridge calculation, not independently warranted progress on premise truth.
The finite separation certificate is the discriminating research finding.
Each of the three premises is indispensable relative to the other two in this
signature, not uniquely minimal among all conceivable semantic commitments.
A still weaker candidate-local anchor would suffice; universal transfer or
universal existence-entailing predication is not claimed necessary in general.

The four fixture cases have a genuinely nonempty nuclear profile (`true`).
The positive case exemplifies all features and is actual and existent.
Dropping Transfer gives empty exemplification and existence. Dropping Anchor
leaves a fully exemplifying but nonactual nonexistent candidate. Dropping
ActualityExists leaves an actual fully exemplifying nonexistent candidate.
These interpretations use a nonempty candidate domain but empty actual-existence
extension. They refute the inference in this signature, not real-world possibility
or the historical theory. The last case challenges precisely the existence bridge,
not ordinary usage of the word actuality.

### Accounts, warrant and surviving routes

**Quantifier/instantiation prong.** Russell's `ExistsPred F := ∃ x, F x` is a
logical account of predicate nonemptiness; `∃ x, ExistsAt w x ∧ Profile x`
uses that quantifier while making its actualist restriction explicit. They
are compatible descriptions. Existing `Description.checks` adds at-most-one
for descriptions; it supplies neither an instance nor metaphysical reasons.
`Grounding.checks` and `Projection.checks` already distinguish a fixed
instantiation extension from a supplied grounding expansion/norm. The
inference from nonemptiness **alone** to a ground fails in those precise
interpretations. This does not refute Russell, whose source treatment does
not claim a complete explanatory analysis. No substantive objection to the
ordinary quantifier analysis is identified here. Rejecting an act-of-esse
analysis does not invalidate its logical nonemptiness consequences; accepting
quantification does not prohibit investigation of why its extension is populated.
See [the Russell assessment](../russell-existence.md).

**Actual/explanatory cosmological prong.** An actual concretum is a positive
starting point independently of a most-real concept. The existing
well-founded-grounded-contingents and thinker-local routes offer actual
necessary outputs under their exact dependence/regress assumptions. Their
positive warrant is the actuality of their starting phenomenon and the
articulated demand that an actual contingent existential dependency receive
an adequate account. The regress and scope commitments remain substantive:
local causal succession alone does not establish whole-order explanatory
sufficiency or terminate dependence. The concrete brute-root and joint-group
controls test precisely those differences, without defeating every explanatory
principle. A primitive necessary existent is compatible with modal necessity;
calling it necessary does not answer an independently admitted why-question.
The intrinsic-reason investigation offers positive explanatory motivation and
specific adequacy tests, but has not certified an adequate individual intrinsic
reason. Its global-structural rival likewise owes existential adequacy, rather
than just successful property explanation. See
[intrinsic existence reason](intrinsic-existence-reason-1595.md).

**Conceptual/modal ontological prong.** The new adapter gives an exact place
for an independently defended actualist necessary-exemplification result to
feed the cosmological necessary-concrete interface. It does not supply that
result. The existing nuclear-profile route still needs Transfer, Anchor and
ActualityExists; its exemplar has neither this actual-concrete dictionary nor
this modal population by definition. Scott's essence-based NE, filtered NE,
world-isolation result and positivity A5 remain distinct from `NecessaryAt`
and from actualist necessary profile population. An adapter from Scott T3
would require its own actualist existence dictionary, profile calibration and
numerical identity bridge; the rejected 1970 actualist antecedent remains
excluded under its retained Ax3/symmetry package. We do not revive it.

The selected positive intrinsic-existence possibility strategy has the
previously recorded defeasible recombination rationale; neither absence of
contradiction nor a nominal definition demonstrates its concrete instance.
Its received-to-unreceived extrapolation remains the specific applicability
burden identified in the existing assessment, not an invented contradiction
or a demand for an endless prior proof. These new conditional adapters add
no independent warrant to that population premise. They show what it would
permit, and exactly which further witness/support commitments are needed.
See [the Scott audit](../scott-necessary-existence-audit.md) and
[the intrinsic-existence possibility assessment](../kant-cosmological-inference.md).

**Proposed overall judgment.** Quantification and an account of existential
actuality/dependence can coexist; they are not rival complete worldviews by
virtue of their different types. Modal distributions, intrinsic reason,
efficient cause of esse and divine identification answer different questions.
The actual-explanatory prong and a well-supported modal/exemplar prong could
provide cumulative support for a necessary concrete source, even with shared
logic, semantics or actuality commitments. The implemented connection is
nonvacuous conditional support-option progress, with a precise additional
price for convergence on the SAME profile bearer. Present evidence does not
warrant treating the ontological population input or the Kant identification
as established, or treating the resulting source as GOD_4. The actualist
population and profile-identity controls delimit consequence; they do not
supply equal philosophical evidence for all assignments.
