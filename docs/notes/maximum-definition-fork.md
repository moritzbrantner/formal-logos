### When does productive transmission warrant strict superiority? (2026-11-24)

The target `d5a94eb1` says that for **every** distinct cause and recipient, causing the quality in this respect implies being *strictly more* such in that respect. It has a recorded direct reading and the conditional reception-limit derivations `924302df` and `a31b3e52`. The latter still require `7030af77`: an unlimited possessor exceeds a limited one under the specified limiter. If the only reason for attributing that excess in the case at hand is that one bearer causes the other, the deduction gives no independent reason for the comparison. [Target](../../TruthCore/Greek/Aristotle/Metaphysics.lean) · [Existing reception argument](../../corpus/arguments/cause-of-quality-more-such-from-reception-limits.json)

**A positive case with a same-kind quality.** Consider the concentration of a single dissolved substance in a feed and a distinct receiving solution at the same time. In the ideal steady-flow model a well-mixed recipient takes donor solution at volume rate `p > 0` and clear solvent at rate `q ≥ 0`; there is one outlet, no solute production or loss, constant volume and additive flows. For positive outlet concentration `r` and donor concentration `d`, solute balance gives `(p+q)r = pd`. We can identify the donor's productive contribution by tracing the incoming solute that continually replaces what leaves. Replacing that stream with clear solvent at the same rate gives zero concentration at the new steady state. Neither identification uses a donor–recipient comparison. Now **the donor is strictly more concentrated exactly when there is positive dilution**. At zero dilution fresh solute still arrives and leaves; feed and recipient then have exactly the same concentration. Their possession has the same measure and meaning even in the tie. This tests a current productive attribution in an explicitly idealized setting. It does not treat a vessel as possessing the color or concentration of its contents.

**Why even equality is not a universal floor for formal degree.** A motor-driven gear transmitting work under a resisting load is distinguishable from the driven gear by the input of work and its transmission under engagement. The gear constraint alone is symmetric and would not identify a driver. For equal pitch and no slip, tooth counts `m,n` and positive unsigned angular speeds `d,r` satisfy `md = nr`. Independent tooth counts determine whether the driver turns faster, equally fast, or slower. A step-up transmits motion while reducing available output torque at a fixed ideal power; ranking by power instead would change the respect. The speed example concerns a magnitude of motion. The solution example tests the same intensive characteristic in both bearers without relying on the treatment of speed as a quality. Both are analyst models, not reports of measurements or historical exegesis.

**Warrant at the actual root use.** Strict superiority has good local warrant where an independently identified productive mechanism has a demonstrated degree-reducing condition. Mere dependence, dependence on a sole supplier, and common formal possession do not specify such a condition. I would reject the unrestricted rule on the ordinary efficient-production and same-formal-degree reading. Its direct participation reading remains a serious narrower option: if a source has undiminished a positive perfection genuinely restricted in its recipient, that difference can ground comparative ascent. Supplying the recipient's actuality does not of itself tell us that it lacks some positive content of that very source. This is decisive for the two uses at *esse* identified above. Neither tooth count nor solute balance calibrates the primitive `MoreIn esse`. Proving the local models excludes no existential giver. To reopen positive ascent at that scope, identify positive actual content belonging to the named source which the specific mode of reception excludes, and justify its ordering as a perfection of being while allowing equal positive content as a live control. For consumers needing only exclusion of an incoming cause, keep that weaker obligation separate. The investigation changes no registered support or closure.

A command specifies forward and transverse components. At the actual forward-only command both stages achieve exactly the requested displacement. A transverse command independently detects the real restriction. Its presence strictly shrinks the range of attainable displacements and makes the stage retain heading under every modeled transverse disturbance. There are three determinate answers on these same bearers: a tie in actual forward displacement, a loss in accessible displacement, and a gain in holding the selected heading. The latter two concern dispositions. The lock's structure explains all three. Renaming or subdividing its parts changes none of the responses. The model concerns mechanisms with passive capacities; breadth of attainable change would be a particularly poor measure of a subsistent act understood to have no passive potency.

This supplies a test beyond counting restrictions. Specify what counts as success *in the respect at issue*, then identify what the constraint prevents under that specification. Here transverse exclusion deprives one stage of a reachable displacement even if nobody prefers lateral motion. The same exclusion is constitutive of keeping heading against disturbance. A real receiving essence can similarly specify the way a whole is actual. Its reality alone leaves open whether it deprives that whole of a perfection of being. The stages are a control for this inference, not an example of creatures receiving esse or of ties in every intrinsic disposition.

**What the subsistence argument earns.** Ask of each whole what accounts for its present actuality *within its constitution*. One tempting argument would subtract the condition of reception from `x` while retaining everything positive, then call the result an improvement. ### When does what is communicated fix the giver’s own degree of esse?

Take one actual participant and the same principal giver as above. Suppose we can
identify a respect in which the participant is actually perfect, specify the
positive content of that perfection without reference to who gave it, and
identify that content in the giver. If this holds for **each determination that
sets the participant’s degree of esse**, it explains why receiving esse cannot
put this participant above the giver. Two claims give this proposal a test.

1. **Preservation in the giver.** Select the determinations in advance. Among
   these, each pure determination realized in the actual participant must also
   be realized in its efficient total giver. The test concerns the two bearers'
   own actualities. The giver can pass it in a different mode only if we can
   say which positive content the modes share and what warrants attributing that
   content to the giver. The fact that the giver can produce the participant
   supplies no such identification on its own. [Preservation premise](../../corpus/statements/191395d0-e3ac-4aea-bff5-77dd437993b9.json)
2. **Calibration of the whole bearer.** Assign each admitted determination a
   level common to all its bearers. An actual bearer’s degree of esse must bound
   the levels it realizes, and every bound on those levels must already bound
   its degree. The second condition is the substantive completeness claim. For
   instance, if two actualities jointly contribute a perfection which neither
   registers alone, the inventory must capture the contribution of their union.
   Renaming or counting more descriptions of the same actuality adds nothing.
   Neither a total ordering nor a highest individual item is required.
   An empty inventory can satisfy calibration when each actual bearer's degree
   is a bottom value. A constant level on nonempty realized profiles likewise
   yields ties. Such readings cannot explain differences in degree. A proposed
   scale meant to distinguish degrees must supply independently identifiable
   intrinsic differences and justify their ordering across bearers.
   [Calibration premise](../../corpus/statements/6ab848a8-9dc0-4b5e-a8f2-82dce793fd1a.json)

Every level witnessed in the participant now has a witness in the actual giver.
So the giver’s own degree bounds those levels, and calibration puts the
participant’s degree at or below it. Equal profiles give bounds in both
directions. On a preorder that is a tie in rank, even when the representing
values are distinct. This reaches the existing participant bound
(`aba6f4e1`) without taking strict superiority (`d5a94eb1`) or adequacy
in every respect (`87f41688`) as a premise. The populated source and
efficient-total giving commitments remain. The [earlier coverage argument](../../corpus/results/e3167aef-eab5-45be-b9eb-bf17e1d1690a.json)
assumes a source with the whole inclusive repertoire. Here only the
participant's admitted actualities need transport, but their levels must
calibrate the very degree used in the conclusion. [Degree argument](../../corpus/arguments/8fe13407-28a6-4c20-b3e2-2905499e13cf.json)

**A control on the inference from total supply.** Send one unit down each
of two ports. A local detector fires when at least two units arrive *at one
port*. Straight routing leaves this detector silent at both ends. Redirecting
both units into the same output makes it fire there. The total supplied
quantity is two in each case, every output unit comes from the inputs, and the
detector rule is fixed before routing. For any positive input of *n* per
port, the greatest local threshold rises from *n* to *2n* under concentration
and stays at *n* under straight routing. This explains exactly how counting
the complete supply can coexist with a reversal on another common, independently
specified measure. A participant's form could likewise determine *how* an
actuality is realized while receiving the whole act by which it exists. The
control gives a reason to demand preservation of the proposed grade-setting
content. It supplies no example of a recipient exceeding its cause in esse.

This supports a tie on that test. To use it as a sufficient measure of esse

### Can finite panels identify the same actual giver of esse?

A panel tests a finite set of actual recipients. A candidate must be actual and unsurpassed in esse across the full carrier. It passes for a recipient if it is that recipient or gives it esse directly. The same candidate must pass for everyone on the panel. If every finite panel passes and one panel has an exhaustively finite set of candidates, one actual maximal giver covers every actual recipient. This applies the existing modal finite-hitting theorem by indexing tests with panels. Taking the union of two panels intersects their candidate sets. The causal dictionary stays fixed throughout. [Registered derivation](../../corpus/results/83352ac5-6ec3-48e1-a91f-3e9379e59f3b.json)

A fixed finite list that supplies a passing candidate for each whole panel also suffices by the finite-hitting law. Such a list may omit other passing candidates. For example, add a universal giver 0 to the flat natural-number comparison below by granting 0 a gift to every positive number. The singleton list containing 0 hits every panel, while arbitrarily large numbers also pass each finite panel. [Finite-hitting law](../../TruthCore/Results/Modal/FiniteHitting/Solution.lean)

With *k* candidates left at the pivot, a rival denying a universal giver must have a missed recipient for each survivor. Those misses yield a failed panel using at most *k* further recipients. A cyclic model attains this bound for each positive *k*. For a numerical example take the prime divisors of 30. Factorization gives the complete list 2, 3 and 5. Every triple drawn from 30, 42, 70 and 105 has a common prime. All four together have none. Replacing 105 by 154 leaves 2 common to all four. In a stipulated print run, a complete record of the two impressions on one sheet can likewise identify every plate that deposited ink on it. Two recorded contacts with an unobserved interval leave room for another plate. The evidential question in each case is what makes the list exhaustive.

An infinite rival can pass *every* finite test. In the checked model every natural number is actual and maximal on a flat comparison, and each supplies every smaller number directly.
